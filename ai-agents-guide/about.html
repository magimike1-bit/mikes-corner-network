What Using AI Agents is

You've heard "AI agent" thrown around everywhere. These guides skip the hype and explain what agents actually are, what they're genuinely useful for, and how to work with one without getting burned. Written for regular people, not engineers.

How the guides are written

Every guide is researched from real sources and written to be genuinely useful — no filler, no invented facts, no copied content. Numbers and recommendations are starting points for your own research, not guarantees.

What this site is not

Using AI Agents — beginner guides to working with AI agents. Content is for information only; AI tools change quickly, so double-check anything time-sensitive. Nothing here is professional advice — consult a qualified professional for decisions that matter.

Who runs this site

Using AI Agents is operated by the Mike's Corner network, a collection of practical guide sites. Content is produced with AI assistance and human oversight.
