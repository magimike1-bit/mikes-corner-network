Read this before you spend a dollar

  • Start with one tool, not six. Every successful small-business AI story looks the same: one well-defined workflow, a measurable baseline, a human in the loop.
  • Free tiers first. Most tools above have a free plan — run the free tier for a month and measure the hours before paying.
  • Most "AI" wins are software wins. Salon no-shows fall 25–40% from automated SMS reminders — a 2015 technology. AI phone answering is the upgrade, not the foundation.
  • Your data is the blocker, not the model. An agent is only as good as the records feeding it. Clean item lists and digital invoices come before agents.
