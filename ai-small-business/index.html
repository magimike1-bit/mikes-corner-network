What "good ROI" looks like here

Every guide on this site uses the same honest formula — illustrated with examples, never presented as guaranteed results.

~$500/mo

Example: 5 hours saved per week at $25/hr ≈ $500/month in freed time. Your numbers will differ — the calculator below uses yours.

Free to start

Most use cases on this site have a free tier or free trial, so the first experiment usually costs an afternoon, not a budget line.

1 afternoon

Typical: many of these setups take an afternoon to a week of evenings — not an IT project. Each guide gives an honest setup estimate.

Figures above are illustrative examples, not promises or study results. See how to measure AI ROI for the full method.
