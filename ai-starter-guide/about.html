What AI Starter Guide is

No jargon, no hype. Eleven beginner's guides to AI chatbots — what they are, how to ask good questions, what the free and paid versions offer, what people actually use them for, how to check their answers, how to talk to them by voice, how image generators work, how to avoid their mistakes, how to protect your privacy, how to choose a first chatbot, and how uploading files and photos works.

How the guides are written

Every guide is researched from real sources and written to be genuinely useful — no filler, no invented facts, no copied content. Numbers and recommendations are starting points for your own research, not guarantees.

What this site is not

AI Starter Guide — plain-English introductions to AI chatbots. General information only, not professional advice. AI tools change quickly — verify current features on each provider's own site. Nothing here is professional advice — consult a qualified professional for decisions that matter.

Who runs this site

AI Starter Guide is operated by the Mike's Corner network, a collection of practical guide sites. Content is produced with AI assistance and human oversight.
