Every number, traced

All figures researched and accessed 2026-09-28. Defaults are starting points — your own numbers always win.

  • Fuel consumption — Natural Resources Canada, 2026 Fuel Consumption Guide (50th Anniversary Edition), 5-cycle lab ratings. Representative models: Mini Cooper Convertible 8.8 city / 6.4 hwy / 7.7 combined L/100km (compact); Honda CR-V 1.5L 8.4 / 7.1 / 7.8 (SUV); Ram 1500 4X4 13.2 / 9.9 / 11.7 (pickup); Hyundai Kona Electric 16.3 / 20.3 / 18.1 kWh/100km (EV). Combined rating = 55% city / 45% highway per NRCan methodology.
  • Gas price: $1.81/L — Ontario regular unleaded average, week of September 21, 2026 (Ontario Ministry of Energy motor fuel prices, Kalibrate survey data).
  • Electricity: $0.098/kWh — Ontario Energy Board regulated price, time-of-use off-peak, effective May 1 – October 31, 2026. Energy charge only; HST and delivery charges extra. New RPP prices effective November 1, 2026 had not been announced as of late September 2026.
  • Depreciation curve — CAA Driving Costs data (Vincentric): ~30% value loss in year one; 60–70% over the first five years. Model residual by vehicle age: 70%, 60%, 52%, 46%, 40% for ages 1–5; ages 9+ are an extrapolated ~7%/yr decline (labeled estimate).
  • Insurance: $2,164/yr — Financial Services Regulatory Authority of Ontario (FSRA), Ontario-wide average annual premium for private passenger vehicles, 12 months ending October 2025. FSRA publishes only this overall average — your class, record, and postal code move your real quote.
  • Maintenance: $600/yr — midpoint of CAA's $500–$700/year range for routine scheduled maintenance (excludes tires and winter tires).
  • New purchase price: $53,400 — DesRosiers Automotive Consultants, average transaction price for new light vehicles in Canada, 2025 (DesRosiers also reported $49,500 for Q1 2026).
  • Used purchase price: $36,690 — AutoHebdo Q2 2026 price index (DesRosiers data, July 2026), average used-vehicle price in Canada. Modeled as a ~3-year-old vehicle for the depreciation curve.
  • Loan rates — new 6.57% (Bank of Canada, average chartered-bank auto loan rate, April 2026); used 10.49% (2026 FCAC lender disclosures via industry reporting). Adjust to your actual offer.
  • 20,000 km/year — NRCan's standard annual driving distance used across the Fuel Consumption Guide.
  • Use blends — mostly city 80/20, mixed 55/45 (NRCan combined definition), mostly highway 20/80. The blends are simulator assumptions; the underlying city/highway ratings are NRCan's.
