Three gifting wins that cost nothing extra

  • Start from the person, not the store. One real detail about their life beats an hour of browsing.
  • Wrap like you mean it. Presentation is the cheapest way to make any gift feel bigger.
  • Set a deadline two weeks before Christmas. Panic buying on December 23rd is where regrets are made.
