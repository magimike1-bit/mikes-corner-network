Food jokes and puns that are easy to digest

Food · 4 min read

Food puns are the most practical dad jokes of all: you're already at the table, the props are right in front of you, and the audience can't leave until dinner's over. Here's a full menu of courses — fruit, veggies, bakery, dairy, and drinks — ready to serve.

Fruit

  1. Orange stop? It ran out of juice.
  2. Orange that sounds like a parrot? A carrot. (Wait, that's a veggie. The orange is innocent.)
  3. Banana go out with the prune? It couldn't find a date.
  4. Sad strawberry? A blueberry.
  5. Grape get stepped on? It let out a little wine.
  6. Mama tomato to slow baby tomato? Ketchup!
  7. Tomato blush? It saw the salad dressing.
  8. Lemon need a hug? It was feeling a little sour.
  9. Apple go to school? To get a little more core knowledge.
  10. Pineapple's favorite sport? It was really into squash. (Pineapples fear nothing.)

Veggies

  1. Melon jump in the lake? It wanted to be a water-melon.
  2. Corn get promoted? It was outstanding in its field... of corn.
  3. Potato's favorite horror movie? The Silence of the Yams.
  4. Lettuce win the race? It was ahead of the rest.
  5. Mushroom go to the party alone? He was a fun-gi.
  6. Celery so good at baseball? It had great stalks.
  7. Pepper put on a jacket? It was a little chili.
  8. Cucumber's favorite instrument? The pickle-o.
  9. Broccoli's favorite band? The Rolling Scones... no wait, that's bakery. Broccoli likes the Beetles.
  10. Carrot win an award? It was out-standing in its field. (Carrots and scarecrows share an agent.)

Bakery

  1. Baker quit making donuts? Tired of the hole thing.
  2. Cookie see a doctor? It felt crumby.
  3. Bread so wise? It had a lot of loaf for thought.
  4. Baguette's favorite movie? Something with a lot of crust appeal.
  5. Croissant never get lost? It always knew which way was flaky.
  6. Muffin win the talent show? It was the batter half of the competition.
  7. Pretzel go to therapy? It was all twisted up inside.
  8. Cake go to the dentist? It needed a filling.
  9. Pie's favorite sport? It loved a good crust-country run.
  10. Roll's favorite subject? History — it loved a good roll model.

Dairy and breakfast

  1. Cheese that isn't yours? Nacho cheese.
  2. Cheese cross the road? To get to the other slice.
  3. Eggs don't tell jokes? They'd crack each other up.
  4. Milk get promoted? It was pasteurized for greatness.
  5. Butter fly? It was on a roll.
  6. Yogurt go to art school? It wanted to be a little more cultured.
  7. Pancake's favorite genre? Flat-out comedy.
  8. Waffle make a great friend? It was always there to lift you up... in squares.
  9. Bacon's favorite movie? Anything sizzling.
  10. Cereal's favorite game? Bowl-ing.

Drinks and snacks

  1. Coffee file a police report? It got mugged.
  2. Soda tell the best jokes? It had all the pop culture references.
  3. Tea get a promotion? It was steeped in experience.
  4. Popcorn's favorite movie? Anything — it just loves the theater.
  5. Chip start a band? It wanted to be a little dip-lomatic.
  6. Peanut go to the party? It was a little nutty about socializing.
  7. Ice cream truck break down? There was a rocky road ahead.
  8. Sandwich's favorite day? Lunch time, obviously.
  9. Hot dog win the race? It was the wurst competitor there.
  10. Taco tell secrets? It always spills the beans.

Serving suggestions

Food jokes work best when the food is actually present. Point at the broccoli when you deliver the broccoli line. Hand someone the orange before asking why it stopped. And the all-time champion move: write one on a sticky note and tuck it into a lunchbox. A kid opening a lunch to find "Have a grate day! (Cheese pun. You're welcome.)" remembers that forever.

Dinner-table delivery tip: keep it to one or two per meal. Dad jokes are like dessert — wonderful in moderation, and the groans are sweeter when they're rare.
