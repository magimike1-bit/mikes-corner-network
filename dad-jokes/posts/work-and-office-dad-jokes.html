Work and office dad jokes (kind enough for Monday)

Work & office · 4 min read

Workplace humor has one rule: laugh with everyone, never at anyone. These jokes target meetings, Mondays, coffee, and computers — the shared experiences, not the people. Safe for the break room, the team chat, and the boss's birthday card.

Meetings

  1. Meeting so well attended? It was a real conference of champions.
  2. Agenda's favorite exercise? Running in circles.
  3. Conference call go so long? It really dialed up the drama.
  4. Project manager bring a ladder to the meeting? To take things to the next level.
  5. Whiteboard's favorite compliment? "You're really drawing me in."
  6. Meeting end early? Everyone was board... with staying. (They left happy.)
  7. Presentation's favorite dessert? PowerPoint pie — lots of slides.
  8. Why do meetings pair well with dad jokes? Both could have been an email, but here we are.
  9. Sticky note's favorite job? Anything — it really sticks with it.
  10. Conference room's favorite song? "Everybody Hurts (for a Break)."

Coffee and the break room

  1. Coffee file a police report? It got mugged.
  2. Espresso's favorite day? Brew Year's Day.
  3. Coffee never get lonely? It was always part of a blend.
  4. Decaf's life motto? "I can quit anytime. I just don't want to."
  5. Break room's favorite game? Microwave roulette.
  6. Donut's favorite meeting? The hole team gathering.
  7. Water cooler's favorite gossip? It's always full of hot takes.
  8. Lunch's favorite compliment? "You're the highlight of my workday."
  9. Sandwich's favorite promotion? Moving up to the top shelf of the fridge.
  10. Tea's approach to deadlines? Stay calm and steep on.

Mondays

  1. Monday's favorite exercise? Running late.
  2. Alarm clock's favorite day? None of them. It's an equal-opportunity buzzer.
  3. Monday morning's favorite song? "Here Comes the Sun (Eventually)."
  4. Coffee's Monday motto? "We've bean through worse."
  5. Commute's favorite book? "The Long and Winding Road."
  6. Monday's favorite superhero? Captain Obvious — "Looks like another week!"
  7. Inbox's Monday greeting? "You have 47 unread messages. Good luck."
  8. Why is Monday like a good friend? It shows up every week, wanted or not.

Computers and tech

  1. Computer see a doctor? It had a virus.
  2. Programmer quit? He didn't get arrays.
  3. Smartphone need glasses? It lost all its contacts.
  4. Wi-Fi's favorite pickup line? "I feel a strong connection."
  5. Password's life advice? "Be strong, stay mysterious, and never share yourself."
  6. Printer's favorite music? Paper jams.
  7. Spreadsheet's favorite vacation? A real cell-ebration.
  8. Mouse's favorite cheese? Clicks.
  9. Keyboard's favorite instrument? The space bar — it loves a good rest.
  10. Cloud's favorite joke? "I'm feeling a little foggy today."

Office life (the kind ones)

  1. Desk plant's favorite review? "You're really growing in this role."
  2. Stapler's career goal? To really hold things together.
  3. Calendar's favorite compliment? "You really date well."
  4. Elevator's favorite joke? It's always uplifting.
  5. Desk lamp's motto? "Stay bright, even on Mondays."
  6. Filing cabinet's dream? To finally get its life in order.
  7. Name badge's favorite introduction? "Hi, I'm with the pun department."
  8. Office chair's favorite activity? Spinning a good yarn.
  9. Notebook's favorite secret? It's full of great ideas — just ask the margins.
  10. Paycheck's favorite holiday? Every other Friday.

The office-joke code of conduct

Three rules keep work jokes safe: one, the joke is about the situation, never a person — roast the printer, not the intern. Two, keep it clean enough for the company all-hands. Three, read the room: a joke that lands at Friday drinks might flop in Monday's budget review.

Done right, a well-timed dad joke is a tiny act of kindness. It breaks the tension, gives everyone permission to smile, and reminds the whole team that work is serious but we don't always have to be.
