About

What Fall Back Sleep is

Fall Back Sleep is a small Canadian guide site about one weekend a year: the end of daylight saving time on the first Sunday of November. It covers when the clocks change, the provinces and territories that have stopped changing them (a list that grew a lot in 2026), how to shift kids' bedtimes so nobody is awake at 5 a.m., and how to sleep well in the week after.

How the guides are written

Every guide is written to be genuinely useful and checked against primary sources where facts matter — provincial government announcements for the clock rules, and plain, conservative guidance for the sleep routines. We don't invent statistics, and where guidance is general rather than settled, we say so. Dates and rules are re-verified each season.

What this site is not

Content here is general information only, not medical advice. Sleep is health-adjacent: if you or your child has a sleep disorder, persistent insomnia, or any medical concern, a qualified health professional is the right resource — not a website, including this one.

Who runs this site

Fall Back Sleep is operated by the Mike's Corner network, a collection of practical guide sites. Content is produced with AI assistance and human oversight.

If you spot an error — especially about your province's rules, which changed for millions of Canadians in 2026 — please tell us via the contact page. Corrections are the most valuable mail we get.
