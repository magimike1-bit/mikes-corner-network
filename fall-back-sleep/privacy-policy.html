Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026. This is a plain-language summary of what this site collects — which is very little.

What we collect

Almost nothing. This is a static informational website. We do not require accounts, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal information through this site. Our bedtime planner tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing you enter into it is sent to us or stored anywhere.

What our hosting provider logs

Like nearly every website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical data (such as pages requested and approximate location derived from IP address) in server logs used for security and reliability. We do not use this to identify you.

Cookies and advertising

This site does not set its own cookies. We plan to show ads through Google AdSense: a verification snippet is present in the page code, and ads will not serve until Google approves this site. Once ads are serving, Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Consent for visitors in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google shows its certified consent message before serving personalized ads. You can choose to consent, decline, or manage your options there; your choice applies across Google's ad network.

Children

This site is a general informational resource and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this site ever starts collecting information (for example a newsletter signup), this policy will be updated to describe exactly what is collected and why, before collection begins.
