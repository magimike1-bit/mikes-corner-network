Fix Your Wi-Fi

What Fix Your Wi-Fi is

Fix Your Wi-Fi — Plain-English fixes for slow, spotty home Wi-Fi. The guides here are practical, plain-language explainers: what something is, how it works, what it costs, and how to avoid the common mistakes.

How the guides are written

Every guide is researched from real sources and written to be genuinely useful — no filler, no invented facts, no copied content. Numbers and recommendations are starting points for your own research, not guarantees.

What this site is not

Nothing here is professional advice — consult a qualified professional for decisions that matter.

Who runs this site

Fix Your Wi-Fi is operated by the Mike's Corner network, a collection of practical guide sites. Content is produced with AI assistance and human oversight.

This site provides general information about home networking. It is not professional IT advice. If you are unsure about changing your router's settings, check your router's manual or ask your internet provider.
