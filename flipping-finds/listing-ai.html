How the price estimate works

The price shown is always a labeled estimate range — never a promise of what your item will sell for. It's a starting point based on the item the AI identifies. Before you list, open the sold-comps links in your result and check what identical items actually sold for recently. Condition, brand, included accessories, and local demand move real prices more than any estimate can.
