What this site provides

GTA Moving Cost Calculator provides cost estimates for local moves in the Greater Toronto Area. Estimates are ranges drawn from published 2026 mover pricing and are shown both pre-HST and with 13% Ontario HST.

Estimates are not quotes

An estimate is not a quote, a booking, or a contract. Your actual moving cost will differ based on the mover, distance, timing, access conditions, and services you choose. Always get written quotes from movers before booking.
