Why this site exists

Insurance paperwork is written for lawyers, not for people. These guides translate the standard Canadian home insurance policy into everyday language: what you're actually buying, the levers that move your price, and the moments when it's worth shopping around. Each guide takes about five minutes to read, stands on its own, and links nothing but the rest of this series. This is general information to help you ask better questions — it is not financial advice, and it can't replace a licensed insurance professional who knows your situation.
