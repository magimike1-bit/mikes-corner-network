School Jokes: Homework, Teachers & Cafeteria Food

School · 6 min read

School gives us plenty to laugh about — math problems that fight back, backpacks heavier than a small elephant, and cafeteria mysteries that scientists still can't solve. These jokes laugh with the school day. One rule: funny is never an excuse to be mean about a real person.

Homework heroics

  • "My dog ate my homework." "So you did it twice?" "No — he ate the answer key too."
  • Why did the math book look sad? It had too many problems.
  • Why did the student eat his homework? Because the teacher said it was a piece of cake.
  • What do you call a homework assignment that never ends? A long division.
  • Why was the spelling homework so confident? It knew all the words worth knowing.
  • Teacher: "Name two days of the week that start with T." Kid: "Today and tomorrow."

Classroom classics

  • Why did the teacher wear sunglasses? Because the class was so bright.
  • What did the pencil say to the sharpener? "You're really grinding me down!"
  • Why did the eraser go to school? To rub out its mistakes.
  • What is a math teacher's favorite dessert? Pi.
  • Why was the broom late to class? It swept in at the last minute.
  • Teacher: "If you have ten cookies and give me three, what do you get?" Kid: "Seven cookies and one really good friend."
  • Why did the geography book go to the gym? To work on its atlas muscles.
  • What did the clock do in math class? It counted down the minutes.

Cafeteria chronicles

  • Why did the cafeteria serve chicken? It was egg-cellent value.
  • What did the lunch lady say to the sandwich? "You lettuce me know if you need anything!"
  • Why did the cookie go to school? To become a smart cookie.
  • What do you call cheese that isn't yours? Nacho cheese.
  • Why did the milk bring a ladder? To reach the high shelf in the fridge.
  • What did the apple say to the orange at lunch? "Nothing — they just sat in the fruit bowl being healthy."
  • Why did the banana wear a coat to lunch? The cafeteria was chilly.

Bus, recess & everything after

  • Why did the kid bring a ladder to school? To get to high school faster.
  • What is a school's favorite kind of music? Class-ical.
  • Why did the playground equipment file a complaint? Too many kids were swinging by.
  • What do you call a backpack that tells jokes? A pack of laughs.
  • Why did the science experiment blush? It saw the class change.
  • What is the best day to go to the beach? Sun-day — but you'll have to skip school first, so don't.
  • Why did the music class sound so good? Everyone was on the same note.
  • What did the janitor say when the hallway was spotless? "Mop mission accomplished."

Test-day chuckles

  • Why did the test go to the principal's office? It was cheating on the answers.
  • What do you call a test you didn't study for? A pop quiz — emphasis on the pop.
  • Why did the student bring a ladder to the exam? The questions were over their head.
  • What did the A+ say to the report card? "We make a great team."
  • Why was the spelling test so relaxing? It was full of easy words — allegedly.

The golden rule of school jokes

Jokes about homework, cafeterias, and tricky math problems are funny because they're about situations everyone shares. Jokes about a specific person's clothes, voice, or mistakes aren't jokes — they're just hurtful. Keep it to the situations, and you'll always be the funniest kid in the room instead of the one people avoid.
