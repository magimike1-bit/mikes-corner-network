The Hybrid Move: Rent the Truck, Hire Only the Loading Help

You drive, they lift. Labour only help loads or unloads your own rental truck or container for an hourly rate, and the saving lives or dies on how ready the load is when the clock starts.

Moving on a Budget · Budget move guide

Yes, you can hire movers without hiring their truck. Labour only services exist for exactly this split: you rent and drive a truck or container from a company such as U-Haul, Penske, BigSteelBox, or PODS, and a crew handles the loading, the unloading, or both. One Metro Vancouver provider, Simple Moves, describes it plainly: many moving companies offer labour only services where they load or unload your rental truck, container, or portable storage unit, while you handle the rental and the transportation. This guide covers the job the way a job should be covered: what it costs, what to have ready, and the order the load goes on.

Job summary

Difficulty: moderate. The lifting is hired out; the driving, the packing, and the scheduling are yours. Time: a professional crew typically loads a 20 foot container for a two to three bedroom home in three to five hours, per Simple Moves. Cost: dated price checks for Ottawa labour only crews on U-Haul MovingHelp, checked November 3, 2026, ran from $60.00 CAD per hour to $99.00 CAD per hour for a two person crew, with a two hour minimum on most Ottawa listings. Hire a pro for the whole move instead if you cannot drive a large rental truck comfortably, if stairs and a piano are both on the list, or if nobody on your side can supervise the load at both ends.

Tools and materials to have ready

  • Your rental truck or container, parked legally and as close to the door as the site allows
  • Furniture blankets or pads, and straps or rope for securing the load
  • Boxes packed, sealed, and labelled before the crew arrives
  • Furniture disassembled where it needs to be, with hardware bagged and taped to each piece
  • A clear carry path, doors propped or removed if a wide piece needs the opening

Good crews bring their own dollies, furniture blankets, straps, and basic tools, per Simple Moves, but confirm that when you book rather than assuming it. Anything the crew has to fetch, assemble, or pack for you is billed time.

The same provider guidance that sets crew size also protects that number: two movers are sufficient for most one to two bedroom loads, while a three person crew suits a three bedroom house and can reduce the total hours billed.

The loading procedure

  1. Book the crew against the truck, not the calendar alone. Book loading help at least one to two weeks in advance, and more in peak season, which Simple Moves puts at May through September. Line the crew start time up with the truck pickup so nobody is paid to watch an empty driveway.
  2. Stage the load before the clock starts. Boxes sealed and stacked by the door, furniture wrapped or ready to wrap, paths clear. Success looks like a crew that starts carrying within minutes of arriving.
  3. Load heavy and bulky first, against the front wall. Appliances, dressers, and couches go in first and low. In a rental truck, distribute heavy items evenly across the floor, centred and low, because uneven loading affects how the truck handles.
  4. Use mattresses as padding along the sides. They form a protective barrier between furniture and the truck or container walls.
  5. Fill the middle with medium weight boxes, tight. A tightly packed load with no gaps is the goal; gaps are where items shift and break in transit. Light and fragile boxes go on top and toward the door.
  6. Strap and pad the load before you close the door. Blankets between pieces, straps across the load at the tie down points rental trucks provide. Success looks like a load you can push on without anything rocking.
  7. At the destination, unload to rooms, not to a pile. Direct each piece to its room as it comes off. Paying a crew to stack everything in the garage and then moving it yourself a second time wastes the hire.

Common mistakes and fixes

Booking too few hours. Most Ottawa listings start with a two hour minimum, and an unfinished load at the buzzer means extension rates or a second trip in your rental. Fix: describe stairs, heavy pieces, and box count honestly when you book, and take the crew size the provider recommends.

Packing while the crew carries. Every unsealed box is billed standing around time. Fix: packing finishes the night before, no exceptions. Our room by room packing system is built for exactly this deadline.

Forgetting liability. When you drive, the load and the driving are yours in a way they are not on a full service move. Read what the rental and the labour booking each cover before moving day, and compare it with the moving insurance guide before you decide the hybrid saving is worth the risk you keep.

Safety note: hired help does not make heavy lifting safe by magic. Keep the carry path dry and clear, keep children and pets out of the route, and let the crew lead on any piece that needs a strap or a second set of hands.
