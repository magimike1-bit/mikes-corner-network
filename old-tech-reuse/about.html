Old Tech, New Life

What Old Tech, New Life is

Before you recycle or donate that retired phone, tablet, router, or PC, check what it can still do. These short, plain-language guides walk you through giving old tech a second job — camera, kitchen display, Wi-Fi extender, or media server — and how to wipe and recycle devices properly when reuse isn't an option.

How the guides are written

Every guide is researched from real sources and written to be genuinely useful — no filler, no invented facts, no copied content. Numbers and recommendations are starting points for your own research, not guarantees.

What this site is not

Old Tech, New Life — DIY project notes for personal use. Content is for information only. Before repurposing, donating, or recycling any device: back up anything you want to keep, wipe your personal data, and unplug equipment before opening or rearranging it. Check local rules for e-waste disposal. Nothing here is professional advice — consult a qualified professional for decisions that matter.

Who runs this site

Old Tech, New Life is operated by the Mike's Corner network, a collection of practical guide sites. Content is produced with AI assistance and human oversight.
