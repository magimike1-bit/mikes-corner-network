About

Run by someone who's actually done the switch.

Hi, I'm Mike.

I'm a business analyst by trade — requirements, process improvement, finding the waste in how things actually work — and I've spent my career inside Canadian retail and logistics. It started in a warehouse office — keeping shipments moving on time and digging into carrier data to build a better process for prioritizing trailer loads. Then a help desk supporting POS systems store by store: hardware upgrades, PCI compliance work, the unglamorous stuff that keeps the tills running. Then four years as a business analyst for purchasing and merchandising — ERP master data, assortment planning, forecast and replenishment, EDI — the systems behind every transaction.

For the past two years I've been with a hotel chain — hotels, bars, and retail shops — which took my sector knowledge beyond pure retail into hospitality: food-and-beverage operations and guest-facing systems that can't go down in the middle of service.

Before that I was the senior POS specialist for a national retail chain — rolling out a new POS platform and hardware to more than 100 stores across the country, managing the application and hardware vendors to scope and budget, and running the escalation path for stores and district managers. New store openings, renovations, moves, closures: every piece of IT in the store was my checklist.

Before that I ran in-store IT infrastructure at one of Canada's largest retailers — three teams, a $15M+ annual budget, and the full lifecycle of everything a store runs on: POS, self-checkouts, pinpads, printers, servers, UPS. My teams replaced 5,900+ pinpads across hundreds of stores and shipped two major POS upgrades.

Along the way I personally led four major payment-processor migrations, so when I say I'll project-manage your switch, I'm not working from someone else's playbook.

  • Full processor replacement across live retail stores
  • Processor migration bundled with a complete new POS rollout across the chain
  • Mid-operation processor switch — cutover without stopping the business
  • Greenfield processor setup, integrated with the retailer's existing POS

That's the whole reason FairRate Audit exists. Most fee consultants can read a statement. Very few have actually stood in a store during a processor cutover and kept the tills running. When I recommend a switch, I'm not guessing how it goes — I've lived the before and after, four times over.

Based in the Toronto area, working with small businesses across Canada — and vendor-independent: Mike works for the business being audited, not for any processor. His specialty beyond payments is retail systems themselves — POS hardware and software, integrations, and inventory — which is why FairRate Audit also offers POS services as a follow-on to the audit.

FairRate Audit is a consulting service. We're not a payment processor, we're not a bank, and we never touch your money — our fee is a monthly invoice for half of the savings we document. If a processor referral bonus is ever part of a recommendation, you'll see it disclosed in writing before you sign anything.
