Referral program

Refer an owner, skip a month of fees

Double-sided, by design. You get one month of your shared-savings fee waived for every referred audit that signs. They get audit priority. Rewards trigger on signed audits — not signups — so there's nothing to game.

1

Anyone who signs up through your link jumps to the front of the audit queue.

3

When their audit is signed, one month of your shared-savings fee is waived. One signed audit, one waived month.

Why it's built this way: one level, no downlines — nobody recruits recruiters. And the reward is a fee waiver, not cash, by design: it kills self-referral fraud. Fake signups earn nothing because nothing pays out until an audit is actually signed. Plain terms

  • Referrer reward: one month of your shared-savings fee waived per signed audit from your link.
  • Referee reward: audit priority — the referred business moves to the front of the audit queue.
  • Trigger: the audit being signed. Signups, clicks, and conversations earn nothing.
  • One level: no downlines, no tiers, no multi-level anything.
  • Non-cash by design: the fee waiver can't be cashed out, transferred, or stacked with anything else — that's what keeps self-referral pointless.
  • Manual confirmation: audit-signed status is confirmed by us manually. When your referral's audit signs, the waiver applies to your next fee month.
  • Changes: if the program ever changes, terms in force at the time your referral's audit signed are the ones that apply.
