Bundled platforms vs. standalone POS

Why all-in-one bundles hide your processing cost — and what changing processor really means.

All sectorsPOS systems
A note on bundled platforms. All-in-one platforms like Square, Toast, Mindbody, and Vagaro bundle the POS software, hardware, and payment processing together — which means the true processing cost is hidden inside the bundle. Switching to a cheaper processor on one of these platforms may require replacing the whole stack. On every sector page, bundled platforms are kept in their own clearly separated category so they are never mixed into standalone provider or POS comparisons.
