How things connect — handled for you
There's no single right answer — so choose per zone in the network plan below. The default is hybrid: the devices that take money get cables; the devices that move get Wi-Fi. Guest Wi-Fi is on or off depending on what's normal for your sector (toggle it below). Cable runs and switch ports follow your plan automatically — every auto-added item tells you why it's there.

🔧 Installation & setup — paid up front, never monthly

One-time work, due at install. Cable drops and setup are paid once — they never belong in a monthly column. Tick what your installer will do; the labor estimate below comes from the install hours on each hardware item × your tech's rate.

🎓 Training — the cost nobody budgets

A new system isn't live when it's installed — it's live when your people can run it at rush without thinking. Training is paid time, and turnover means you pay it again every year.

🧸 Toys — nice to have

Genuinely useful for some, a luxury for most. Same honest pricing, visually de-emphasized.
