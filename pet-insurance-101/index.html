A Money-guide series for pet owners

Pet insurance sits at the intersection of two things people feel strongly about: their pets and their budgets. These guides take a review/guide approach — each one walks through the mechanics, gives you the what-to-look-for checklist, and ends with our take on what actually matters. No sponsored rankings, no pressure, and no invented numbers: premiums and coverage vary widely by pet, breed, age, and provider, and we keep it honest about what we don't know.

Start anywhere — but if you're new to the topic, reading in order will give you the full picture in about twenty minutes.
