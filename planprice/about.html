About PlanPrice

PlanPrice is a deals directory for business software. We track list prices and current promotions for the tools small businesses actually buy, accounting, CRM, ecommerce, point of sale, scheduling, and more.

Our standard: every discount headline on this site links to the vendor's own page. If we couldn't find a public promotion, the page says so plainly instead of dressing up standard list prices as a deal.

Review scores: deal pages show aggregate user ratings from third-party review sites (G2 and Capterra), these are not PlanPrice's own ratings. Review scores are checked on their own schedule, separately from the monthly price sweep. Each score shows its source and the date it was last checked.

Prices are checked by Mike, editor of PlanPrice, and dated. Vendors change prices and promotions without notice, so always confirm on the vendor's pricing page before purchasing.

PlanPrice earns no affiliate commissions today. The plan is to fund the site through affiliate commissions in the future, but our affiliate partnerships are still being set up, so every link on this site is currently a plain link to the vendor and buying through it earns PlanPrice nothing. When affiliate links go live, this page and our affiliate disclosure will say so, and buying through them will never cost you extra.

How we verify prices

Every price on PlanPrice is checked against the vendor's own pricing page by Mike, editor of PlanPrice, and the check date printed next to each price is the date of the last full sweep that covered that product, or the date of a later spot check where the log says so. There is no single site wide verification date, because products are covered sweep by sweep and page by page. When a vendor runs a public promotion, the deal page shows the promo terms and links to the vendor's own offer page; when no public promotion exists, the page says so plainly instead of dressing up list prices as a deal. Prices are re-checked in a full sweep once a month, and a page's verified date moves only when a full sweep covers that product, or when a spot check re-reads that product's full list pricing and the log records it as one.

Review scores are labelled with their source and their own check date, separately from pricing. A score date is never presented as a price verification date. Scores describe other users' experience; they don't include pricing, and a high score on an expensive tool doesn't make it the right tool for a tight budget.

What PlanPrice doesn't do

PlanPrice doesn't sell software and doesn't take a cut of anything you buy, right now our affiliate partnerships are still being set up, so every outbound link is a plain link to the vendor (see the affiliate disclosure). We don't invent prices, fabricate promotions, or publish "deals" we couldn't find on the vendor's own site. And we're a pricing directory, not an advisor: nothing here is financial or tax advice, and the final price is always whatever the vendor's own checkout shows.

Spot something wrong? A price that changed, a promo that ended, a product we should track, tell us. Corrections get re-checked against the vendor's site, and the page gets updated when the correction checks out.

Meet The Editor

PlanPrice is edited by Mike, a retail IT professional who has spent more than ten years in IT vendor management.

That background is the working basis for this site. Vendor management means reading vendor pricing pages, plan tiers, and contract terms as a day job, and comparing what vendors publish against what they actually charge. PlanPrice applies the same reading to the business software prices and promotions tracked here.

Mike directs what PlanPrice covers and reviews the prices, guides, and deal pages before they are published. When a reader reports a price that looks wrong, the correction comes to Mike, gets re-checked against the vendor's own site, and the page is updated when the correction checks out. See the Contact page for how to send a correction.
