Mike, Editor Of PlanPrice

Mike is the editor of PlanPrice. He is a retail IT professional who has spent more than ten years in IT vendor management.

Vendor management work involves reading vendor pricing, comparing plan tiers, and checking published terms against real offers. PlanPrice covers business software pricing the same way: every price is checked against the vendor's own pricing page, every promotion is linked to the vendor's own offer page, and pages with no public promotion say so plainly.

What Mike Does At PlanPrice

Mike directs what PlanPrice covers, which products are tracked, which guides are written, and what gets published. He reviews the prices, review scores, guides, and deal pages on this site before publication, including the check dates printed next to prices and scores.

PlanPrice does not do hands-on product testing, and its review scores are aggregate user ratings from G2 and Capterra, not Mike's own ratings. How products are selected, checked, and ordered is set out in full on the Methodology page.

Corrections And How To Reach Mike

Corrections reach Mike through the Contact page. For a pricing correction, include the vendor and plan name, the price you saw, the date you saw it, and a link to the vendor's pricing page where you saw it. Mike re-checks each correction against the vendor's own site. If the correction checks out, the page is updated and its verified date moves. If the price shown here is still the price the vendor lists, the reply will say so.

Product suggestions and questions about how PlanPrice verifies prices and review scores also reach Mike through the Contact page.

AI Assistance

AI tools assist with content production on this site. Mike directs that work and reviews the content before it is published. The AI disclosure on the About page states the same policy.
