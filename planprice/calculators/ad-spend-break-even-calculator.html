Ad Spend Break-even Calculator

Free ad break-even calculator: the maximum cost per click you can pay — from a page's ad revenue (RPM) or from a conversion's value and conversion rate.

How it works

Display-ad version: a pageview earns RPM ÷ 1,000. A visit is worth that × pageviews per visit, and your cost per click must stay under it. At a $15 page RPM and a strong 3 pageviews per visit, break-even CPC is $15 ÷ 1,000 × 3 = $0.045 — which is why buying traffic to flip into display ads almost never works. The full evidence is in our paid-ads arbitrage guide.

Conversion version: value per click = profit per conversion × conversion rate. A $50 profit per sale at a 2% conversion rate values each click at $1.00.
