Break-even Point Calculator

Free break-even calculator for small business: enter fixed costs, price, and variable cost per unit to find how many units you must sell to cover costs.

How it works

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ (price − variable cost per unit). The amount left from each sale after its direct costs — the contribution margin — is what pays down your fixed costs. Divide the fixed costs by that margin and you get the number of sales needed before profit starts.

Worked example: $5,000 of monthly fixed costs, a $50 price and $30 of variable cost leaves a $20 margin — $5,000 ÷ $20 = 250 units a month, or $12,500 of revenue, just to break even.
