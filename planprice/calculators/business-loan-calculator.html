Business Loan Payment Calculator

Free business loan calculator: monthly payment, total interest, and total cost for a term loan at a fixed rate.

How it works

Monthly payment = P × r(1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12), and n is the number of monthly payments. Early payments are mostly interest; the principal share grows over the term.

Worked example: $50,000 at 8% over 5 years = $1,013.82/month, $60,829.20 paid in total, of which $10,829.20 is interest.
