Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Calculator

Free CAC calculator: total sales and marketing spend divided by new customers — plus what that CAC means against your customer lifetime value.

How it works

CAC = (all sales + marketing spend in a period) ÷ (new customers won in that period). Worked example: $4,000 of marketing + $1,000 of sales cost winning 50 customers = $100 CAC. The most common error is counting only ad spend and leaving out salaries, freelancers, and software — which understates CAC and overstates how much room you have to spend.
