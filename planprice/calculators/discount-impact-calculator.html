Discount Impact Calculator

Free discount impact calculator: how much extra volume you need to sell after a discount just to make the same gross profit.

How it works

Volume multiplier = old unit profit ÷ new unit profit. A discount comes straight out of margin, not out of price proportionally: with a 40% margin, a 20% discount halves your per-unit profit ($40 → $20), so you must sell twice as many units — +100% volume — just to stand still. The thinner the margin, the more violent this gets: at a 25% margin, a 20% discount needs 5× the volume.
