Email List Value Calculator

Free email list value calculator: subscribers, click rate, conversion rate, and order value into monthly and annual revenue — and value per subscriber.

How it works

Revenue per send = subscribers × click rate × conversion rate × average order value. Worked example: 5,000 subscribers × 2% clicking = 100 clicks; × 3% converting = 3 orders; × $60 = $180 per send, $720/month at 4 sends, $8,640/year. Click rate does the heavy lifting — the same list at a 1% click rate is worth half as much, which is why relevant, segment-matched emails beat bigger lists.
