Employee Turnover Cost Calculator

Free turnover cost calculator: what employee departures cost your business per year, based on salary and a replacement-cost percentage you control.

How it works

Total = salary × replacement-cost % × departures. The percentage bundles recruiting, interviewing time, onboarding, training, and the months of lower productivity before a new hire reaches full speed. Gallup has estimated replacement cost at one-half to two times the departing employee's annual salary, depending on the role — the 50% default here is the conservative end; skilled and hard-to-fill roles run higher.

Worked example: three departures at a $55,000 salary and 50% replacement cost = $82,500 a year.
