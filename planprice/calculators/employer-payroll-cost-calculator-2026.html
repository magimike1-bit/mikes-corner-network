Employer Payroll Cost Calculator 2026 (Canada)

Free Canadian employer cost calculator: salary plus employer CPP, CPP2, EI premiums, and optional WCB and benefits — the true cost of an employee in 2026.

How it works

Canadian employers match the employee's CPP contribution dollar-for-dollar and pay 1.4× the employee's EI premium. Using 2026 CRA rates: CPP is 5.95% of pensionable earnings between the $3,500 basic exemption and the $74,600 maximum (employer max $4,230.45); CPP2 adds 4% on earnings from $74,600 to $85,000 (max $416); EI is 1.63% of insurable earnings up to $68,900 (employee max $1,123.07, so the employer's 1.4× max is $1,572.30).

Worked example: a $60,000 salary costs the employer CPP of ($60,000 − $3,500) × 5.95% = $3,361.75 and EI of ($60,000 × 1.63%) × 1.4 = $1,369.20 — a true cost of $64,730.95 before WCB or benefits.

What changed for 2026

The CPP and EI rates themselves held steady — what moved is the earnings ceilings they apply to, plus a small EI rate cut. That means mid-range salaries barely change, while higher salaries cost more because CPP now applies to a bigger slice of pay.
