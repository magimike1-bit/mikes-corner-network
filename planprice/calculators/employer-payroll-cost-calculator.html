Employer Payroll Cost Calculator (Canada)

Free Canadian employer cost calculator: salary plus employer CPP, CPP2, EI premiums, and optional WCB and benefits — the true cost of an employee in 2025.

How it works

Canadian employers match the employee's CPP contribution dollar-for-dollar and pay 1.4× the employee's EI premium. Using 2025 CRA rates: CPP is 5.95% of pensionable earnings between the $3,500 basic exemption and the $71,300 maximum (employer max $4,034.10); CPP2 adds 4% on earnings from $71,300 to $81,200 (max $396); EI is 1.64% of insurable earnings up to $65,700 (employee max $1,077.48, so the employer's 1.4× max is $1,508.47).

Worked example: a $60,000 salary costs the employer CPP of ($60,000 − $3,500) × 5.95% = $3,361.75 and EI of ($60,000 × 1.64%) × 1.4 = $1,377.60 — a true cost of $64,739.35 before WCB or benefits.
