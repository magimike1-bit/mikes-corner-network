Freelance / Hourly Rate Calculator

Free freelance rate calculator: turn a target salary, overhead, and billable hours into the hourly rate you actually need to charge.

How it works

Rate = (target income + overhead) ÷ billable hours, then grossed up for profit: ÷ (1 − margin). The two inputs most freelancers get wrong are billable hours — a 40-hour week rarely yields more than 20–30 billable hours after admin, marketing, and quoting — and weeks actually worked.

Worked example: $70,000 income + $6,000 overhead = $76,000, over 25 billable hours × 48 weeks = 1,200 hours, gives a $63.33 break-even rate; with a 15% profit margin, $63.33 ÷ 0.85 = $74.51/hr.
