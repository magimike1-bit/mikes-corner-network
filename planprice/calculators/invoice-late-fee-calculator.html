Invoice Late-Fee & Early-Payment Discount Calculator

Free invoice calculator: the late fee owed on an overdue invoice, and the true annualized cost of skipping — or offering — an early-payment discount.

How it works

Late fee = invoice × monthly rate × (days overdue ÷ 30) — a simple, non-compounding charge. Worked example: 1.5%/month on a $10,000 invoice 30 days late = $150.

Early-payment discount, annualized = (discount ÷ (100 − discount)) × (365 ÷ days early). "2/10 net 30" on $10,000 saves $200; earning $200 for paying 20 days early annualizes to 37.2% — which is why buyers with cash almost always take it, and why offering it is an expensive way to speed up payment.
