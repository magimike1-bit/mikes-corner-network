Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) Calculator

Free LTV calculator: average order value, purchase frequency, lifespan, and margin into customer lifetime value — with the LTV:CAC ratio.

How it works

LTV (profit basis) = average order value × purchases per year × years as a customer × gross margin. Worked example: $80 orders, 4× a year, for 3 years at a 60% margin = $960 of lifetime revenue and $576 of lifetime gross profit. Against a $100 CAC, the ratio is 5.76 : 1.

Always compare like with like: profit-basis LTV against CAC. Revenue-basis LTV flatters the ratio by the inverse of your margin.
