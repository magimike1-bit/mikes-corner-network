Marketing ROI / ROAS Calculator

Free marketing ROI and ROAS calculator: campaign spend vs revenue, with an optional cost-of-goods adjustment so the ROI is profit-based, not revenue-based.

How it works

ROAS = revenue ÷ ad spend — a revenue multiple, not profit. ROI = (gross profit − spend) ÷ spend, where gross profit is revenue minus the cost of goods sold. Worked example: $1,000 of spend producing $4,000 of revenue is a 4.0× ROAS and, with no COGS counted, a 300% ROI. Add a 60% COGS and the same campaign's gross profit is $1,600 — ROI falls to 60%. Platforms report ROAS; your bank account experiences ROI.
