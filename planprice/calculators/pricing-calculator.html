Pricing Calculator

Free pricing calculator: enter your cost and a target margin or markup and get the selling price — with the profit per unit shown both ways.

How it works

By margin: price = cost ÷ (1 − margin). By markup: price = cost × (1 + markup). The two targets are not interchangeable — a 30% markup on a $40 cost gives $52.00 (a 23.1% margin), while a 30% margin needs $57.14.
