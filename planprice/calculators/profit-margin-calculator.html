Profit Margin & Markup Calculator

Free margin and markup calculator: enter cost and price to get gross profit, margin %, and markup % — plus the price needed for a target margin.

How it works

Margin = (price − cost) ÷ price. Markup = (price − cost) ÷ cost. They describe the same profit from two directions, which is why a 100% markup is only a 50% margin — the most common pricing mix-up in small business.

Worked example: cost $60, price $100. Profit is $40. Margin = $40 ÷ $100 = 40%. Markup = $40 ÷ $60 = 66.7%. To earn a 30% margin on a $60 cost, price = $60 ÷ (1 − 0.30) = $85.71.
