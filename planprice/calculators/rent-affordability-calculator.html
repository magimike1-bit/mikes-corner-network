Rent Affordability Calculator

Free rent affordability calculator for small business: rent as a percentage of revenue, the annual cost, and the maximum rent at common target ratios.

How it works

Rent ratio = monthly rent ÷ monthly gross revenue. Worked example: $3,000 of rent on $30,000 of revenue = 10%. Landlords and lenders look at the same ratio from the other side: a space is "affordable" when projected revenue supports the rent at a ratio your industry can sustain — high-margin services can carry more rent than thin-margin retail.

The 5–10% band is a rule of thumb, not a standard — restaurants and retail often run higher, home-based and online businesses near zero. Use your own gross margin as the real test: rent must fit inside it with room for payroll and profit.
