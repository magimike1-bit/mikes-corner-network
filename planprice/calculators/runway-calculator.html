Business Runway Calculator

Free runway calculator: cash on hand divided by monthly net burn — how many months your business can operate before the money runs out, and the date.

How it works

Runway = cash ÷ net burn, where net burn = monthly expenses − monthly revenue. Worked example: $120,000 of cash against a $10,000 net monthly burn = 12.0 months. Every $1,000/month cut from burn adds runway without a dollar of new revenue — which is why subscription creep (see the annualizer) matters more than it looks.
