Salary ↔ Hourly Wage Converter

Free salary-to-hourly converter: turn an annual salary into an hourly wage (and back), with weekly and monthly figures for Canadian budgeting.

How it works

Hourly = salary ÷ (hours per week × paid weeks per year). At 40 hours × 52 weeks = 2,080 paid hours, the quick rule is salary ÷ 2,080: a $62,400 salary = $30.00/hr, and $30/hr = $62,400/yr. If vacation is unpaid, reduce paid weeks (a freelancer taking 4 unpaid weeks works 48 — the same $30/hr then yields $57,600).
