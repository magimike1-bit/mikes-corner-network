HST/GST Sales Tax Calculator (Canada)

Free Canadian sales tax calculator: add or remove GST, HST, PST, RST, and QST by province, all 13 provinces and territories.

How it works

To add tax: amount × (1 + combined rate). To back tax out of a tax-included total, divide by (1 + combined rate), don't just subtract the percentage, which overstates the net. Nova Scotia's HST dropped to 14% on April 1, 2025; the other HST provinces remain at 15% except Ontario at 13%. Quebec's QST (9.975%) is calculated on the pre-GST amount, so the combined effective rate is 14.975%.

Worked example: $100 in Ontario, adding 13% HST gives $13.00 of tax and a $113.00 total. Backing tax out of $100 gives a net of $88.50 and $11.50 of tax.

Sources

Rates are general consumer and business rates; zero rated and exempt supplies, and item specific PST and QST rules, are outside what a single combined rate can show.

Frequently Asked Questions

How Do GST, HST, PST, And QST Stack?

In HST provinces there is one combined tax. In British Columbia, Saskatchewan, and Manitoba, GST and the provincial tax are calculated separately on the same pre tax amount and added. Quebec is the exception in method: QST at 9.975 percent is calculated on the pre GST amount, giving a combined effective rate of 14.975 percent alongside the 5 percent GST.

Why Not Just Subtract The Percentage To Remove Tax?

Because the tax was calculated on the net amount, not on the total. Dividing by one plus the rate recovers the true net; subtracting the percentage of the total overstates the net and understates the tax.

Which Province's Tax Do I Charge?

Generally the customer's province, the place of supply, for goods and services sold across provincial lines. The rules have exceptions by supply type, so confirm yours before invoicing out of province customers.
