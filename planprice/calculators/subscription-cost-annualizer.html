Subscription Cost Annualizer

Free subscription cost calculator: list your monthly subscriptions and see the true annual and three-year cost of your small business software stack.

How it works

Annual cost = monthly total × 12. Trivial math with a non-trivial effect: monthly pricing is designed to look small. The three defaults here, $29 + $15 + $49 = $93/month, are really $1,116 a year, $3,348 over three years, before the price increases most vendors apply annually. Listing every subscription in one place is also the fastest audit there is: anything nobody can explain is a cancellation candidate. (Comparing what those subscriptions should cost is PlanPrice's home turf, see the pricing guides.)

Worked Example

Take one real subscription: Shopify Basic on the Canadian list at CAD 49 per month (checked 2026-10-27). Annualised, that single line is CAD 588 per year, CAD 1,764 over three years, before any price increase. Most stacks hold several lines like it, which is why this calculator totals the whole list rather than one subscription at a time: the annual figure is the one a budget actually feels.

Sources And Limitations

Sources: prices you enter are your own; the Shopify figure above is a PlanPrice research store reading (entity shopify, checked 2026-10-27) from Shopify's published Canadian pricing. Limitations: the annualizer multiplies what you enter, so a per seat subscription entered at its single seat price understates the true line. Enter the total you actually pay per tool each month.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why Annualise A Monthly Price?

Monthly pricing is designed to look small. The annual figure is the honest comparison against salaries, rent, and other yearly costs.

Should I Include Free Trials And Free Tiers?

List them at zero. They matter at renewal time, when a converted trial quietly becomes a paid line nobody re-approved.

How Often Should I Re Run This?

Twice a year is enough for most small businesses, and once whenever you hire, since per seat lines grow with headcount.
