Subscription Cost Annualizer

Free subscription cost calculator: list your monthly subscriptions and see the true annual and three-year cost of your small business software stack.

How it works

Annual cost = monthly total × 12. Trivial math with a non-trivial effect: monthly pricing is designed to look small. The three defaults here — $29 + $15 + $49 = $93/month — are really $1,116 a year, $3,348 over three years, before the price increases most vendors apply annually. Listing every subscription in one place is also the fastest audit there is: anything nobody can explain is a cancellation candidate.
