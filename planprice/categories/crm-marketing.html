Buyer's guide: how to choose CRM & Marketing software

How to compare CRM & Marketing pricing

CRM pricing runs on two axes at once: seats and contacts. HubSpot's free tier covers unlimited users with core CRM, deals, and basic email marketing — the price stays $0 until you outgrow it. The trap is the second axis: paid hubs, marketing contacts, and sales seats are priced separately, so a "free CRM" quietly grows into a paid stack as your contact list and team grow. Map which hubs you'll actually need in year one, and price the contact tier you'll hit, not the one you start on.

Canadian buyers should read the marketing side carefully. Canada's anti-spam law (CASL) is stricter than the US CAN-SPAM rules most American tools are built around: you need consent to send commercial email, and a free tier with basic email marketing doesn't exempt you. If the vendor bills in USD, budget the conversion on top of the sticker price, and check whether the email tools support the unsubscribe and consent records CASL expects you to keep.

The paid jump happens when you add a hub, add seats, or cross a contact threshold; compare on the configuration you'll be running twelve months from now, and confirm current pricing on the vendor's site before committing. If your main need is a simple contact database rather than marketing automation, the free tier may be all you ever need — many small businesses never leave it.
