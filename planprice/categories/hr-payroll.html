Buyer's guide: how to choose HR & Payroll software

How to compare HR & Payroll pricing

HR and payroll tools are priced per employee per month, or — as with Humi on this page — by custom quote. "Custom" means there's no published list price: you request a quote at your headcount, and the price scales with employee count plus which modules you turn on (payroll, benefits, time-off, onboarding). The only honest way to compare is at the same headcount with the same modules: a quote for 10 employees tells you nothing about the cost at 50, and a payroll-only quote tells you nothing about the full platform.

This is the category where a Canadian vendor earns its keep. Canadian payroll compliance — CRA remittances, records of employment, T4s, and provincial rules — is the whole reason to pick a Canadian-built system, and the page flags Humi as exactly that. Ask where employee data is hosted, because payroll records are the most sensitive data a small business holds. And get the quote in writing with the per-employee rate spelled out, so renewal increases don't surprise you.

Reading the prices above: "Custom" means no public list price to verify — the ✓ pill confirms the listing was checked 2026-09-30, not that a price was found. Don't treat one vendor's quote as comparable to another's until both are built on the same headcount and module list, and re-confirm before each renewal. Since there's no list price anchoring the negotiation, the per-employee rate you get quoted is the number to benchmark — get competing quotes at the same headcount and let the vendors compete.
