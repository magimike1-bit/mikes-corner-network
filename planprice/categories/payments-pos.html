How to compare Payments & POS pricing

Payments pricing is two-part: a percentage of each sale plus a fixed fee. Stripe's 2.9% + 30¢ per transaction and Square's free POS software with per-transaction pricing both follow this shape. The number that decides the winner is your average ticket: the fixed 30¢ bites harder on a $5 coffee than on a $500 invoice, so a lower percentage with a higher fixed fee favours big tickets and punishes small ones. There is no monthly fee to recover on the standard plans — the rate is the same whether you process $1,000 or $100,000 — so compare on your actual ticket mix, not on volume discounts you won't get.

Canadian specifics: the 2.9% + 30¢ figure quoted here is the domestic Canadian card rate, and international cards carry extra surcharges — if you sell to US or overseas customers, price that segment separately. In-person and online rates can differ, and card readers are usually a separate hardware purchase, so check what "free POS" actually includes before you count it as zero cost. Also confirm payout timing in CAD: next-day versus two-day payouts matter to a small retailer's cash flow.

Reading the "From" prices above: Stripe's "From 2.9% + 30¢" is a rate, not a subscription — there is no base plan to upgrade from. Square's "From $0" is free software; the cost arrives per transaction. Both verified 2026-09-30; processors change rates with notice, so confirm on the vendor's pricing page before you switch.
