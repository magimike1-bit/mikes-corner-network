The Short Answer, by Buyer Type

  • Solo invoicer: FreshBooks. The estimate to invoice to payment loop is the job, and its shape beats a general ledger first.
  • Growing firm with an accountant: QuickBooks Online. When an accountant names a platform, that answer usually ends the shopping.
  • Multi-currency seller: Xero. Price the tier that includes multi-currency from the start, not the capped entry tier.
  • Zero budget starter: Wave. Free accounting and invoicing that is genuinely free for the core job.

Decision Rules That Actually Decide This

If your accountant names a platform, stop shopping. The accountant relationship costs more than the software saves. A bookkeeper who knows your platform bills fewer hours, files cleaner year ends, and catches problems a stranger to the system misses. Every dollar of licence saving can vanish into one slow year end.

Count monthly invoices before choosing a capped tier. Entry tiers cap the things that matter: billable clients at FreshBooks, invoices and bills on Xero's entry list. Count a normal month, including your busiest week, and price the tier that fits that month. Upgrading mid year at list price, after the intro period has passed, is how the cheap plan becomes the expensive one.

If you bill by the hour, FreshBooks' shape beats a general ledger first. Time tracking that turns into invoices is FreshBooks' home ground. QuickBooks and Xero do it with add-ons and workarounds; FreshBooks does it as the main flow. Product businesses run the rule in reverse: inventory and bank reconciliation depth matter more than invoicing polish.

Currency is a budget line, not a footnote. FreshBooks, QuickBooks, and Wave price in CAD. Xero sells from both a US list and a Canada list, and the two do not match. Confirm which list your quote uses before comparing any Xero figure with a CAD figure from a rival.

One Worked Scenario: The Five Person Firm, Year One

Price the same five person firm four ways, at list, for a full first year. QuickBooks Plus at CAD 110 per month is CAD 1,320, plus Payroll Core at CAD 25 per month, for CAD 1,620 all in. Xero Standard on the Canada list at CAD 60 per month is CAD 720, with payroll priced separately through an integrated payroll app, a second subscription this table does not hide. FreshBooks Plus at CAD 42 per month is CAD 504 for the invoicing led firm that does not need the full suite. Wave Starter is CAD 0, with Pro and payroll as separate decisions if the free tier's limits bite.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which accounting tool should a solo freelancer start with?

FreshBooks if you bill time or projects, Wave if the budget is zero and the books are simple. Graduate to QuickBooks or Xero when an accountant or inventory enters the picture.

Does the cheapest first year win?

No. Intro promos expire and switching accounting software mid year is painful. Compare the year two list prices in the scenario table, not the promo quarter.

Which of these handles Canadian payroll itself?

QuickBooks sells payroll as its own add-on. Xero and FreshBooks pair with separate payroll apps, and Wave offers payroll as a paid add-on. Price the payroll line beside the accounting line, always.
