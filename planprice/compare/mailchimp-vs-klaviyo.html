Mailchimp vs Klaviyo: Email Pricing for Canadian Stores (2026)

Published 2026-10-25 · Last updated 2026-10-25

Verdict up top: Mailchimp is the generalist: cheaper entry ($13/month at 500 contacts on Essentials), a free tier, and a current promotion — 15% off the first 12 months for accounts with 10,000+ contacts on Standard and Premium. Klaviyo is the ecommerce instrument: $20/month at 500 active profiles, priced on profiles rather than contacts, with SMS as a metered add-on ($18 per 1,000 sends in Canada). Stores that live on flows and segments get Klaviyo's value; everyone else should start on Mailchimp's price.

Where they actually differ

The billing units differ. Mailchimp counts contacts (with unsubscribes handled per its plan rules); Klaviyo counts active profiles. Two lists that look the same size can bill differently — compare at your real, cleaned list size, not your raw import.

SMS: Klaviyo prices Canadian SMS explicitly ($18 per 1,000 sends in the data checked). Mailchimp's SMS availability and pricing vary by market — confirm Canadian SMS on the vendor page before building a plan on it.

Depth: Klaviyo's segmentation and ecommerce integrations (built around store data) are its premium justification; Mailchimp covers newsletters, basics and small-list automation at a lower entry price.

Who should pick which

Pick Mailchimp if email is a newsletter-and-basics job, your list is under ~10,000, or the 15% promotion applies to you. Pick Klaviyo if you run an online store and abandoned-cart/browse flows are revenue lines you already measure. Full vendor pages: Mailchimp · Klaviyo.

List-size breakpoints

At 500 contacts/profiles, Mailchimp Essentials ($13) undercuts Klaviyo Email ($20) and probably covers a generalist sender entirely. At 5,000, both bills scale with the list and the features diverge: Klaviyo's flows and segments start producing measurable store revenue, which is the only honest justification for its premium. At 10,000+, Mailchimp's current 15%-off promotion applies for a year (Standard/Premium), temporarily reversing the price story for qualifying accounts — a switching window, not a permanent state. The breakpoint nobody publishes: the size at which your list hygiene lapses. Both platforms bill on stored audience size concepts, so a bloated list taxes you monthly on either — clean the list before you compare the bills.

CASL and the Canadian angle

Canadian senders work under CASL regardless of platform: consent records, identification and working unsubscribe are legal requirements, and both platforms support the mechanics. The practical difference is where your data sits and how each platform handles Canadian SMS (Klaviyo prices it explicitly per 1,000 sends in Canada; confirm Mailchimp's current Canadian SMS terms on its page before relying on them). Neither platform choice changes your CASL obligations — but a platform whose consent fields and audit trail you actually understand is worth more than a feature checklist here.

Frequently asked questions

Which is cheaper at 2,500 contacts? Roughly comparable — both scale with audience size. Price your exact cleaned list size on each vendor page; the deciding factor at this size is usually features (Klaviyo's flows) versus simplicity (Mailchimp), not a few dollars.

Do either charge for unsubscribed contacts? Billing rules differ and change; both platforms' current audience-billing definitions are on their pricing pages. List hygiene — archiving dead contacts — is the reliable saving either way.

Can I move my automations between them? Templates and lists export; automations/flows rebuild manually. Budget a day for a serious program's migration, and run both in parallel for one send cycle.
