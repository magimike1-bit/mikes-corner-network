Semrush vs Ahrefs

2 products, side by side · Each Section Shows Its Own Check Date

The Short Answer, by Job

  • All in one marketing suite: Semrush. Keyword research, site audits, rank tracking, PPC research, and content tools under one subscription.
  • Backlink and SEO depth specialist: Ahrefs. The crawler and the link index are the product, and SEO teams buy it for exactly that depth.
  • Evaluating on a budget: Ahrefs Starter at USD 29 per month is the cheapest honest evaluation in this pair, because Semrush's trial is limited and its entry plan starts far higher.

How PlanPrice ranks, and how the big directories make their money, is documented once, in our methodology.

Decision Rules

Team size first. Both tools price per seat at professional rates, so a suite bought for one analyst and shared informally across a team becomes a limits problem within a quarter. Count the people who will open the tool weekly; that count, not the feature list, sets the real tier.

PPC research is the Semrush tell. If competitor paid search research is part of the job, Semrush is the natural buy, because that research lives inside its suite. If the job is organic only, backlinks, keyword depth, and site audits, Ahrefs covers it without paying for the wider marketing shelf.

Content tooling is the second tell. Semrush bundles content marketing tools into the suite. A content operation that will brief and grade from one dashboard gets value from that bundle; a technical SEO buying crawl and link data does not, and should not pay for it.

Evaluation cost is asymmetric. Ahrefs offers no free trial and no discounts, so its USD 29 Starter tier is the paid evaluation. Semrush's trial is limited, which pushes real evaluation onto its USD 139 entry plan. Budget the evaluation as a purchase, not as a free look, on both sides.

One Worked Scenario: One Seat, Year One

Price one seat for a full year at list, in USD as billed. Semrush's entry SEO plan is USD 139 per month billed monthly, USD 1,668 a year; billed annually at USD 117.33 per month it is about USD 1,408 a year. Ahrefs Lite is USD 129 per month billed monthly, USD 1,548 a year; Ahrefs annual billing takes two months off, so Lite is USD 1,290 a year. The working tiers converge: Semrush Pro+ at USD 299 per month and Ahrefs Standard at USD 249 per month are both serious money, and at that level the choice should be made on the job rules above, not on the few hundred dollars between them.

No. Both bill in USD, so Canadian buyers pay conversion on every figure in the table.
