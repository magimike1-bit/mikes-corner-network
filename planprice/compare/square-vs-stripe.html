Square vs Stripe

The Short Answer, by Channel

  • In-person first: Square. The 2.5% in-person rate with no fixed fee, free POS software, and a CAD 69 reader is the honest starter till.
  • Online first: Stripe. The benchmark online processor, priced 2.9% plus CAD 0.30, with subscription billing and developer tooling Square does not match.
  • Hybrid: neither wins by slogan. The worked scenario below decides it on your channel mix and average ticket.

Decision Rules: The Fixed Fee Is the Whole Game

Run the fixed fee against your average ticket. Stripe's online fixed fee is CAD 0.30 per transaction. At a CAD 15 average ticket that fixed fee alone is 2.0% before the percentage is added. At a CAD 100 ticket it is 0.3%, a rounding error. The same headline rate is expensive for a coffee cart and cheap for an invoice heavy store, which is why no verdict that ignores ticket size is worth reading.

Channel decides the rate, not loyalty. Square's best rate is at the till in person. Stripe's best case is online and through Terminal, its in-person product, which still carries a small fixed fee. A store that takes the online rate at the till, or the till product to its web checkout, pays the wrong rate on every sale until someone audits the statement.

Hardware is a one time rounding error. Square's reader is CAD 69 and Stripe's WisePad 3 is CAD 79. Ten dollars of hardware should never decide a processor; the rate on CAD 30,000 a month decides it thousands of times over.

One Worked Scenario: CAD 30,000 a Month, 1,000 Transactions

Take CAD 30,000 a month across 1,000 transactions, an average ticket of CAD 30, and run it three ways. In person on Square at 2.5%, processing is CAD 750 a month. The same volume online on Stripe at 2.9% plus CAD 0.30 is CAD 870 in percentage fees plus CAD 300 in fixed fees, CAD 1,170 a month. The middle case is Stripe Terminal in person at 2.7% plus CAD 0.05: CAD 810 plus CAD 50, which is CAD 860 a month. The till belongs to Square by CAD 110 to CAD 420 a month on this mix; the web checkout belongs to Stripe on tooling, because Square's own online rate, 2.8% plus CAD 0.30, prices nearly the same as Stripe's while offering less billing depth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use both?

Many hybrid businesses do: Square at the till, Stripe online. The cost is two payout streams to reconcile, which most bookkeepers handle without complaint at this scale.

Which is cheaper for a CAD 15 average ticket?

In person, Square, because there is no fixed fee to amplify. Online, the two are within a few dollars of each other at most volumes, so choose on tooling.

Do either charge monthly fees?

Neither charges a monthly fee for the core processing product. Paid plans exist on both sides for extra features, and each is optional.
