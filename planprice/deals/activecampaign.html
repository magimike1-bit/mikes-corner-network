Think of it as: the automation engine for email-led sales.

ActiveCampaign pairs email marketing with serious automation and an optional CRM. There is no free plan — a 14-day trial capped at 100 subscribers is the on-ramp. Pricing scales by contact count, and unsubscribed and bounced contacts still count toward your tier.

Best for: Businesses whose follow-up sequences are the sales team.

Canadian note: ActiveCampaign bills in USD; contact-count pricing means a Canadian list pays conversion on a number that grows with the list.
