Think of it as: the email veteran for creators.

AWeber is one of the oldest email platforms, aimed at creators and small businesses. Lite caps you at one list and three automations; Plus removes the caps. A $4.99/month Hold package pauses billing — a genuinely useful feature most rivals don't offer.

Best for: Creators and newsletters that send regularly to a modest list.

Canadian note: AWeber bills in USD only — the pricing page lists prices in USD with no CAD option.
