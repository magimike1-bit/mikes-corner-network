Canadian pricing notes

Big Cartel gates its plans by catalog size, not seats: Gold is $0 forever with 5 products and one image each, Platinum is $15 per month for 50 products, and Diamond is $30 per month for 500. Billing is in USD, so Canadian sellers pay conversion on the paid tiers. The platform itself takes no cut of your sales, card processing still runs through your payment gateway at that gateway's rates, which is why the subscription looks so cheap next to a percentage-based platform. Count your SKUs before you pick: the free Gold tier is a genuine home for a five-product shop, but outgrowing it mid-season means migrating under pressure.
