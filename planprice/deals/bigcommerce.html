Think of it as: the Shopify rival that bundles more in the box.

BigCommerce is Shopify's closest hosted rival, recently renamed into Core, Growth and Scale tiers. Annual billing carries a discount over monthly. It tends to include more built-in features (and fewer app purchases) than Shopify at the same tier — the comparison worth doing is total cost with apps included.

Best for: Stores that want more built in and fewer paid apps.

Canadian note: BigCommerce bills in USD; Canadian stores pay conversion on the plan price.
