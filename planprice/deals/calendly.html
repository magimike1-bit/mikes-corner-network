Think of it as: the OpenTable for business meetings.

Calendly lets clients book time with you from a link — no more 'does Tuesday work?' email threads. The free plan covers one event type, which is enough for a solo consultant. Paid tiers add multiple event types, team scheduling, reminders, and CRM integrations, priced per seat.

Best for: Consultants, sales reps, and teams that schedule a lot of external meetings.

What users report

Commonly praised

  • Google/Outlook calendar integration syncs in real time to prevent double-booking.
  • Fast, straightforward initial setup.

Commonly criticized

  • Free plan is limited, pushing users toward paid tiers.
  • Calendar sync issues occur, including missed or duplicated events.
  • Per-user pricing considered expensive for small businesses.
  • Lacks Apple/iCloud calendar support.
