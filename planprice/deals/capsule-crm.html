Think of it as: the CRM for people who found Salesforce exhausting.

Capsule is the deliberately simple CRM: contacts, pipelines and tasks without the enterprise sprawl. Editions scale mainly on contact counts and pipelines. AI features start at the Growth tier, which is also where workflow automation arrives.

Best for: Solo operators and small teams that want a CRM they'll actually keep updated.

Canadian note: Capsule bills in USD; there is no CAD price list.
