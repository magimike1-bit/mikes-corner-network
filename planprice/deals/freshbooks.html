Think of it as: the QuickBooks for freelancers.

FreshBooks is built around invoicing: send estimates and invoices, track time and expenses, and accept payments online. Higher tiers add team members, automation, and inventory tracking.

Best for: Canadian freelancers and service businesses that invoice clients and want accounting in CAD.

Who It Fits, and Who It Does Not

Fits: freelancers and small service firms that bill by time or by project, want CAD native pricing from a Canadian founded company, and live in the estimate to invoice to payment loop.

Does not fit: product businesses that need inventory, and firms with many staff on the books, where the per person team add-on math below quietly eats the tier saving.

Total Cost: A Worked Example

Take a solo consultant who adds one team member on Plus. Plus is CAD 42 per month. The team member add-on is CAD 13 per month. Together that is CAD 55 per month, CAD 660 a year. Add Advanced Payments at CAD 25 per month only if card on invoice volume justifies it; with it, the year is CAD 960. The trap tier is Lite at CAD 26 per month: it caps at 5 billable clients, which a growing practice passes in a quarter, and the upgrade conversation then happens on FreshBooks' schedule, not yours.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the billable client cap actually work?

The cap counts billable clients, not invoices. Lite allows 5, Plus 50, and Premium is unlimited. Archived clients still count until they are deleted, so tidy the client list before you assume you need a bigger tier.

What does a second team member cost?

Team members are a per person monthly add-on on top of the plan. The worked example prices one added member at CAD 13 per month; a second member adds the same again.

Is FreshBooks priced in CAD?

Yes. FreshBooks is headquartered in Toronto and the tiers on this page are Canadian dollar list prices.

Does it file Canadian sales tax?

FreshBooks tracks GST and HST on invoices and reports, which covers the invoicing side of sales tax. Filing itself stays with you or your accountant.

When does Plus stop being enough?

When you pass 50 billable clients, need inventory, or your accountant wants a full general ledger workflow. At that point the comparison is QuickBooks or Xero, not Premium by default.

Before You Sign: The Renewal Checklist

  • Count billable clients the way FreshBooks counts them. Active and archived clients both count toward the cap; deleted clients do not. A ten minute tidy of the client list can be the difference between Lite and Plus at renewal.
  • Use the promo months to test the real tier. The current promotion (80% off for 4 months for new subscribers) applies to whichever tier you start on. Start on the tier your client count needs, because upgrading after the promo ends means paying list sooner on the bigger plan.
  • Decide Advanced Payments with numbers. At CAD 25 per month, the add-on earns its fee only when card on invoice payments are frequent enough that faster payment beats the fee. Occasional card payers should leave it off.
  • Revisit the tier when the team grows. Every team member is a per person add-on, so a firm that adds three staff in a year has quietly added a second plan's worth of cost. Recount people and clients together, twice a year.
