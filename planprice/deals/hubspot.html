Think of it as: the Salesforce for small businesses.

HubSpot's CRM is free for unlimited users, which makes it one of the lowest-risk ways for a small business to get off spreadsheets. The free tier covers contacts, deals, and basic email marketing; paid Starter and Pro editions add automation, deeper reporting, and advanced sales tools. Pricing is per seat, so costs scale with your team.

Best for: Small teams that want a free CRM to start and can upgrade into sales and marketing automation later.
