Think of it as: the Salesforce for small businesses.

HubSpot's CRM is free for unlimited users, which makes it one of the lowest-risk ways for a small business to get off spreadsheets. The free tier covers contacts, deals, and basic email marketing; paid Starter and Pro editions add automation, deeper reporting, and advanced sales tools. Pricing is per seat, so costs scale with your team.

Best for: Small teams that want a free CRM to start and can upgrade into sales and marketing automation later.

Who It Fits, and Who It Does Not

Fits: teams who will live in one CRM and marketing stack, and who can stay on Starter while they prove the process before anyone says the word Professional.

Does not fit: anyone whose year one quote includes Professional without a named admin. The onboarding fees below are the reason, and they are not optional extras.

Total Cost: A Worked Example

Price five seats on Sales Hub Professional, billed annually, in the currency HubSpot actually bills. Professional is USD 90 per seat per month: USD 90 times 5 seats times 12 months is USD 5,400 a year. Add the mandatory onboarding fee of USD 1,500 and year one is USD 6,900. The Starter contrast is the warning: USD 20 per seat times 5 times 12 is USD 1,200 a year, so the Starter to Professional step is not an upgrade, it is a different budget category. Marketing Hub Professional is a second cliff entirely: USD 890 per month on monthly billing plus USD 3,000 of onboarding. Frequently Asked Questions

Why is HubSpot free until it suddenly is not?

Because the free CRM is the top of a priced ladder. Contacts, seats, and hubs are priced separately, so growth on any one axis starts an invoice, and Professional adds mandatory onboarding on top.

What onboarding fees are mandatory?

Professional hubs carry required onboarding: USD 1,500 for Sales Hub Professional and USD 3,000 for Marketing Hub Professional in our readings. Budget them in year one, not as a surprise in month two.

What is the Starter to Pro jump in real dollars?

For five seats, from USD 1,200 a year on Starter to USD 5,400 a year plus USD 1,500 onboarding on Sales Hub Professional. That is the jump to model before anyone falls for a Pro feature in a demo.

Can I negotiate the onboarding fee?

Ask. Partners sometimes deliver onboarding differently, and terms move at quarter end. Get whatever is agreed in writing on the quote, because the default answer is that the fee applies.

What does renewal look like after a promo year?

List price, on more seats and more contacts than you started with, unless you audit both before the renewal date. The year two number is the one to budget on day one.

Before You Sign: The Renewal Checklist

  • Budget year two on day one. Promo years end. The number that matters is the list price for the seats and contacts you will have in month thirteen, and it belongs in the approval email, not in a renewal surprise.
  • Count marketing contacts like an auditor. Contacts are the second price axis. Import discipline, deleting the unengaged, and keeping test lists out of the CRM are worth real money at Professional contact tiers.
  • Name the admin before you sign for Pro. Professional without an owner decays into an expensive address book. If nobody can be named, that is the answer: stay on Starter until someone can.
  • Get onboarding terms in writing. Who delivers it, what it covers, and what happens if the fee is disputed. Mandatory fees are negotiable at the edges, but only before signature, and only in writing.

Switching and Exit Notes

HubSpot is easy to enter and genuinely work to leave, because a mature portal accumulates custom properties, workflows, lists, and reports that encode how the business actually runs. The data exports, but the logic must be rebuilt wherever you land. For a Starter team this is a small problem; for a Professional portal with two years of automation, it is a project with a quarter in it. None of that makes HubSpot a bad buy. It makes the free tier's real function clearer: it is the cheapest possible way to learn whether your team will build its habits inside this ecosystem before the ecosystem becomes load bearing. Use the free and Starter period to keep properties tidy and workflows documented, and the exit stays priced in weeks. Skip that discipline, and the renewal negotiation happens with a vendor who knows your switching cost better than you do, which is the weakest seat at any table.

Verdict

HubSpot is a good product sold like a timeshare presentation. The free tier is real, the Pro invoice is also real. In vendor management we budgeted the year two number on day one; do the same.
