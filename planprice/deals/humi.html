Think of it as: the Gusto of Canada.

Humi (now part of Employment Hero) is a Canadian HR platform covering onboarding, time off, documents, and payroll. The core HR platform is quote-based, you talk to sales for pricing. Payroll is the published piece: $7 per employee per month with a $70 monthly minimum, or a managed-payroll service at $30 per employee per month with a $500 monthly minimum.

Best for: Canadian businesses that want HR and payroll in one Canadian-compliant system.

Total Cost: A Worked Example

Price ten employees on Payroll. At CAD 7 per employee per month, ten employees cost CAD 70 per month, which meets the CAD 70 monthly minimum exactly, so the year is CAD 840. Now the managed contrast, priced honestly: Managed Payroll is CAD 30 per employee per month, which is CAD 300 for ten employees, but the CAD 500 monthly minimum binds at this size, so the real bill is CAD 500 per month, CAD 6,000 a year. The gap between doing payroll yourself and outsourcing it is therefore CAD 5,160 a year at ten staff, and the page should frame it exactly that way: managed payroll is priced for firms that value the hours and the liability cover, not as a small step up.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Humi cost per employee?

Payroll is CAD 7 per employee per month with a CAD 70 monthly minimum. Managed Payroll is CAD 30 per employee per month with a CAD 500 monthly minimum. The HR platform itself is quote priced.

What changed under Employment Hero?

Employment Hero acquired Humi in 2025 and moved to the Employment Hero brand across Canada in September 2025; Humi by Employment Hero remains the product name for Quebec offices and existing Humi customers (entity humi, fact checked 2026-11-13). Watch your renewal terms under the new owner.

Is managed payroll worth it?

At ten employees the minimum makes it CAD 6,000 a year against CAD 840 for self serve payroll. It earns that only if the hours saved and the compliance cover are worth about CAD 430 a month to you.

Does Humi handle Quebec?

The Humi by Employment Hero product remains the offering for Quebec offices, which is a good sign for Quebec payroll support. Confirm your RL-1 and QPP handling in the quote process.

Can I buy payroll without the HR suite?

Payroll carries its own published per employee price, while the HR platform is quote priced. Get the quote itemized so you can see what the suite adds before you sign for both.

Before You Sign: The Renewal Checklist

  • Get the HR modules itemized. Payroll pricing is published per employee; the HR platform is quote priced, and our research holds no dated module prices. An itemized quote is the only way to know what the suite adds, and it is your baseline for every renewal after.
  • Watch the minimums, not just the rates. At small headcounts the minimums are the price: CAD 70 a month on Payroll and CAD 500 on Managed Payroll. A five person firm pays the Managed minimum, which works out to CAD 100 per employee, not the CAD 30 headline.
  • Put the ownership change in the file. Humi is now under Employment Hero, with the brand change completed in Canada in September 2025. Note your current per employee rate and minimums at signing, so any quiet change at renewal is visible against your own record.
  • Decide managed versus self serve with hours. Count the hours payroll and HR admin take a month, honestly. Managed payroll at the CAD 500 minimum buys those hours back; if the count is two hours, self serve wins and the difference is profit.

Switching and Exit Notes

Payroll is the stickiest software a business owns, because the record is statutory: pay history, remittances, and year end filings must remain reachable for years after any switch. Moving payroll providers is therefore a January decision in practice, timed to a new payroll year, with the old provider kept accessible for the history it holds. Any Humi evaluation should include the exit questions up front: how pay history exports, how long records stay available after cancellation, and who owns the year end filing in a transition year. Ask the same of every rival on this page, because the answers differ more than the prices do. The second exit is internal: if your firm grows into needing deeper benefits administration or multi province complexity, the move is to a larger platform, and the itemized quote you collected at signing becomes the baseline that proves what you are leaving behind. Payroll choices are long choices; make this one with the paperwork open.

Canadian payroll with the HR filing cabinet attached. The per employee math is friendly at ten staff. Just confirm what Employment Hero does to your renewal; ownership changes are when quiet price moves happen.
