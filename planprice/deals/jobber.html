Fits: home service businesses, cleaning, landscaping, HVAC, and the trades, dispatching crews of 1 to 15 people.

Does not fit: pure retail or office businesses, and general contractors who need construction grade project accounting rather than job management.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Jobber cost for a five person crew?

On Connect billed annually, USD 149 per month covers the five user crew, USD 1,788 a year before add-ons and tax.

Are prices in USD?

The official price list our research reads is published in USD, including the tier and add-on figures above. Budget the card conversion if you bill clients in CAD.

Which add-ons are worth paying for?

Only the ones tied to a measured problem: missed calls (Receptionist), no review and repeat business engine (Marketing Suite), or a sales pipeline you actually work (Pipeline). At USD 99 a month, Marketing Suite needs to produce work, not just activity.

Does Jobber charge per user or per business?

Both shapes appear: solo tiers price per user, and team bundles price the crew (five users at USD 149 per month on Connect annual, ten users at USD 229). Extra users beyond a bundle cost USD 29 per month each in our readings.

Can clients pay invoices through Jobber, and at what rate?

Yes, clients can pay online through the client hub. The processing rate is the line to confirm on your quote, because it applies to every paid invoice and dwarfs small plan differences at volume.

Before You Sign: The Renewal Checklist

  • Freeze the add-on stack for the first quarter. Turn on the plan, run it clean for ninety days, and add Marketing Suite, Receptionist, or Pipeline one at a time, each against a named problem. Stacks bought in the demo month are usually still being paid for, unused, at renewal.
  • Choose the billing cadence deliberately. Annual billing saves a solo Core user USD 240 a year, and the saving grows with the crew. It is the single cheapest negotiation you will ever do with this vendor, because it requires no conversation at all.
  • Confirm the payment rate on invoices. Clients paying through the client hub is the feature that gets invoices settled faster, and its processing rate applies to every dollar collected. That rate, at real invoice volume, matters more than the plan gap between Core and Connect.
  • Count seats the way crews actually work. Bundles price the crew (five users, ten users), and extra users cost USD 29 per month each. Seasonal staff who need logins for eight weeks can cost more in seats than the plan saves in dispatch time if nobody plans for them.

Verdict

Trades businesses do not die from software features, they die from double booked crews and unpaid invoices. Jobber fixes the second one first. Watch the add-on stack at renewal; that is where I would negotiate.
