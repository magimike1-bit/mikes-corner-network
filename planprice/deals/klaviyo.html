Think of it as: the email platform built for online stores.

Klaviyo is the ecommerce-focused email and SMS platform. It bills on active (emailable) profiles, not emails sent — a distinction that decides your real bill. SMS is priced per send and costs more in Canada than in the US.

Best for: Online stores whose email revenue justifies profile-based pricing.

Canadian note: Klaviyo bills in USD, and SMS sends to Canadian numbers are priced above US sends ($18 vs $15 per 1,000).
