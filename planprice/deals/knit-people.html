Think of it as: Wagepoint's payroll plus the HR features Wagepoint charges extra for, in one price.

Knit is a Toronto-based platform combining Canadian payroll with HR tools — time-off management, document e-signing, onboarding, and performance reviews — under one subscription. The pricing differentiator is unlimited pay runs: weekly payroll, off-cycle corrections, and bonus runs all cost the same flat monthly fee, where per-run competitors charge extra each time.

Best for: Growing Canadian teams (roughly 10–200 employees) that want payroll and HR together without buying two products.

Canadian note: Knit is Canadian-built and bills in CAD. CRA remittances, T4/T4A/RL-1 generation, and provincial tax handling are native. The support team is Toronto-based, which matters when a payroll question needs an answer before the pay run closes.

What users report

Commonly praised

  • Unlimited pay runs at a flat price — a real saving for teams on weekly payroll or frequent off-cycle runs.
  • Modern, intuitive interface compared to legacy payroll providers.
  • HR features included rather than sold as separate add-ons.
  • Canadian support team with payroll expertise.

Commonly criticized

  • No native mobile app — web-based and responsive, but no iOS/Android app.
  • Smaller company means a smaller support team; response times can vary at peak periods.
  • Very complex multi-province or union payroll may exceed its compliance depth versus ADP or Payworks.
