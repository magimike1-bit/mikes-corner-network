Think of it as: Wagepoint's payroll plus the HR features Wagepoint charges extra for, in one price.

Knit is a Toronto-based platform combining Canadian payroll with HR tools, time-off management, document e-signing, onboarding, and performance reviews, under one subscription. The pricing differentiator is unlimited pay runs: weekly payroll, off-cycle corrections, and bonus runs all cost the same base plus per employee fee, where per-run competitors charge extra each time.

Best for: Growing Canadian teams (roughly 10 to 200 employees) that want payroll and HR together without buying two products.

Canadian note: Knit is Canadian-built and bills in CAD. CRA remittances, T4/T4A/RL-1 generation, and provincial tax handling are native. The support team is Toronto-based, which matters when a payroll question needs an answer before the pay run closes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Knit cost for ten employees?

CAD 130 per month on Complete: CAD 50 base plus ten times CAD 8 per employee, which is CAD 1,560 a year.

What is included in Complete?

Full payroll plus the complete HR suite: time off, documents, e-signing, onboarding, and performance tooling in one subscription, with unlimited pay runs.

How does Knit compare with Wagepoint in dollars?

At ten employees Knit Complete is CAD 360 a year more (Knit CAD 1,560, Wagepoint Core CAD 1,200). The decision is the bundle: HR included at Knit, payroll only at Wagepoint.

Does Knit file T4s?

Knit generates T4s and handles CRA remittances as part of Canadian payroll. Confirm your year end checklist during onboarding so nothing is assumed in January.

Is there a base fee minimum that hurts tiny teams?

The CAD 50 base fee means a two person team pays CAD 66 per month on Complete, where Wagepoint Lite would cost CAD 28. Below about five staff, price Lite seriously before you buy the bundle.

Before You Sign: The Renewal Checklist

  • Price the crossover against Wagepoint yourself. At two employees, Knit Complete costs CAD 66 per month against Wagepoint Lite at CAD 28. At ten, Knit Complete is CAD 130 a month against Wagepoint Core at CAD 100. The bundle has to earn its premium; find your own crossover with your real roster before you sign either.
  • Confirm the HR scope you will use. Complete includes the full HR suite, and our research holds only four dated readings for Knit, none of which itemizes benefits administration depth. If benefits are central to your decision, get that scope confirmed on the vendor page and in the quote.
  • Use unlimited runs, or do not pay for them. The base plus per employee price rewards weekly payrolls, off cycle corrections, and bonus runs. A monthly payroll that never runs off cycle is paying for flexibility it never spends; Lite class pricing may fit better.
  • Ask about support at peak. January and February are payroll's hard season everywhere. Ask what response times look like at year end, when every client needs the same week.

Switching and Exit Notes

Canadian payroll data is portable in principle and sticky in practice: the new provider needs year to date figures mid year, and the clean move, as with every payroll system, is at a payroll year boundary with the old system kept readable for history. Knit's base plus per employee pricing helps the decision in one specific way: because there are no per run charges, there is no financial penalty for testing it properly, with parallel runs in a quiet month before the real cutover. Run the parallel test. Payroll is the one category where a trial should reproduce your actual roster, deductions, and a month end, not a demo file. Also record what the bundle replaced. Knit's case is payroll plus HR in one price; if your firm later unbundles, moving HR documents, time off balances, and performance records out is its own small project. Keep originals of signed documents outside the platform as a matter of routine, and the unbundling, if it ever comes, stays routine too.

Verdict

Base plus per head is the pricing shape I trust, because you can model growth without a sales call. At ten people Knit Complete runs CAD 30 a month above Wagepoint Core; pick on the HR features, and make sure they are worth the premium.
