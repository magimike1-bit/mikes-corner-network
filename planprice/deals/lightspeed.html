Think of it as: the Montreal POS for serious retail.

Lightspeed — headquartered in Montreal — is the POS for retailers and restaurants that have outgrown a basic till: deep inventory, multi-location and ecommerce sync. Software is priced per location register plan; Lightspeed Payments charges 2.6% + 10¢ in person. Model software plus processing together — either alone misleads.

Best for: Retailers and restaurants with real inventory complexity or multiple locations.

Canadian note: Lightspeed is a Canadian company (Montreal). Published plan prices are in USD — confirm the billing currency for your account, and budget processing on top of software.
