Think of it as: the first email tool most businesses outgrow.

Mailchimp is the default first email platform for small businesses: campaigns, automations and a free tier to start. Pricing scales by contact count, so the same plan gets more expensive as your list grows — the 5,000-contact prices below show the jump. The current 15%-off offer applies to accounts with 10,000+ contacts.

Best for: Businesses sending regular email campaigns to a growing list.

Canadian note: Mailchimp bills in USD; Canadian lists pay conversion on top of the contact-based price.
