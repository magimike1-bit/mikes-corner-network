Think of it as: the pipeline-first alternative to HubSpot for sales-led teams.

Pipedrive is built around a visual deal pipeline, drag deals between stages, see exactly where every opportunity sits. Unlike HubSpot's free-first model, Pipedrive has no free tier; every plan is paid, starting at $14 per user per month on annual billing. The tradeoff is focus: where HubSpot spreads across marketing, sales, and service hubs, Pipedrive does one thing (sales pipeline) and stays out of your way doing it.

Best for: Small sales teams that want a CRM their reps will actually open every day, without paying for marketing automation they won't use.

Canadian note: Pipedrive bills in USD. A $14 USD seat runs roughly $19 to 20 CAD at typical card exchange rates, budget the conversion, not the sticker price. There's no CAD billing option; the invoice your bookkeeper reconciles will show the USD charge plus your card's foreign-exchange fee.

Who It Fits, and Who It Does Not

Fits: owner led sales teams of 2 to 15 people who need a pipeline they will actually update every day.

Does not fit: marketing led teams that need built-in campaigns, which is HubSpot or ActiveCampaign territory, and enterprises that need Salesforce grade permissions and audit controls.

Total Cost: A Worked Example

Price five reps on Growth, billed annually, in USD as billed. Growth is USD 39 per user per month: USD 39 times 5 times 12 is USD 2,340 a year. Lite at USD 14 per user per month is USD 840 a year for the same five reps, and it is the right starting point for most small teams. Ultimate at USD 79 per user per month is USD 4,740 a year and needs a named feature reason, not a someday argument. One genuine differentiator deserves print: our research records an implementation fee of USD 0 on plans over USD 400 a year, which is a real contrast with HubSpot's mandatory onboarding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Pipedrive tier do small teams actually need?

Most start honestly on Lite and move to Growth when email sync and automation stop being optional. Growth is the practical working tier; buy it when the reps feel the Lite limits, not before.

Is billing in USD?

Yes. Budget the conversion on top of every figure here; the invoice your bookkeeper reconciles carries the USD charge plus your card's exchange fee.

What add-ons push the price up?

LeadBooster, Campaigns, and Projects are billed separately where a tier does not bundle them. Price the stack you will actually run, not the plan alone.

Can I start on Lite and upgrade mid year?

Yes, tiers upgrade in place and your pipeline data comes with you. That is the right sequence: start on Lite, earn the upgrade with usage.

How does it compare with HubSpot Starter in dollars?

Five seats on Pipedrive Growth cost USD 2,340 a year. Five seats on HubSpot Starter cost USD 1,200 a year in our HubSpot readings, but HubSpot's Professional step, the one most growing teams eventually take, is USD 6,900 in year one with onboarding.

Before You Sign: The Renewal Checklist

  • Start one tier below the demo. Demos run on Ultimate. Small teams live honestly on Lite or Growth. The upgrade path preserves your data, so underbuying costs a settings change while overbuying costs a year of seats nobody opens.
  • Audit add-ons at renewal. LeadBooster, Campaigns, and Projects each look small beside the plan. Together they can add a third to the bill. Renew the stack line by line, and make each add-on name the work it did this year.
  • Price in CAD before you approve. Every figure here is USD as billed. Convert at your card's real rate, add the exchange fee, and approve the CAD number. A USD 2,340 year is a materially different budget line in a Canadian ledger.
  • Watch seat drift. Per user pricing punishes untidy teams quietly. Former reps, contractors, and the owner who never logs in all bill the same. A quarterly seat audit is free money on any per seat CRM.

Verdict

The CRM reps do not hate, which is the whole ballgame. A pipeline nobody updates is a spreadsheet with a subscription. Start on Lite, earn the upgrade.

What users report

Commonly praised

  • The visual pipeline is consistently called intuitive, new sales reps are productive within days, not weeks.
  • Activity-based selling (calls, emails, meetings tied to deals) keeps pipelines honest and follow-ups visible.
  • Setup is fast compared to enterprise CRMs; no implementation consultant required for small teams.

Commonly criticized

  • No free tier, every seat is paid from day one, unlike HubSpot's free CRM.
  • Key features (email sync, automations) start at Growth ($39), making the $14 Lite tier feel stripped down.
  • Add-ons (LeadBooster, Campaigns, Projects) are billed separately and can push the real cost well above the plan price.
  • Reporting is basic on lower tiers; serious analytics require Premium or above.
