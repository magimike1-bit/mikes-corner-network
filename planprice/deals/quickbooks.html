Think of it as: the Kleenex of small-business accounting.

QuickBooks Online is the accounting package accountants ask for by name: bank reconciliation, payroll, invoicing, and tax-ready reports in a Canadian edition. Four tiers scale from a sole proprietor to a larger team, all priced in CAD. Intuit frequently runs steep introductory discounts for new subscribers.

Best for: Small businesses that want an accountant-compatible accounting platform priced in CAD.

Who It Fits, and Who It Does Not

Fits: Canadian incorporated and sole proprietor businesses whose accountant already works in QuickBooks, teams that need real multi user books, and retailers with light inventory who want invoicing, bank feeds, and reports in one place.

Does not fit: solo invoicers who send five invoices a month, who pay Plus money for a job FreshBooks or Wave does for less, and businesses that need deep job costing, which QuickBooks Online handles thinly even on higher tiers.

Total Cost: A Worked Example

Take a five person retailer on Plus. Plus is CAD 110 per month, so the subscription line is CAD 110 times 12, which is CAD 1,320 a year. Add Payroll Core at CAD 25 per month, CAD 300 a year, and the year one total is CAD 1,620 before tax. The contrast matters: EasyStart at CAD 30 per month is CAD 360 a year, but it includes one user, so at five users it is false economy, not a saving. The firm would either squeeze everyone through one login, which breaks the audit trail an accountant needs, or upgrade mid year at list price after the intro period has passed.

Alternatives

  • Xero: pick it for cleaner multi-currency handling and a deep app ecosystem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is QuickBooks Online priced in CAD in Canada?

Yes. The Canadian edition lists in Canadian dollars, and the tiers on this page are the Canada list: EasyStart CAD 30, Essentials CAD 70, Plus CAD 110, and Advanced CAD 220 per month.

How many users does each tier actually include?

EasyStart includes 1 user, Essentials 3, Plus 5, and Advanced 25. Count everyone who needs a login, including your accountant, before you pick a tier.

What does payroll cost on top?

Payroll is an add-on. The worked example above uses Payroll Core at CAD 25 per month on top of the Plus subscription.

Can I migrate from Sage 50 or desktop QuickBooks?

Yes, migration paths exist from both, but treat the move as a project: export your history first, agree the cutover date with your accountant, and run the year end in one system only.

What happens to the intro discount at renewal?

The intro price ends and the subscription renews at list. Budget the list price from day one, which is why this page leads with list prices rather than the promo.

Before You Sign: The Renewal Checklist

  • Write the list price in the calendar. The intro discount (currently 90% off for 6 months for new subscribers) ends on a date you can know today. Put the renewal date and the full list figure for your tier beside it, so the jump is a planned budget line and not a surprise invoice.
  • Audit seats quarterly. Tiers are bought in user bands (1, 3, 5, and 25). A former employee's login does not cost extra inside the band, but it is the reason firms drift up a tier at renewal without noticing. Remove leavers, and re-count before any upgrade conversation.
  • Price payroll as its own line. Payroll Core at CAD 25 per month is separate from the subscription in every quote. A comparison that folds payroll into one side's price and not the other's is rigged; insist both sides carry it.
  • Confirm the accountant's tier. If your accountant works in Plus daily and you buy EasyStart, the savings conversation happens again at year end, with interest. The cheapest tier your accountant will actually live in is the real entry price.

Verdict

The default for a reason, and the renewal is where they get you. I have watched stores pay Advanced money for Plus usage for years because nobody audited the seats. Buy Plus, audit users every quarter, and put the renewal date in the calendar with the list price written next to it.
