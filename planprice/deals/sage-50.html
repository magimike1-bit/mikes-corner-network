Think of it as: the desktop accounting your bookkeeper refuses to give up.

Sage 50 is the Canadian desktop accounting line many bookkeepers grew up on, sold as annual licences in Canadian dollars with a separate Canadian price book. Payroll bundles roughly double the licence. It is the non-cloud option in this category — your data lives on your machine, and so does the responsibility for backups.

Best for: Businesses whose bookkeeper already runs Sage 50 and isn't moving to the cloud.

Canadian note: Sage 50 Canada prices are published in CAD on Sage's Canadian site — no conversion, and the Canadian price book is separate from the US one.
