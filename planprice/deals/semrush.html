Think of it as: the Bloomberg Terminal of marketing.

Semrush is the standard all-in-one toolkit for keyword research, site audits, rank tracking, and spying on competitors' search traffic. It is priced per user and aimed at agencies, in-house marketers, and serious content operations. Annual billing cuts roughly 17% off the monthly rate. There is a limited free tier for casual use.

Best for: Agencies and content teams doing serious SEO, PPC research, and competitive analysis.
