Think of it as: the Bloomberg Terminal of marketing.

Semrush is the standard all-in-one toolkit for keyword research, site audits, rank tracking, and spying on competitors' search traffic. It is priced per user and aimed at agencies, in-house marketers, and serious content operations. Annual billing on the entry SEO plan works out to USD 117.33 per month against USD 139 billed monthly. There is a limited free tier for casual use.

Best for: Agencies and content teams doing serious SEO, PPC research, and competitive analysis.

Who It Fits, and Who It Does Not

Fits: agencies, in-house marketers, and serious content operations that will use keyword research, site audits, rank tracking, and competitor analysis every week. The toolkit rewards a team with a process: briefs come out of the keyword data, audits turn into tickets, and rank tracking proves whether the work moved anything.

Does not fit: a small business buying its first SEO tool for occasional checks. The entry plan is priced like professional software because it is professional software, and a solo operator who opens it twice a month is renting a Bloomberg terminal to check one stock. Start with the job, not the brand: if the job is backlink and SEO depth on a budget, read the Ahrefs deal page and the head-to-head comparison linked below before paying for the wider suite.

Alternatives

  • Ahrefs: pick it for backlink analysis and SEO depth, with a cheaper entry tier for evaluation.
  • Moz: pick it for a gentler learning curve and local SEO tooling.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Semrush cost to start?

Our latest research reading records the entry SEO plan at USD 139 per month, with an annual billing figure of USD 117.33 per month (entity semrush, checked 2026-11-10). The plan ladder was renamed in 2026, so match the plan name on your quote to the current ladder before you compare it with older reviews.

Is there a useful free trial?

Trial utility is one of the criticisms summarized in the reviews section above: the trial is limited, so go in with a fixed list of questions, the keywords, competitors, and audit checks you need answered, and use the trial days to answer exactly those.

Semrush or Ahrefs for a small Canadian business?

Semrush is the all in one marketing suite, Ahrefs is the SEO depth specialist. Both bill in USD. The comparison page works one seat for a year on each from our research readings, which is the honest way to choose.

Verdict

The heavy duty toolkit earns its price in a team that runs on it daily. Buy it for a workflow, not for a logo on a shortlist, and count seats and projects before the renewal does it for you.
