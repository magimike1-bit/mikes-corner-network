Think of it as: the WordPress of ecommerce.

Shopify hosts your online store, checkout, and payments in one platform, the reason it powers most new ecommerce businesses. Pricing is USD at list; Canadian merchants are billed in CAD at checkout at Shopify's exchange rate. The Grow and Advanced tiers cut transaction fees and add reporting, while Plus targets high-volume brands.

Best for: Businesses selling products online that want a managed storefront without managing servers.

Who It Fits, and Who It Does Not

Fits: retailers selling online and in store who want one inventory pool, one checkout, and one set of reports across both channels.

Does not fit: content only sites, which pay ecommerce money for a brochure (see the Wix and Squarespace deal pages), and high volume sellers who have not modelled the gateway and POS Pro lines before signing.

Total Cost: A Worked Example

Take a single store on Basic, billed monthly. Basic is CAD 49 per month, which is CAD 588 a year. Billed annually the same tier is CAD 37 per month, CAD 444 a year, a CAD 144 saving for paying up front. Now the line the headline hides: POS Pro is CAD 119 per location per month, CAD 1,428 a year, if the store needs full POS features such as advanced inventory and register roles. The honest year one range for one store is CAD 444 (annual billing, no POS Pro) to CAD 2,016 (monthly billing plus POS Pro). Model both lines before you fall in love with the CAD 49 headline.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Shopify actually cost in Canada, in CAD?

The Canada list for Basic is CAD 49 per month billed monthly, or CAD 37 per month billed annually. Add POS Pro, apps, and processing before you call it the total.

Is POS Pro worth it for one store?

Only if you use what it adds: deeper inventory, register roles, and exchange workflows. At CAD 1,428 a year per location it more than doubles a Basic bill, so trial the Lite POS first.

What are the third-party gateway fees?

If you take payments through an outside processor instead of Shopify Payments, Shopify adds a per transaction fee that steps down by tier. At volume that fee is the strongest argument for comparing BigCommerce, which does not charge one.

When does Grow beat Basic?

When the lower gateway fee and extra staff accounts save more than the tier difference, or when you need the reporting Grow adds. Do the math on your own card volume, not on the feature list.

What does Plus cost, and who needs it?

Plus is the high volume plan, priced from USD 2,300 per month at list. It earns its keep on checkout scale, multiple stores, and wholesale tooling, not on a single shop.

Before You Sign: The Renewal Checklist

  • Separate the three bills. Subscription, processing, and apps arrive as different lines and grow for different reasons. Review them separately each quarter; a store that watches only the subscription misses the two lines that scale with success.
  • Decide POS Pro per location, not per brand. At CAD 119 per location per month, a flagship store can justify register features that a kiosk cannot. Mixed estates should run mixed setups rather than one blanket tier.
  • Use the promo window for setup, not for deciding. The $1 per month introductory window is the cheapest time to migrate products, test the till, and train staff. It is a terrible time to judge the platform's real cost, because almost none of the real lines are visible yet.
  • Model annual billing against cash flow. The CAD 144 saving on Basic is real, but annual prepayment is still cash out the door in month one. Take it when the business is stable; keep monthly flexibility while the store format is still changing.

Verdict

The platform fee is the small print nobody reads backwards. In retail the POS Pro line is the real price. Model both lines, the subscription and the till, before you commit the store to it.
