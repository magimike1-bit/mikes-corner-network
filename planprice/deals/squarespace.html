Think of it as: the beautiful website that also sells.

Squarespace is the design-first website builder whose commerce tiers double as an online store. Watch the transaction fee: the entry tier historically carried a 3% fee that disappears on the commerce plans — on real volume that fee dwarfs the plan difference.

Best for: Design-conscious businesses selling a modest catalogue online.

Canadian note: Squarespace bills in USD; Canadian sellers pay conversion and should check the transaction-fee tier carefully.
