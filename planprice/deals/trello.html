Think of it as: the digital corkboard for team tasks.

Trello organizes work on boards, lists and cards — the simplest visual project tracker in this directory. The free plan caps a workspace at 10 boards; paid plans remove the cap and are billed per user. The cost trap is guests: multi-board guests in a paid workspace bill at the full seat rate.

Best for: Small teams that want a visual board without a project-management learning curve.

Canadian note: Trello bills in USD. Budget the card conversion on top of the sticker price — there is no CAD price list.
