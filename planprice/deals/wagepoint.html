Think of it as: payroll done the Canadian way, without paying for an HR suite you don't need.

Wagepoint was built in Canada for Canadian payroll — CPP/QPP, EI, federal and provincial tax tables, T4/T4A and RL-1 generation, and ROEs are all native, not adapted from a US product. Pricing is a flat base fee plus a small per-employee charge, with no per-pay-run fees on the Unlimited plan. That makes it one of the most predictable payroll costs a small business can budget.

Best for: Canadian small businesses (roughly 1–100 employees) that want straightforward, compliant payroll without a full HR platform.

Canadian note: This is the rare vendor on PlanPrice that bills in CAD natively — the invoice matches your books with no conversion. Being Canadian-built also means provincial edge cases (QST, Quebec parental insurance, Ontario ESA rules) are handled as first-class features rather than afterthoughts.
