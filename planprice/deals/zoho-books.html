Think of it as: the per-organization alternative to per-seat accounting.

Zoho Books prices per organization rather than per user, which makes it unusually cheap for teams. The free tier covers businesses under US$50K revenue with 1,000 invoices a year. It pairs naturally with Zoho CRM for businesses buying into the Zoho suite.

Best for: Small teams that need several accounting seats without per-seat pricing.

Canadian note: Zoho bills in USD for the Canadian edition; per-organization pricing means the conversion hits one line, not every seat.
