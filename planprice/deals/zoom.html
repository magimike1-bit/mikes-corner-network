Think of it as: the meeting room everyone already knows how to join.

Zoom Workplace bundles meetings with chat, whiteboard and mail. The Business tier carries a 10-licence minimum — teams under 10 pay for 10 — which makes Pro the real small-team tier. Phone and webinar add-ons are priced per user on top.

Best for: Small teams whose day runs on video calls with clients.

Canadian note: Zoom bills in USD; the Phone US & Canada plans are the relevant add-ons for Canadian teams.
