6 products with a free tier · prices checked 2026-09-30

How this page works

Every product on this page has a $0 tier — not a free trial, a tier you can stay on indefinitely. The six currently tracked: HubSpot (Free Tools — unlimited users with core CRM, deals, and basic email marketing), Monday.com (Free — up to 2 seats with basic boards), Wave (Starter — free accounting and invoicing), Square (Square POS software plus a free Appointments tier for one location), Calendly (Free — one event type on one scheduling page), and Big Cartel (Gold — 5 products with one image each). The free-notes line under each product spells out exactly what the $0 tier includes and where it caps.

The cap is the whole story with free tiers. Before you commit to one, check the limit that will hit you first: seats (Monday.com's 2-seat ceiling), products (Big Cartel's 5-product ceiling), event types (Calendly's single event type), or processing costs (Square's POS software is free, but card processing still charges per transaction — the software is free, the payments aren't). A free tier is genuinely free within its caps; the question is whether your actual usage fits inside them.

Free tiers change less often than promos, but they do change — vendors quietly adjust seat caps and feature lists. Each tier on this page was confirmed against the vendor's own pricing page on 2026-09-30, and each product's deal page shows the full pricing table so you can see what the paid upgrade costs when you outgrow $0. Confirm the current free-tier terms on the vendor's site before you build a workflow on them.
