Multi-currency billing

Definition: Whether the vendor bills and operates in your currency — and what happens when it does not. List-price currency and billing currency are different questions.

Why it matters when comparing prices

For Canadian buyers this is a first-pass filter: FreshBooks, Wave, and QuickBooks bill in CAD, and Shopify bills Canadian merchants in CAD at checkout, while Xero lists in USD. Beyond the sticker, ask how the product handles CAD bank feeds and tax categories — billing in your currency means little if the bookkeeping does not follow.

What to watch for in Canadian pricing

Ask one question before anything else: what currency actually hits my card? List-price currency, billing currency, and settlement currency are three different things — a vendor can advertise CAD prices, invoice in USD, and settle through a US entity, and only the last one determines your foreign transaction fee. Ask sales to confirm the charge currency in writing, and check a real invoice (or a trial invoice) rather than trusting the pricing page.

Then check the operational side. If the vendor bills in CAD, confirm whether their Canadian entity charges GST/HST or QST and shows it as a separate line — you will need that for input tax credits if you are registered. If they bill in USD, budget the ~2.5% card conversion fee on every invoice and reconcile the CAD amounts on your statements, since the converted total will drift from the invoiced USD total every month. Either way, ask whether you can switch billing currency mid-contract without being repriced.

A concrete example

Illustrative arithmetic, not a real vendor quote: say a tool lists CAD 50 a month but invoices USD 36. At a typical exchange rate that converts to about CAD 49 — close to the list price — but the card's ~2.5% foreign transaction fee adds roughly CAD 1.25, so the statement shows about CAD 50.25 each month, drifting with the exchange rate. A buyer comparing it against a true CAD 50-a-month competitor is really comparing about CAD 50.25 (variable) with CAD 50.00 (fixed).
