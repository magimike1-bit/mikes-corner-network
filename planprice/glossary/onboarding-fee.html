Onboarding fee

Definition: A one-time charge for setup, data migration, or training when you start. Sometimes waived, sometimes mandatory, sometimes disguised as implementation.

Why it matters when comparing prices

Add the onboarding fee to the year-one total before comparing vendors — a cheaper monthly rate with a steep setup fee often loses. Ask exactly what the fee covers, whether migration help is included, and whether it can be waived; it frequently can be, but only if you ask before signing.

What to watch for in Canadian pricing

Onboarding fees are a favourite hiding spot for currency traps. A US vendor may advertise the monthly plan in CAD but quote the setup fee in USD — or run the two through different billing entities — so the one-time charge converts at a worse rate and picks up the ~2.5% card foreign transaction fee on top. Ask for the fee quoted in CAD, fixed in writing, and check that it is charged in the same currency as the subscription; split-currency invoices also make your bookkeeping and input tax credit claims messier than they need to be.

Get the scope in plain line items: data import, workflow configuration, training sessions, and whether anyone travels. Remote onboarding should cost less than on-site work, and if a vendor flies a specialist to your office, travel time and expenses often sit outside the quoted fee entirely. Put the waiver question on the table early — onboarding fees are frequently waived in competitive deals, and the waiver is easiest to win before you have signed, while the vendor still fears losing the deal.

A concrete example

Illustrative arithmetic, not a real vendor quote: say a 12-person team buys an HR platform at CAD 15 per employee per month — 12 × 15 × 12 = CAD 2,160 a year — plus a one-time onboarding fee of CAD 1,500. Year one costs CAD 3,660, which means the fee is nearly 70% of the first year's subscription. Negotiating that fee down to CAD 500 saves CAD 1,000, which covers almost half of year two. The monthly rate was never the expensive part of this deal.
