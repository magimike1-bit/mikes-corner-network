SaaS

Definition: Software you rent by subscription and use in a browser instead of buying once and installing. You pay for access, and the vendor handles hosting, updates, and security.

Why it matters when comparing prices

Nearly every product on PlanPrice is SaaS, so the pricing question is never the sticker price — it is the total cost over the years you will use it. Compare subscription totals across two or three years, not month one, and remember that when you stop paying, access ends: your data export plan matters as much as the monthly rate.

What to watch for in Canadian pricing

Most SaaS vendors are American, and many list prices in USD while billing your Canadian card in USD too. That means the exchange rate and your card's foreign transaction fee — typically around 2.5% on non-CAD purchases — stack on top of the subscription every single month. Before you compare two tools, check for a CAD pricing page or a "billed in CAD" note at checkout; if the checkout says USD, add roughly 2.5% to every quote and expect the total to drift with the exchange rate. Ask sales for a CAD quote in writing — vendors with real Canadian operations can usually produce one.

Then look at the invoice itself. Software bought from outside Canada is taxable here, and the rate depends on your province — 5% GST in Alberta, 13% HST in Ontario, 15% in the Atlantic provinces, 14.975% combined in Quebec — so the tax line can exceed a month of the subscription. If your business is GST/HST registered, you need an invoice that shows the tax separately to claim input tax credits; some US vendors' invoices show no tax line at all. Ask how they handle Canadian sales tax before year one, not at tax time.

A concrete example

Illustrative arithmetic, not a real vendor quote: say a 12-person team subscribes to a project-management tool at CAD 20 per seat per month. That is 12 × 20 = CAD 240 a month, or CAD 2,880 a year. Add 13% Ontario HST and the real annual cost lands at about CAD 3,254 — the tax line alone costs more than two months of the subscription. If the same tool billed in USD instead of CAD, the card's ~2.5% foreign transaction fee would stack roughly CAD 72 on top, every year, for nothing.
