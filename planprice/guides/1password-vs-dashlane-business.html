1Password vs Dashlane Business Pricing: The 10-User Team Math

Published 2026-10-12

Most 1Password-vs-Dashlane comparisons price the personal plans. Businesses buy differently: per-seat business tiers, admin controls, and what happens when the team grows past 10. And for Canadian buyers there's a fact the roundups skip — 1Password is a Toronto company. This guide prices the business tiers for real team sizes, with a dated October 2026 check.

The 10-user team: why the Teams Starter Pack changes everything

At exactly 10 users, this isn't close. 1Password's Teams Starter Pack is a flat US$24.95/month — US$299.40/year (≈ CA$427). Dashlane at US$8/user/month is US$80/month — US$960/year (≈ CA$1,369). The flat pack makes 1Password less than a third of Dashlane's price at this team size.

But the pack caps at 10 seats, and eSecurity Planet's reviewer calls the per-user math cleanly: 1Password wins for small teams on the flat pack; at standard business level, Dashlane's flat US$8/user/month undercuts 1Password's US$8.99. Their overall ratings land at 1Password 4.6/5 and Dashlane 4.7/5 — effectively tied, with Dashlane's credential-risk detection and phishing protection against 1Password's usability and developer workflows.

Seat 11: the growth cliff

Hire an 11th person and the 1Password math jumps: the flat pack no longer applies, and Business at US$8.99/user/month means 11 users cost US$98.89/month (US$1,186.68/year ≈ CA$1,692). The same 11 on Dashlane: US$88/month (US$1,056/year ≈ CA$1,506). At 15 users it's US$1,618/year vs US$1,440/year.

The crossover is worth knowing before you buy: 1Password is dramatically cheaper at ≤10 seats and slightly more expensive past it. A team planning to grow from 8 to 15 within the year should price the year-two bill, not the day-one bill.

The Canadian angle: 1Password is a Toronto company

1Password is built by a Toronto-based company (company profile) — the rare case where the "buy local" instinct and the cheapest option align for a small Canadian team. Practically, a Canadian vendor means support in your timezone and a supplier in your own legal jurisdiction, which simplifies procurement conversations. Dashlane, meanwhile, bundles a VPN and AI-powered phishing detection that 1Password doesn't offer at any tier — if your team needs the VPN anyway, Dashlane's bundle can displace a separate subscription.

What reviewers say

On support, SafetyDetectives gives Dashlane the win — live chat, multilingual support, and developers active in the Reddit community — while noting 1Password has no live chat but fast help-center answers. Their reviewer still leans slightly toward 1Password overall for its apps and browser extensions. Spotsaas, which re-checked every vendor's pricing page in September 2026, calls 1Password the best pick for most businesses on rollout ease and Dashlane the fit for companies that want credential-risk detection alongside the vault.

Decision: which team buys which

Buy 1Password if you have 10 or fewer people and can lock the Teams Starter Pack — nothing touches US$299/year — or if your developers need its secrets-management tooling and Secret Key architecture. Buy Dashlane if you're past 10 seats and want predictable US$8/user/month scaling, if the bundled VPN displaces a subscription you already pay for, or if credential-risk monitoring and phishing protection are the actual job. Either way, price the team you'll be in twelve months, not the team you are today.

Frequently asked questions

Is 1Password cheaper than Dashlane for business?

At 10 or fewer users, yes — dramatically: US$24.95/month flat vs US$8/user/month. Past 10 users, Dashlane is slightly cheaper per seat (US$8 vs US$8.99).

Which has better security, 1Password or Dashlane?

Reviewers rate them equally overall. 1Password uses a Secret Key plus account password (two-key derivation); Dashlane uses zero-knowledge architecture with AES-256 and Argon2 key derivation. Both have undergone independent security validation.

Do either offer a free business plan?

No — neither offers a free business tier. Both offer 14-day trials; Dashlane's personal free tier is capped at 25 passwords on one device, which functions more as a trial than a usable free plan.
