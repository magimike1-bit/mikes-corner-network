ActiveCampaign Pricing Canada: Plans by Contact Tier

Published 2026-10-12

ActiveCampaign does not sell you software — it sells you contact slots. The headline price of US$15 a month is real, but only at 1,000 contacts on the cheapest plan; grow the list and the same plan costs ten times more. Add USD billing for Canadian buyers and a monthly send cap most teams never notice, and the pricing page needs a translator. This guide is that translator.

General information, not financial advice. Prices change — always confirm the current rates on the vendor's own pricing page before buying.

The four plans at 1,000 contacts

ActiveCampaign prices by plan and contact count, checked October 2026 (USD, annual billing, 1,000 email contacts). Starter: US$15/month — marketing automation, email marketing, limited segmentation. Plus: US$49/month — standard segmentation, landing pages, deeper automation. Pro: US$79/month — advanced segmentation, predictive content, CRM integrations, 3 users. Enterprise: US$145/month — premium segmentation, custom objects, SSO, 5 users. Month-to-month billing runs roughly 20%+ higher: US$19 / US$59 / US$99 / US$179. There is no free plan — only a 14-day trial — and a 30-day money-back guarantee.

For Canadian buyers: ActiveCampaign bills in USD. At recent rates (≈ US$1 = CA$1.43), Starter at 1,000 contacts is about CA$21.50 a month, and Pro is about CA$113. Budget the FX spread into every comparison with CAD-billed rivals.

The send cap: the limit nobody quotes

Every plan caps monthly email sends at 10x to 15x your contact count (10x on Starter and Plus, up to 15x on Enterprise). At 1,000 contacts on Starter, that is 10,000 emails a month — about 2.5 emails a week per contact. A list that emails weekly plus automations can hit the send ceiling before the contact ceiling, and the pricing page does not model this. SMS is a separate add-on on Plus and above — roughly US$16.83 a month for 1,000 sends — and WhatsApp/SMS credits stack on top of the plan price.

What reviewers say

ActiveCampaign holds 4.4 out of 5 on G2 from over 14,780 reviews and 4.6 out of 5 on Capterra from 2,560 reviews. A verified GetApp Canada reviewer, Emily, gave it 9/10 after two-plus years of daily use: automated workflows keep "communication consistent without manual follow-ups," with the learning curve on advanced features the main complaint. That split — powerful automation, real learning curve — is the consistent theme across review platforms. The automation depth is why teams accept the contact-based pricing; the learning curve is why small teams sometimes overbuy and underuse.

ActiveCampaign vs Brevo: contact pricing vs send pricing

The fundamental choice in email marketing pricing is contacts vs sends. ActiveCampaign charges by contacts (list size); Brevo charges by monthly email volume. At 1,000 contacts Brevo Starter is about US$17 a month to ActiveCampaign's US$19 — close. At 20,000 contacts with modest sending, Brevo's send-based model pulls far ahead, because Brevo's Starter tier can hold up to 500,000 contacts at the same send allowance. Heavy senders with big lists should price Brevo; frequent mailers with small, engaged lists can favor ActiveCampaign's model. Know which kind of sender you are before comparing headline prices.

The decision framework

This is a buying-decision guide, not a setup procedure — the decision framework near the end is the whole process.

  1. Price at your 18-month contact count, not today's. The ladder is steepest between 1,000 and 10,000 — today's cheap plan is tomorrow's US$189.
  2. Check sends against the 10x cap before the contact count; weekly mailers hit the send ceiling first.
  3. Convert to CAD at your card's rate — the USD price is not the price a Canadian business pays.
  4. Don't buy Pro for features you'll learn "later." The learning-curve complaints are real; buy the automation depth you'll actually configure.
  5. Compare against send-based pricing (Brevo) if your list is large and your cadence is light.

Frequently asked questions

Does ActiveCampaign have a free plan?

No — only a 14-day free trial on paid plans, plus a 30-day money-back guarantee. Budget from day one.

How many emails can I send per month?

10x your contact count on Starter and Plus, up to 15x on Enterprise. At 1,000 contacts on Starter, that is 10,000 emails a month.

What happens when I outgrow the Starter plan?

Starter caps at 25,000 contacts. Past that — or when you need deeper automation, landing pages, or CRM integrations — the move is Plus, which converges in price with Starter as lists grow.
