Asana vs Monday vs ClickUp Pricing: Three-Way Shootout and the Minimum-Seat Trap

Published 2026-10-11

Per-seat stickers say ClickUp $7, monday.com $9, Asana $10.99 — a tidy ladder that falls apart the moment you price your actual team. Monday's 3-seat minimum means a solo buyer pays for three. Asana's free plan stops at two users while ClickUp's covers unlimited members. And none of the stickers include AI. This guide prices real teams, minimums applied, with 2026 list rates.

Note the automation gap hiding behind the prices: monday.com Standard caps automations at 250 actions/month (25,000 on Pro), while Asana includes unlimited rules from Starter up and ClickUp includes unlimited automations on paid plans.

The minimum-seat trap: monday's 3-seat floor

Monday.com doesn't sell you one seat. Paid plans carry a 3-seat minimum, so the "$9/seat" sticker has a $27/month floor. A freelancer buying Basic pays $324/year for one person — more than Asana Starter would cost that same solo user ($131.88/year). A 2-person team pays for three seats: $324/year on Basic.

The trap bites hardest at the bottom and disappears at the top: at 10 users the minimum is irrelevant. But most comparisons quote $9/seat without ever multiplying by the minimum, which is exactly backwards — the smaller the team, the more the minimum matters.

The AI add-on nobody puts in the table

AI pricing is the least comparable line in project management software. Trackers report ClickUp AI as a paid per-seat add-on, while Asana and monday bundle AI features differently by tier — and all three vendors have moved the packaging in the last year. Whatever the comparison says, add the AI line to your shortlist total from each vendor's current pricing page before you decide; it's the one cost that can reorder the ranking.

Decision framework: which tool for which team

  • Cheapest credible setup: ClickUp — lowest sticker, unlimited free members, unlimited automations on paid plans. The tradeoff is a steeper learning curve.
  • Visual teams that adopt fast: monday.com — but price the 3-seat minimum first if you're under 5 people.
  • Structured, goal-driven orgs: Asana — portfolios, goals, and workflow approvals; the premium is for governance, not features-per-dollar.
  • Under 3 people: rule monday.com out on price alone unless you need its specific workflow — the minimum makes it the most expensive option at the smallest size.

FAQ

What is monday.com's minimum seat purchase?

Three seats. Monday.com sells paid plans with a 3-seat minimum, so a solo buyer or a 2-person team pays for three seats — $324/year on Basic before any discount.

Which is cheapest for a small team: Asana, Monday, or ClickUp?

ClickUp, at every headcount in the comparison. At 5 users on annual billing: ClickUp Unlimited $420/year, monday.com Basic $540/year (5 seats), Asana Starter $659.40/year — and ClickUp's free plan covers unlimited members.

Is Asana still free?

Only for up to 2 users. Asana's free plan now stops at two users; larger teams start at $10.99/user/month on Starter with annual billing.
