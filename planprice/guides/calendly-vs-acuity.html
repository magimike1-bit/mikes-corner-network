Calendly vs Acuity for Canadian Service Businesses

Last updated 2026-10-05

Calendly and Acuity Scheduling solve the same problem — ending the booking-email thread — but they were built for different customers. Calendly is a meeting scheduler that grew into payments; Acuity is an appointment-and-payments platform for businesses that sell their time. For a Canadian salon, clinic, or contractor, that difference decides the winner before you ever open a feature list.

The staffing math: per-seat vs flat fee

Calendly multiplies by headcount; Acuity is flat-fee with a cap on calendars:

  • Solo: Calendly Standard (US$10/seat ≈ CA$14.24) beats Acuity Starter (US$16 ≈ CA$22.79) by ≈ CA$8.55/month — ≈ CA$103/year.
  • Two staff: Calendly Teams at 2 seats (US$32 ≈ CA$45.58/month) loses to Acuity Standard (US$27 ≈ CA$38.45/month) — 6 calendars, packages, and SMS reminders included.
  • Three staff: Calendly Teams at 3 seats (US$48 ≈ CA$68.37) versus Acuity Premium (US$49 ≈ CA$69.79) — within ≈ CA$1.40. Price is a wash; features decide.

The crossover lands between two and three people. The 7-day Acuity trial against Calendly's free plan is the honest way to confirm which side you're on.

Pick by use case

  • Salon or barbershop: Acuity — per-stylist calendars, a browsable service menu, deposits at booking, and packages for regulars. If you already take Square in person, Acuity's Square integration keeps payments in one place.
  • Clinic — physio, dental, wellness: Acuity — intake forms attached to each booking, packages for treatment plans, and a HIPAA-tier option on Premium. Check either tool's data handling against your provincial privacy obligations first.
  • Contractor or mobile trades: Acuity — a deposit at booking is the strongest no-show protection, and intake forms capture job details before you drive out.
  • Coach or consultant selling packages: Acuity — packages with payment plans, memberships, and gift cards on Standard and up. If your "coaching" is really paid Zoom calls, Calendly Standard is simpler and cheaper.
  • When Calendly wins: you book meetings, not appointments — sales calls, consultations, interviews. You want the free tier, the simplest link for non-technical clients, or deep Salesforce/HubSpot integration. (Squarespace users note: Squarespace acquired Acuity in 2019, so Acuity's native embed is the path of least resistance.)

Quick checklist

  • One call type fits Calendly's free plan; a service menu needs Acuity.
  • Taking money at booking — deposits, packages — is Acuity's home turf.
  • Count staff and run the math above: solo favours Calendly, two-plus usually favours Acuity.
  • Budget in USD — both bill Canadians in US dollars; CAD figures are estimates, taxes extra.
  • Trial both first: Acuity's 7 days against Calendly's free plan.

FAQ

Does Calendly have a free plan?

Yes — free forever, with one event type and one connected calendar. Paid tiers start at US$10 per seat per month on annual billing (≈ CA$14.24 at current rates; both vendors bill in USD).

Does Acuity Scheduling have a free plan?

No. Acuity offers a 7-day trial on any plan, then paid plans start at US$16 per month on annual billing (≈ CA$22.79 at current rates; both vendors bill in USD). No permanent free tier exists.

Which is better for a small service business?

Acuity, if you sell time to clients and need intake forms, deposits, packages, or class scheduling — salons, clinics, coaches, studios. Calendly, if you book meetings and calls, want the free tier, or need deep CRM integration.

How much does Acuity Scheduling cost in Canada?

US$16, US$27, or US$49 per month for Starter, Standard, and Premium on annual billing — all USD-billed (≈ CA$22.79, CA$38.45, CA$69.79 at the Oct 5, 2026 mid-market rate; taxes extra). Monthly is roughly 17–20% higher (US$20, US$34, US$61).
