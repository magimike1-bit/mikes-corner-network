Calendly vs Microsoft Bookings: The Bundling Question

Published 2026-10-12

Every Calendly-vs-Bookings comparison that lines up two per-seat prices is asking the wrong question. Microsoft Bookings has no price — Microsoft doesn't sell it standalone at any price. It rides inside your Microsoft 365 licence. So the real comparison is: what does one more Calendly seat cost you versus what you're already paying Microsoft? This guide does that math, flags the SKU trap, and covers Calendly's new 2026 tiers.

General information, not financial advice. Prices change — always confirm the current rates on each vendor's own pricing page before buying.

The bundling question first

If your team already runs Microsoft 365 Business Basic or higher with Teams, Bookings costs you exactly $0 extra — it's included. Buying Calendly on top means paying per seat for a second scheduling layer. If you don't run Microsoft 365, the comparison flips: Bookings requires buying M365 licences (from ~US$7/user/month), and Calendly's US$10/seat/month starts looking competitive. Start from your current stack, not the feature list.

The Teams-less SKU trap

Here's the detail that trips up buyers: Microsoft sells cheaper Business Basic SKUs without Teams (around US$5.40/user/month) — and Bookings is only "included in the version with Teams." Buy the no-Teams SKU to save a dollar-fifty and you get no scheduling at all; the real floor for Bookings is ~US$7/user/month. If you're renewing or buying M365 anyway, check the SKU line before assuming Bookings comes with it.

What you give up with Bookings

Zero marginal cost is real, but so are the compromises. Trafft's head-to-head is blunt: Bookings wins on governance and native Teams meetings — no new vendor, no new data processor — but calendars must be Microsoft, branding is thin, there's no payment processing or weighted distribution, and reporting stops at a tab-separated export. Calendly brings round-robin and routing forms on Teams tier, built-in payment collection, 150+ integrations, and a polished invitee page — but costs scale linearly with headcount with no relief below 31 seats, and client data leaves your tenant, which triggers a vendor review in regulated sectors.

Do the math for your team: three scenarios

  • 8 people, already on M365 Business Basic: Bookings US$0 extra. Calendly Standard would be 8 × US$10 × 12 = US$960/yr for the second layer.
  • 8 people, no M365, Google shop: Calendly Standard US$960/yr vs buying M365 Basic for Bookings at 8 × US$7 × 12 = US$672/yr — but that's US$672 for email and Office too, so compare the whole bundle, not just scheduling.
  • Sales team needing round-robin: Bookings can't do automated distribution (manual staff selection only). Calendly Teams at 8 × US$16 × 12 = US$1,536/yr is the price of the feature — Bookings isn't a cheaper alternative here, it's a different product.

What reviewers say

Software Advice (updated September 30, 2026) scores Calendly 4.7/5 across 4,163 reviews — 79% from small businesses — against Bookings' 4.4/5 across 168 reviews. Calendly's pros: client self-scheduling, clean interface; cons: high cost, UI complexity for first-timers. Bookings' pros: seamless Outlook/Teams integration; cons: confusing navigation and complex initial setup. One Calendly reviewer, a lead design researcher, calls it "an indispensable tool in my workflow when scheduling customer interviews" (5.0/5); a Bookings reviewer, a scientist, says it "makes booking for meetings whether in person or virtual a breeze" (4.0/5). Capterra's head-to-head lands similarly: Calendly 4.8 (1,523 reviews) vs Bookings 4.5 (80).

Decision: which team buys which

Stay on Bookings if you're all-Microsoft, email reminders are enough, and your scheduling is appointment-based (services, clinics, consultations) — check you're on the Teams-bearing SKU. Buy Calendly if you need round-robin lead routing, payment collection, non-Microsoft calendars, or a polished external booking page — and price the seat count honestly, since there's no volume relief below 31 seats. If neither fits perfectly, the alternatives (Cal.com's generous free tier, meetergo, Acuity for client appointments) are worth a look before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

Is Microsoft Bookings free?

There's no standalone price — it's included in eligible Microsoft 365 business plans (Business Basic with Teams and up, from ~US$7/user/month). If you already pay for M365, the marginal cost is $0.

Can Bookings do round-robin scheduling?

No — Bookings offers manual staff selection only, no automated distribution. Calendly offers automated round-robin on its Teams tier.

Does Calendly work without Microsoft 365?

Yes — Calendly is calendar-agnostic (Google, Outlook, Office 365, iCloud) and works regardless of which provider each person uses. Note that video meetings need a separate Zoom or Google Meet subscription, since Calendly hosts no video itself.
