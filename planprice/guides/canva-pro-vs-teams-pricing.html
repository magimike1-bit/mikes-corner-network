Canva Pro vs Teams Pricing: The Per-Person Math and the 3-Person Minimum

Published 2026-10-11

Every Canva pricing roundup quotes "$10 per person per month" for Teams and moves on. That number is technically correct and practically misleading, because Teams has a 3-person minimum: the cheapest Teams bill is not $10/month, it's $30/month (or $300/year), no matter how many people actually design. This guide does the per-person math properly — and flags the 2026 plan restructure that may have changed the tiers entirely.

Pro is the solo plan: one person, 100GB storage, background remover, brand kit, premium content library. Teams includes everything in Pro plus shared brand kits, team templates, and admin controls. Enterprise is custom-quoted, with one 2026 source reporting a 150-seat minimum that pushes mid-size teams toward the team tier instead.

The crossover is exactly 3 users. Below 3, Teams charges you for phantom seats — a solo user on Teams pays $300/year for what Pro sells at $120. At 3 users the plans break even-ish (Teams wins by $60/year plus the admin features), and above 3, Teams is cheaper per person every time. In CAD at the 2026-10-11 mid-market rate (1 USD = 1.42616 CAD), the Teams floor is about $428/year before anyone designs anything.

Solo and 2-person teams: when Teams wastes money

If you're a freelancer with an occasional collaborator, the instinct to "get Teams so we can share" costs real money. Two Pro annual plans ($240/year combined) beat one 2-user Teams subscription ($300/year) by $60 — and each person keeps their own brand kit and storage. Teams only earns its premium for small groups when you specifically need shared brand governance, approval workflows, or centralized admin — features Pro doesn't offer at any price.

One exception worth knowing: verified nonprofits and educators get Teams features free for up to 50 users, per a 2026 small-business guide. If you qualify, the entire math above is moot — apply first.

The 2026 restructure you should know about

A 2026 pricing breakdown (updated August 2026) reports that Canva retired the flat-rate Teams plan and rebranded it as Canva Business, moving to per-seat pricing of roughly $20–$25/user/month — a meaningful jump from the old $10/person/month. Every other 2026 source still prices legacy Teams tiers, so the two can't be reconciled from public information alone.

What to do with this: check canva.com/pricing before you budget. If the Business rebrand has rolled out to your region, the 3-person-minimum math in this guide understates the cost — $20–$25/seat/month is $240–$300 per person per year, roughly double the legacy Teams figure. The decision logic (crossover at the seat minimum, phantom-seat waste below it) still holds; only the numbers move. Prices in this guide were checked 2026-10-11 against published guides, not against a live Canva checkout.

FAQ

Is Canva Teams worth it for a 2-person team?

Usually not. The 3-person minimum means you pay $300/year (billed annually) for 2 users — 50% more per active user. Two separate Pro annual plans ($240/year combined) are cheaper unless you need Teams-only admin features.

Did Canva Teams change in 2026?

One 2026 pricing guide (August 2026) reports Teams was rebranded to Canva Business with per-seat pricing around $20–$25/user/month. This is reported by a single source and not confirmed on Canva's site — verify the current plan names on canva.com before buying.

How much does Canva Pro cost per month in 2026?
