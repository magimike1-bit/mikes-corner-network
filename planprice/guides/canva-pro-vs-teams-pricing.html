Canva Pro vs Business Pricing: The Per-Person Math After Teams

Published 2026-10-11, pricing re-checked 2026-11-13

Every older Canva pricing roundup quotes "US$10 per person per month" for Teams and moves on. That plan no longer exists for new buyers: Canva has discontinued Teams for new sign-ups and replaced it with Canva Business, at US$25 per person per month. This guide does the per-person math properly on the plans Canva actually sells today, and shows why the old 3-person crossover no longer applies to anyone buying now.

The published prices, dated 2026-11-13

Prices below are Canva's published US list prices. Canva prices regionally, so your checkout may differ: Canva's Canadian pricing page, checked the same day, lists Pro at CA$142 a year and Business at CA$260 per person a year. Figures like "US$12.99 Pro / US$14.99 Teams" still circulate on older guides; they predate the late-2024 price increase and the Teams shutdown, and should be treated as stale.

The per-person math: the crossover is gone

Here is the math no roundup does. Under the old Teams plan, per-person pricing fell as you added people, and the comparison crossed over at 3 users. Business has no seat minimum and no volume break: it costs US$250 per person per year at every team size, against US$144 per person for separate Pro plans. Your real annual bill is:

Business costs US$106 more per person per year than Pro at 1 person and at 5, because the gap is a flat per-person premium for the collaboration and admin layer, not a bulk rate. The good news buried in the change: with no seat minimum, nobody pays for phantom seats anymore, and a solo user who wants Business features pays for exactly one seat. In Canadian dollars, on Canva's own Canadian pricing page, the same comparison is CA$260 per person a year for Business against CA$142 for Pro.

Solo and 2-person teams: when Business wastes money

If you're a freelancer with an occasional collaborator, the instinct to "get the team plan so we can share" costs real money. Two Pro annual plans (US$288/year combined) beat Business for two people (US$500/year) by US$212, and each person keeps their own brand kits and storage. Business only earns its premium for small groups when you specifically need shared brand governance, approval workflows, or centralized admin, features Pro doesn't offer at any price.

One exception worth knowing: Canva's pricing page says K-12 educational organizations and registered nonprofits get most premium features free. If you qualify, the entire math above is moot, apply first.

What happened to Canva Teams

Canva's own pricing page now answers this in its FAQ: "Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups or upgrades. If you're already on Canva Teams, you'll remain on your current plan unless you switch or cancel." New paid features exclusive to Business are not added to Teams, so a grandfathered Teams plan keeps its old price and slowly falls behind on features.

What to do with this: if you are on Teams today, the old 3-person-minimum math in older guides (including the first version of this one) still describes your bill, and nothing forces you off it. If you are buying new, budget on Business pricing, and check canva.com/pricing before you commit, because Canva prices regionally and the figure at your checkout is the one that counts. FAQ

Is Canva Business worth it for a 2-person team?

On price alone, no. Business costs US$250 per person a year, so 2 people pay US$500 a year, against US$288 for two separate Pro plans at US$144 each. Business earns the extra US$212 only if you need what Pro does not offer: 100 brand kits, approval workflows, and team admin controls.

Did Canva Teams change in 2026?

Yes. Canva's own pricing page (checked 2026-11-13) says Canva Teams is no longer available for new sign-ups or upgrades. Existing Teams subscribers stay on their current plan unless they switch or cancel. The plan Canva now sells to teams is Canva Business.

How much does Canva Pro cost per month in 2026?

Canva Pro is US$18 a month billed monthly, or US$144 a year billed annually (US$12 a month effective). Prices checked 2026-11-13 against Canva's published US pricing.
