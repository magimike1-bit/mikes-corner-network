ClickUp Pricing Decoded: The $7 Plan, the $19 Month, and the AI Add-On

Published 2026-10-15 · Last updated 2026-10-15

ClickUp advertises some of the lowest per-seat prices in project management, and the list rates are genuinely low. The bill a team actually receives, though, is shaped by two multipliers the headline hides: billing cadence, which moves the price by roughly a third, and AI, which is sold as a separate per-user add-on that can cost more than the underlying plan. Add a workspace-wide upgrade rule — a paid plan applies to every member, not just the team that asked for it — and the real decision is quite different from picking between US$7 and US$12. This guide works the full arithmetic for Canadian teams, checked on 2026-10-15, in US dollars as ClickUp publishes them.

The four tiers and the two billing cadences

Free Forever costs US$0, with unlimited tasks and unlimited free-plan members, and a small storage allowance. Unlimited costs US$7 per user per month billed yearly, or US$10 billed monthly, and adds unlimited storage, integrations, dashboards, Gantt charts and custom fields. Business costs US$12 per user per month billed yearly, or US$19 billed monthly, and adds advanced dashboards, a much larger automation allowance, Google SSO and unlimited proofing. Enterprise is quote-only, adding SAML SSO, SCIM, audit logging and the largest automation allowance. The annual discount is material — about 30% on Unlimited and closer to 37% on Business measured against the posted monthly rates — so a team comparing ClickUp monthly against a competitor's annual rate is comparing two different products. Always normalise every quote in a comparison to the same cadence before ranking anything.

The free tier deserves a precise reading, because it is the reason many teams start here. Unlimited tasks and members at no cost is a real offer, and for a small team with simple boards it can hold for a long time. Its limits are in the allowances around the work — storage and automation runs in particular — rather than in the task list itself. Teams usually upgrade not because they run out of tasks but because they run out of room to automate and store, which is a healthier upgrade trigger than a feature wall, provided someone is watching the allowances before they bind mid-project.

The AI add-on: a second per-user price list

ClickUp sells AI separately from the plan. Brain AI costs US$9 per user per month and Everything AI costs US$28 per user per month, each with its own AI credit allowance, and the charge applies across paid members rather than only to the people who use the AI features. Stacked on a seat, the arithmetic changes character: Business at US$12 plus Everything AI at US$28 is US$40 per user per month before any credit top-ups — more than triple the headline Business rate. Even the modest stack, Unlimited at US$7 plus Brain AI at US$9, is US$16 a seat, which is no longer the cheapest seat in most comparisons. None of this makes the AI add-ons bad value; summarisation, drafting and agent features may well earn their price. It does mean the honest comparison is stack against stack: a competitor that bundles AI into its mid tier should be compared with ClickUp's plan-plus-AI total, never with ClickUp's base rate alone. Budget the AI decision as its own line, with its own yes or no, at the same meeting that approves the plan.

The workspace rule: upgrades apply to everybody

Two billing rules change the arithmetic for multi-team organisations. First, upgrading a ClickUp Workspace upgrades all members in that Workspace — a paid plan cannot be scoped to the one team that needs it while the rest stay free. Second, payment is per Workspace, so an organisation running several Workspaces pays for each one separately. A company that casually creates a Workspace per department can therefore multiply its bill without adding a single person, and a company that puts everybody in one Workspace pays the top plan rate for people who may only ever view a board. Paid users added mid-cycle are billed at a prorated rate for the time left in the cycle, which is fair, but it also means headcount growth flows straight into the bill with no natural pause. Before upgrading, decide the Workspace structure deliberately: how many Workspaces, who is a paid member in each, and who can be a guest instead. That org-chart decision is worth more than any negotiation on the per-seat rate.

Guests are the pressure valve. Paid plans include permission-controlled guest access, with allowances tied to member count, which lets clients and occasional collaborators participate without a full seat — up to the allowance. Past it, a guest can turn into a paid seat, so the guest list needs the same quarterly audit discipline as the member list. The pattern is familiar across this category: the seat price is published, the seat definition is where the money moves.

Year-one math: base, stacked, and at scale

At annual list prices for a 20-person team, Unlimited costs 20 × US$7 × 12 = US$1,680 a year, and Business costs 20 × US$12 × 12 = US$2,880 a year. Add Brain AI across the team and those become US$3,840 and US$5,040 respectively. Add Everything AI instead and Business reaches US$9,600 a year — a number that belongs in a different budget conversation than the US$12 headline suggests. At 10 people the same stacks are US$840 (Unlimited base), US$1,440 (Business base) and US$4,800 (Business plus Everything AI). The lesson is not that ClickUp is expensive; at base rates it is among the cheapest credible options in the category. The lesson is that ClickUp has two prices — the platform price and the platform-plus-AI price — and a team should know which one it is actually signing up for, because the gap between them is larger than the gap between ClickUp and most of its competitors.

The decision framework

This is a buying-decision guide, not a setup procedure — the framework below is the process.

  1. Normalise the cadence first. Compare annual with annual; the monthly rates run roughly a third higher.
  2. Decide AI as a separate purchase. Price plan-only and plan-plus-AI stacks side by side, and approve each on its own merits.
  3. Fix the Workspace structure before upgrading. Upgrades cover every member of a Workspace and each Workspace bills separately.
  4. Audit members and guests quarterly. Prorated mid-cycle additions and guest allowances mean the bill drifts upward by default.
  5. Watch allowances, not tasks, on Free. Storage and automation runs are the limits that trigger the first upgrade.

Frequently asked questions

How much does ClickUp cost per user?

Unlimited is US$7 per user per month billed yearly (US$10 monthly) and Business is US$12 yearly (US$19 monthly). Enterprise is quote-only. AI is extra: Brain AI at US$9 and Everything AI at US$28 per user per month.

Is ClickUp really free?

The Free Forever plan is a standing plan, not a trial, with unlimited tasks and members. Its practical limits are storage and automation allowances, which are what push growing teams to Unlimited.

Can one team in a Workspace upgrade without the others?

No. Upgrading applies to all members of the Workspace, and each Workspace is billed separately — so Workspace structure should be decided before the first upgrade, not after.
