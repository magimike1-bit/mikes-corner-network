Dashlane Business Pricing: Password Management vs Omnix

Published 2026-10-12

Dashlane rebranded its business line to Omnix in 2026 — and left two generations of pricing on the internet. Depending on which page you land on, Dashlane business costs US$5, US$6, US$8, or US$11 per user per month, and the official business page now leads with "contact sales" instead of a number. This guide reconciles the generations, explains what Omnix actually adds, and compares the real cost against 1Password and Keeper.

General information, not financial advice. Prices change — always confirm the current rates on the vendor's own pricing page before buying.

Password Management vs Omnix: the two tiers

Dashlane's current business lineup has two tiers, billed annually, per 2026 pricing guides. Password Management at about US$8/user/month: unlimited passwords and passkeys, unlimited devices, group sharing, dark-web monitoring, SSO, SCIM and SIEM integrations, admin console, and policy controls. Omnix at about US$11/user/month: everything in Password Management plus Credential Protection — proactive risk detection, AI phishing alerts, and in-context nudges to employees before they click something dangerous.

Omnix is the repositioning: from "a vault with monitoring" to "a credential security platform." For organizations where targeted phishing against employee credentials is a material risk — financial services, healthcare — the browser-layer phishing interception is the differentiator. For everyone else, Password Management is the complete product and Omnix is a US$3/user/month upsell.

The pricing confusion: old plans vs new plans

Here is why the numbers disagree across the web. The older generation — Team at US$5 and Business at US$8 — still appears on aggregator pages that haven't updated. The current generation — Password Management at US$8 and Omnix at US$11 — is what pricing guides updated in 2026 report, though some pages show Password Management at US$6, suggesting regional or promotional variance. And the official Dashlane business page publishes no per-seat amounts at all for Omnix, leading with contact-sales instead.

Practical advice: treat every third-party number as indicative and get the quote in writing. When the vendor won't publish a price, the price is negotiable — and the number you negotiate from should be the current US$8/US$11 generation, not the stale US$5/US$6 figures.

What reviewers say

Editorial reviews put Dashlane at 4.7 out of 5 against 1Password's 4.6 in a September 2026 head-to-head, with Dashlane winning on advanced features and support and 1Password on usability. Reviewers consistently highlight the same trade: Dashlane's admin console and phishing protection are best-in-class, while 1Password is easier to roll out and better for developer workflows. The free Premium Family account bundled with every business seat — about US$96 a year in value per employee — is the perk reviewers mention most, and it meaningfully softens the per-seat premium.

Note the personal-plan context: Dashlane ended its free plan in 2025. Personal Premium is US$2.50 a month billed annually, Friends & Family US$3.75 — annual billing only, no monthly option. Employees comparing the business tool against what they use at home should know the personal side is paid-only now.

The decision framework

This is a buying-decision guide, not a setup procedure — the decision framework near the end is the whole process.

  1. Negotiate from US$8/US$11 — the current generation — and get the quote in writing, since the official page won't publish one.
  2. Buy Omnix only for the phishing story. If credential-targeted phishing isn't a top-three risk, Password Management is the complete product.
  3. Price the family-plan perk. The bundled Premium Family account per seat offsets the premium versus Keeper and Bitwarden.
  4. Trial with five or six people across teams for the 14-day trial — low trial adoption predicts low post-purchase adoption.
  5. Past ~25 staff or with Okta/Entra ID, require SCIM in the quote — joiner/leaver automation is where the admin cost actually lives.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Dashlane business cost?

About US$8 per user per month for Password Management and US$11 for Omnix, billed annually, per 2026 pricing guides. The official business page is quote-based, so confirm your number with sales.

What is Dashlane Omnix?

The 2026 rebrand of Dashlane's business line: Password Management plus a Credential Protection layer with AI phishing alerts and proactive risk detection. It positions Dashlane as a credential security platform rather than just a vault.

Is there a free Dashlane plan?

No — Dashlane ended its free plan in 2025. There is a 14-day free trial on business plans.
