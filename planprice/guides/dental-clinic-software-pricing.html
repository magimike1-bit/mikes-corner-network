Dental Practice Management Software Pricing: The Published Prices & the Quote Wall

Published 2026-10-12

Dental practice management is the least transparent software category we cover: of the eight major vendors, only three publish a price. This guide states the three published price lists as fact, names the five behind the quote wall, and budgets the full clinic stack — hosting, imaging, migration — that comparison tables skip. Prices checked September–October 2026; all USD as listed.

General information, not financial advice. Prices change — always confirm current rates on the vendor's own pricing page before buying.

Three publish, five don't

The published three, as of September 18, 2026: Open Dental at US$199/month per office (falling to US$149); tab32 at US$125/month in year one, stepping up to US$225 after, for three to five providers; CareStack with two published plans, Essentials from US$829/month and Intelligence from US$1,299/month, priced on locations, chairs, and providers. The quote-only five: Curve Dental, Dentrix Ascend, Denticon, and Sensei Cloud publish no price at all — nobody outside a sales call can tell you whether Curve is cheaper than Dentrix Ascend. Treat any guide that ranks them on price as fiction.

The quote-wall checklist

For the five quote-only vendors, get the same seven answers in writing before you compare: the all-in monthly figure at your provider count for years one and three; implementation and migration fees; training costs; the imaging integration (Dexis bridges or equivalents) and its cost; support hours and SLAs; the contract term and exit; and what happens to your patient data if you leave. The published vendors already answer most of these on their pricing pages — that's the transparency premium you're buying.

The full clinic budget (beyond the subscription)

The subscription is the headline, not the budget. Open Dental's US$199/month is server software: add third-party hosting (~US$100/month), your own backups and IT upkeep, and the imaging bridges. Dentrix's reported setup costs run as high as ~US$8,000. For a 3-provider clinic, year one looks roughly like: Open Dental ~US$3,588 (subscription + hosting), tab32 US$1,500, CareStack US$9,948 plus implementation, Dentrix Ascend US$4,800–8,400 on user-reported figures. The cheapest subscription can be the most expensive stack — budget the whole thing.

Year-two math

tab32's step-up is the clearest example of why year-one pricing misleads: US$125/month becomes US$225 — an 80% increase in year two. Before you sign, compute the two-year total for every vendor, not the first-year teaser. Ask each quote-only vendor for the year-three figure too; the published-price vendors have already shown you theirs.

The decision framework: if you want published pricing, flat costs, and on-premise control, Open Dental is the only independent-clinic option with all three. If you want cloud with published pricing, it's tab32 vs CareStack at very different price points. If you're evaluating Dentrix Ascend or Curve, you're buying on demo quality — so make the demo answer the seven checklist questions, and don't let a polished interface substitute for a number.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Open Dental cost?

Open Dental publishes US$199/month per office, falling to US$149. It's self-hosted server software, so budget hosting and IT separately. No setup fee.

Is Curve Dental cheaper than Dentrix Ascend?

Neither publishes a price, so nobody outside a sales call can say. Ask both for the all-in monthly figure at your provider count for years one and three, in writing.

What does Dentrix cost?

Dentrix pricing is not publicly disclosed. Practices have reported roughly US$400–700+/month depending on features and provider count, with setup costs reported up to ~US$8,000 — these are third-party reports, not quoted prices. Get an itemized quote.
