Ecommerce Brand Software Stack Costs Beyond Shopify

Published 2026-10-12

The platform fee is the cheapest line on the bill — the apps cost multiples of it. This guide prices the real ecommerce stack at revenue bands, using 2026 data: a representative $1–5M brand runs about US$2,000/month in apps, and half the stack bills on tickets, resolutions, and messages rather than flat fees. The revenue bands (2026 data)

A 2026 analysis of Shopify app spend by revenue band puts the numbers at: under US$1M revenue ~US$175/month in apps; US$1–5M US$1,000–3,500/month (midpoint ~US$2,000); above US$20M, US$50,000+/month. The stack doesn't grow linearly — it jumps when ticket volumes, contact lists, and message counts cross tier thresholds. Price your stack at the band you're growing into, not the one you're in.

On reviews, Gorgias holds 4.6/5 across 582 G2 reviews (2026): praise for the Shopify integration, ease of use, and automation; the top complaints are pricing as volume grows and billing clarity on what counts as a billable ticket — which leads directly to the next section.

Gorgias AI double-billing: the bill-shock mechanic

Gorgias charges US$1.00 per AI-resolved conversation on monthly billing (US$0.90 annual) — on top of the ticket plan, not instead of it. Five hundred AI resolutions in a month is US$500 extra, and overages run US$150 per 100 AI interactions. Annual billing saves ~16% on the base plan but the AI meter runs regardless. Before you enable the AI agent, model your resolution volume: the feature that deflects tickets can cost more than the tickets it deflects.

The contract trap

Recharge Plus (US$499/month) and Custom plans carry 12-month commitments — you're locked for a year at the tier you signed, not the tier your volume justifies six months later. Klaviyo's annual discount works the same direction. The stack guides that total monthly prices without flagging which lines are annual commitments understate the exit cost: switching Recharge mid-year means paying out the contract or negotiating your way out. Price the commitment, not just the month.

The dead-weight app audit

A 2026 cost guide puts the "app tax" for growing stores at US$50–500/month across categories: email US$20–150, reviews US$0–50, upsells US$30–100, support US$50–300. Most brands accumulate apps they stopped using two redesigns ago — each still billing. The audit is simple: list every app, its monthly cost, and its last 30 days of attributable revenue or tickets handled. Anything under a 3× return gets cancelled. Run it quarterly; the stack regrows otherwise.

Frequently asked questions

How much do Shopify apps cost per month?

2026 band data: ~US$175/month under US$1M revenue, US$1,000–3,500/month at US$1–5M (midpoint ~US$2,000), US$50,000+/month above US$20M. A representative $1–5M stack (Klaviyo, Gorgias, Recharge, Postscript, Yotpo, ShipStation) totals ~US$1,270–2,520/month.

How does Gorgias pricing work?

Ticket tiers: US$10/month (50 tickets), US$60 (300), US$360 (2,000), US$900 (5,000); annual saves ~16%. AI resolutions bill separately at US$1.00 each monthly (US$0.90 annual) — 500 AI resolutions adds US$500 on top of the ticket plan.

Is Recharge worth it, and what does it cost?

Representative cost ~US$300/month plus a percentage of GMV; Recharge Plus is US$499/month on a 12-month commitment. The commitment is the real price — switching mid-year means paying out the contract.
