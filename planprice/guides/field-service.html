How to Choose Field Service Software

Last updated 2026-10-01

Field-service businesses live or die on the quote-to-cash loop: quote the job, schedule the crew, do the work, invoice before you leave the driveway. Software earns its keep when it tightens that loop instead of adding paperwork to it.

Jobber is built for exactly this — plumbers, cleaners, landscapers, and contractors managing quotes, scheduling, dispatch, invoicing, and client communication in one place. It is a Canadian company with CAD pricing. This guide covers how to evaluate it and what to demand from any field-service tool.

Map your quote-to-cash loop

Write down every step between the customer calling and the money landing in your account. Then check which steps the software actually handles. The expensive gaps are the manual ones: retyping quotes into invoices, texting schedule changes, chasing late payments.

Client communication is part of the loop, not a bonus. Automated reminders, on-my-way texts, and follow-up review requests directly affect no-shows and repeat business. If the tool does not handle communication, you will bolt on a second tool — price that.

Schedule for reality, not the demo

Demos show clean calendars. Your reality is emergency calls, jobs that run long, and techs who call in sick. Look for drag-and-drop rescheduling, crew views, and conflict warnings — and trial it with a real chaotic week, not a tidy one.

Dispatch matters as much as scheduling. Who sees what, on which device, with what offline behavior? A dispatcher in the office and a tech in a basement with no signal need different things from the same system.

Check the mobile experience first

Your crew will live in the mobile app, so evaluate it before the desktop dashboard. Can a tech see the day's jobs, navigate to them, log time and materials, and invoice from the driveway? Every back-at-the-office step is a step that will not happen.

Photos are the unsung feature: before-and-after shots protect against disputes and justify the invoice. Confirm the app handles photo capture and attaches them to the job record without friction.

Mind the money details

As a Canadian company with CAD pricing, Jobber removes the exchange-rate guessing for Canadian shops — worth confirming with any vendor you evaluate. Beyond the sticker, ask about per-user costs as you add techs, and what the invoicing and payment-processing story looks like.

Annual versus monthly billing usually trades a discount for commitment. Only take the annual deal after a full busy season on the monthly plan — field-service demand is seasonal, and you want to know the tool survives your worst month before you prepay.

Quick checklist

  • Map your quote-to-cash loop and mark every manual step the software must absorb.
  • Trial scheduling with a real chaotic week, including an emergency call and a sick tech.
  • Test the mobile app from the field: jobs, navigation, time logging, invoicing, photos.
  • Confirm client communication features: reminders, on-my-way texts, review requests.
  • Price per tech as the crew grows, and check CAD billing if you operate in Canada.
  • Run one full busy season on monthly billing before considering an annual plan.
