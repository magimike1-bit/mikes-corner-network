FreshBooks Plans for Freelancers: Client Limits and the Real Cost per Client

Published 2026-10-11

FreshBooks prices by billable client, not by user — and the entry tier caps you at five. For a freelancer, the plan decision isn't about features first; it's about how many clients you serve and what each one costs you per month. Here's the Canadian pricing in CAD, the per-client math, and the two triggers that actually force the upgrade.

The four tiers in CAD, and the client caps

Official Canadian prices, verified October 2026: Lite $26/month — invoicing for up to 5 billable clients, expense tracking, estimates, card/debit/Apple Pay/Google Pay/pre-authorized debit acceptance, and tax-time reports. Plus $42/month — up to 50 billable clients, adding proposals and retainers, double-entry accounting reports, receipt scanning, and accountant access. Premium $72/month — unlimited clients, adding accounts payable (bills and vendors), project profitability tracking, and email template customization. Select is custom-quoted for larger operations. Yearly billing saves 10% on all tiers, and a 30-day money-back guarantee covers new signups.

Two structural facts shape everything: every plan includes just one user seat (extra team members are $13/month each in Canada), and the client caps are hard — a sixth billable client makes Lite unusable at any price.

Cost per billable client at 5, 20, and 50 clients

The per-client math, at regular CAD rates:

  • 5 clients on Lite ($26): $5.20 per client/month — $312/year
  • 6 clients — forced to Plus ($42): $7.00 per client/month — the sixth client costs you $16/month in plan jump
  • 20 clients on Plus ($42): $2.10 per client/month — $504/year
  • 50 clients on Plus ($42): $0.84 per client/month — the sweet spot of the tier
  • 51 clients — forced to Premium ($72): $1.41 per client/month — the 51st client costs $30/month in plan jump

The curve is the whole story: FreshBooks gets dramatically cheaper per client as you fill a tier, then punishes you at the boundary. A freelancer growing from 5 to 6 clients sees the per-client cost jump 35% overnight — the single most common upgrade trigger, and it has nothing to do with features.

The two real upgrade triggers

Trigger 1: the client cap. Lite's 5-client ceiling is the sharpest constraint in freelancer accounting software — most working freelancers hit it within months. The move to Plus ($42) is forced by headcount, not ambition. Trigger 2: the accountant and the payables. Lite has no accountant access and no double-entry accounting reports; if your accountant needs to log in, Plus is the minimum. Premium's trigger is narrower: accounts payable and project profitability. Unless you manage supplier bills or need job-costing, the unlimited client count alone rarely justifies the $30/month step from Plus to Premium.

The add-ons that change the math

Three add-ons can move the total: extra team members at $13/month each (a two-person shop on Plus pays $55, not $42), Advanced Payments at $25/month, and payroll through FreshBooks' PaymentEvolution partnership (quoted separately). A solo freelancer on Plus with Advanced Payments pays $67/month — within striking distance of Premium. Price the add-ons before the tier: sometimes the "cheap" plan with add-ons costs more than the next tier up.

Which plan for which freelancer

Just starting, ≤5 clients → Lite ($26). Watch the client count like a hawk — the sixth client is a $16/month event. Working freelancer, 6–50 clients → Plus ($42). The sweet spot: accountant access, proper reports, and room to grow. Agency or supplier-heavy, 50+ clients → Premium ($72). Buy it for accounts payable and project profitability, not the unlimited clients. And remember the promo math: 90%-off-for-3-months offers are real but temporary — budget from the regular rate, because that's what year two costs.

A note on format: this is a pricing comparison, not a how-to — there are no step-by-step actions because the question it answers is which plan fits your business, and that is what the tables above are for.

FAQ

How many clients does FreshBooks Lite allow?

Five billable clients. A freelancer with a sixth client cannot use the Lite plan at any price — the cap is hard, and the only move is Plus at $42 CAD/month for up to 50 clients.

What is FreshBooks pricing in Canada?

In CAD: Lite $26/month (5 clients), Plus $42/month (50 clients), Premium $72/month (unlimited clients), Select by custom quote. Yearly billing saves 10%. Prices verified October 2026.

When should a freelancer upgrade from FreshBooks Plus to Premium?

When you need accounts payable (bills and vendors), project profitability tracking, or email template customization — those are Premium-only. The unlimited client count alone rarely justifies the $30/month step from Plus.
