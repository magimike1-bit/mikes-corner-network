Grammarly Business Pricing: What Teams Actually Pay Now

Published 2026-10-11

If you search "Grammarly Business pricing" you'll find articles quoting $15 per seat per month — for a plan that no longer exists. Grammarly restructured its lineup: the standalone Business plan is gone, and team pricing now lives on the Pro plan at $12 per member per month (billed annually). This guide gives the current price, the old-vs-new math for real team sizes, and exactly where the free tier ends — all verified against Grammarly's live plans page on October 11, 2026.

Grammarly Business is gone — here's what replaced it

Grammarly's plans page now sells three tiers: Free ($0), Pro ($12 USD per member per month), and Enterprise (custom pricing). The company's own FAQ states it plainly: the Pro plan includes the advanced writing features of the old Premium tier plus the team features that were previously available only in the Business plan — style guides, brand tones, and team analytics. If you were budgeting for "Business," budget for Pro instead.

The current team price: Pro at $12/member/month

Pro costs $12 per member per month billed annually ($144 per member per year), or $30 per member per month on monthly billing — a 2.5× penalty for not committing. What the $12 buys beyond Free: full-sentence rewrites, tone adjustment, plagiarism and AI-generated-text detection, 2,000 AI prompts per member per month, one style guide, one brand tone, two user groups, and a team analytics dashboard.

Enterprise, at custom pricing, is where the larger-organization controls live: unlimited AI prompts, unlimited style guides and brand tones, SAML single sign-on, SCIM provisioning, data loss prevention, BYOK encryption, and dedicated support. If your IT team is asking about SSO and DLP, you're an Enterprise conversation, not a Pro one.

Old Business vs new Pro: the team-size math

The legacy Business plan charged $15 per seat per month with volume discounts: $14.50 at 10–49 seats and $12.50 at 50–149 seats. Here's what changed:

  • 5-person team: Pro annual = 5 × $12 × 12 = $720/year (~$1,030 CAD). Legacy Business would have been 5 × $15 × 12 = $900/year. Savings: $180/year.
  • 10-person team: Pro annual = $1,440/year (~$2,059 CAD). Legacy Business at the $14.50 tier = $1,740/year. Savings: $300/year.
  • 10 people, monthly billing: 10 × $30 × 12 = $3,600/year — two and a half times the annual-billing price for identical seats.

The restructure was a genuine price cut for small teams on annual billing. The trap is monthly billing: at $30 per member, a team that "tries it for a few months" pays two and a half times the annual per-member rate for the same seats.

The free-vs-paid gate, feature by feature

Free is a capable proofreader: basic grammar and spelling checks, tone detection, and 100 AI prompts a month. The paywall sits exactly where professional writing starts: full-sentence rewrites, tone adjustment, plagiarism detection, AI-text detection, and the jump from 100 to 2,000 AI prompts a month. Team features — style guides, brand tones, and analytics — don't exist on Free at all. Rule of thumb: if one person needs rewrites or plagiarism checks, buy that person Pro; if the team needs consistent voice, that's the Pro team-features gate, and it's worth pricing per seat honestly rather than sharing logins.

When Enterprise (custom) is the real answer

Pro covers teams that need better writing. Enterprise covers organizations that need governed writing: SSO, SCIM, DLP, and BYOK encryption. The tell is procurement: if your security review asks for any of those acronyms, Pro can't pass it and the custom quote is the only path. For everyone else, Pro is the whole product.

FAQ

A note on structure: this is a price-comparison explainer, not a repair procedure — there are no settings to change, only the per-seat math to run before you buy.

Is there still a Grammarly Business plan?

No. Grammarly now sells Free, Pro ($12/member/month annual) and Enterprise (custom). Pro absorbed the old Business team features — style guides, brand tones, team analytics — per Grammarly's own FAQ.

How much does Grammarly cost for a team of 10?

$1,440/year on annual billing (~$2,059 CAD at the October 2026 rate), or $3,600/year on monthly billing. Annual billing is less than half the monthly-billing price.

What does Grammarly Free leave out?

Full-sentence rewrites, tone adjustment, plagiarism and AI-text detection, the jump from 100 to 2,000 AI prompts a month, and all team features (style guides, brand tones, analytics).
