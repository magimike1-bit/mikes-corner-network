Hootsuite vs Buffer Pricing: The Per-Account Math

Published 2026-10-11

These two tools don't charge for the same thing at all, which is why "which is cheaper" has no single answer. Buffer bills per connected social channel; Hootsuite bills per user seat. That one structural difference decides the winner for your team before features enter the picture. This guide lays out the 2026 prices, works the crossover math for real team sizes, and converts it all to CAD.

How each tool charges: per channel vs per seat

On Buffer, a "channel" is one connected social profile — one Instagram account, one LinkedIn page, one TikTok account. Your bill is channels × the per-channel rate, no matter how many people log in (on the Team plan, users are unlimited). On Hootsuite, your bill is users × the per-seat rate, no matter how many accounts are connected (Standard includes up to 10 accounts; Professional and Advanced include unlimited). Channels grow slowly; headcount grows fast. That asymmetry is the whole comparison.

The published prices, October 2026

Buffer (checked September 2026 on buffer.com/pricing): Free — $0, 3 channels, 10 queued posts per channel, 1 user. Essentials — $6 per channel per month on monthly billing ($5 on annual), 1 user, unlimited scheduling. Team — $12 per channel per month on monthly billing ($10 on annual), unlimited users and approvals.

Hootsuite (re-verified against Hootsuite's plans page August 2026): Standard — $99 per user per month, up to 10 social accounts. Professional — $199 per user per month, unlimited accounts. Advanced — $399 per user per month, unlimited accounts plus approvals and routing. Enterprise — custom. All figures below are annual-billing rates. Note the 2026 restructure: the old flat $249 Team plan for 3 users is gone — Hootsuite is now purely per-seat.

The crossover table: your team size × your channels

  • Solo creator, 3 channels: Buffer $0 (free plan) vs Hootsuite $99/month. Not close.
  • 3-person team, 8 channels (monthly billing): Buffer Team 8 × $12 = $96/month vs Hootsuite Standard 3 × $99 = $297/month.
  • 3-person team, 8 channels (annual billing): Buffer Team 8 × $10 = $80/month vs Hootsuite $297/month.
  • 5-person team, 4 channels (annual): Buffer Team 4 × $10 = $40/month vs Hootsuite 5 × $99 = $495/month — more than 12× the price.

In CAD at the October 2026 rate (~1.43), that 3-person/8-channel scenario is roughly $114 CAD/month on Buffer versus $425 CAD/month on Hootsuite — a $311 CAD monthly gap, or about $3,700 CAD a year, for the same eight accounts.

The one-line decision rule

If your headcount exceeds your channel count, Buffer wins on price — and headcount almost always exceeds channel count. Hootsuite only starts to make sense when your channel count dwarfs your headcount — for example, an agency managing 30 accounts with 2 staff pays Buffer Team 30 × $12 = $360/month versus Hootsuite Standard 2 × $99 = $198/month. A 10-person team that actually runs approvals through Hootsuite Advanced may find $3,990/month justifiable; a 10-person team that just schedules posts is burning money versus Buffer Team.

The free-tier gap Hootsuite can't close

Buffer's free plan — 3 channels, 10 queued posts each — is a genuine $0 baseline for a solo creator or a tiny business. Hootsuite lists no free tier in its 2026 pricing, so its floor is $99/user/month from day one. If you're scheduling for three or fewer accounts, the price comparison isn't Buffer vs Hootsuite — it's Buffer vs $1,188 a year.

A note on structure: this is a price-comparison explainer, not a repair procedure — there are no settings to change, only the per-seat vs per-channel math to run before you buy.

FAQ

Is Buffer cheaper than Hootsuite?

In nearly every realistic scenario, yes — typically 3–12× cheaper. A 3-person team on 8 channels pays ~$80/month on Buffer Team (annual) vs $297/month on Hootsuite Standard, because Buffer charges per channel while Hootsuite charges per user.

How does Hootsuite charge in 2026?

Per user seat: Standard $99/user/month (up to 10 accounts), Professional $199/user/month (unlimited accounts), Advanced $399/user/month, Enterprise custom — all annual-billing rates.

Does Hootsuite have a free plan?

No — Hootsuite lists no free tier in its 2026 pricing. Buffer's free plan (3 channels, 10 queued posts each) is permanent.
