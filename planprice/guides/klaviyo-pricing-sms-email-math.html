Klaviyo's Email + SMS Pricing: The Two-Meter Problem, Worked in Canadian Terms

Published 2026-10-11

Klaviyo's pricing looks simple until you turn on SMS. Then you discover you're being billed on two completely independent meters: one for how many profiles you can email, another for how many text messages you send. Each meter has its own tiers, its own gotchas, and — if you sell in Canada — its own exchange rate on the cost of a text. This guide combines both meters into the single monthly bill you'll actually pay.

The two meters

Meter one: active profiles. Anyone you can email counts — subscribers, past buyers, SMS-only contacts. The Email plan starts at $20/month for up to 500 active profiles and climbs with list size (roughly $150/month at 10,000 profiles). The moment you cross into the next profile band, you pay the higher rate immediately — even mid-cycle. And unengaged profiles keep billing until you actively suppress them; there is no automatic cleanup.

Meter two: SMS messages. SMS can't be bought without a paid plan — the free plan includes $5 of mobile messages a month but no top-ups. You pay per message (or per credit pack), priced by destination country, and unused credits don't roll over. The two meters never interact: a quiet email month doesn't discount a heavy SMS month.

What each meter costs in 2026

Email first, verified October 2026 (USD): $20/month covers up to 500 active profiles with 5,000 monthly email sends; around 1,000 profiles runs about $30/month; around 10,000 profiles roughly $150/month. Combined Email + SMS bundles exist at higher volumes — for example, 5,000 SMS credits with 30,000 emails at $115/month, or 20,000 credits with 105,000 emails at $355/month.

SMS per-message costs, verified from Klaviyo's own pricing in August 2026: about $15 per 1,000 sends in the US, $18 per 1,000 in Canada, and $29 per 1,000 in Australia. Under the credit model, a US text costs 1 credit ($0.009) while a Canadian text costs 3 credits ($0.028). An MMS costs roughly three times an SMS. Credit packs scale down in unit cost: 1,250 US credits at $15, 5,000 at $45, 10,000 at $90, 25,000 at $225.

The combined bill: email + SMS at the entry tier

Here's what the two meters add up to on the $20/month Email plan (US SMS rates, USD):

  • Email only: $20/month
  • + 5,000 SMS/month: $70/month
  • + 10,000 SMS/month: $125/month
  • + 20,000 SMS/month: $240/month

Notice the shape of it: at 10,000 texts a month, SMS costs more than six times the email plan it rides on. That's the two-meter problem in one table — the meter you thought of as an add-on becomes the bill.

Worked example: a Canadian store's real month

Take a Canadian Shopify store with 5,000 active profiles sending 5,000 SMS a month to Canadian numbers. Email at that list size runs roughly $100–125/month. SMS: 5,000 Canadian sends at ~$18 per 1,000 = $90/month. Combined: about $190–215/month, or roughly $2,300–2,600/year — with SMS nearly doubling the email-only cost.

Now the trap inside the trap: those 5,000 SMS credits expire monthly whether you send them or not. A seasonal business that texts heavily in December and goes quiet in February pays for December-sized SMS capacity all year unless it resizes the commitment. And because Canadian texts cost 3 credits each versus 1 in the US, a cross-border store's SMS bill can triple without sending a single extra message — the country mix is a cost driver most buyers never model.

Keeping the bill down

Three disciplines control the Klaviyo bill. Suppress ruthlessly: anyone who hasn't engaged in a year still counts as an active profile until you suppress them — schedule a quarterly suppression pass. Watch the tier edge: crossing from one profile band to the next raises the rate immediately, so time big list imports (a contest, a trade show) with eyes open. Size SMS to reality: buy credits for the messages you'll actually send, not the campaign calendar you aspire to — unused credits evaporate monthly.

The pattern from SaaS pricing reviews holds here too: the surprise is never the headline fee. It's the second meter — the per-message charge, the per-country multiplier, the credits that don't roll over. Price both meters before you enable SMS, not after the first invoice.

FAQ

How much does Klaviyo SMS cost in Canada?

Roughly $18 USD per 1,000 SMS sends to Canadian numbers — about 20% more than the US rate of $15 per 1,000. Canada consumes 3 SMS credits per message versus 1 in the US, working out to about $0.028 per Canadian text.

Can you buy Klaviyo SMS without a paid email plan?

No. SMS requires an active paid plan — the free plan includes $5 of mobile messages but no top-ups. The entry Email plan starts at $20/month for up to 500 active profiles.

Do unused Klaviyo SMS credits roll over?

No. SMS credits do not carry over month to month — you lose what you don't use. Size the SMS commitment to your real send volume, not your aspirations.
