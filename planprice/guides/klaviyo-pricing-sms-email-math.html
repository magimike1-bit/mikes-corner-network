Klaviyo's Email + SMS Pricing: The Two-Meter Problem, Worked in Canadian Terms

Published 2026-10-11 · By Mike, Editor

Klaviyo's pricing looks simple until you turn on SMS. Then you discover you're being billed on two completely independent meters: one for how many profiles you can email, another for how many text messages you send. Each meter has its own tiers, its own gotchas, and, if you sell in Canada, its own exchange rate on the cost of a text. This guide combines both meters into the single monthly bill you'll actually pay.

The two meters

Meter one: active profiles. Anyone you can email counts, subscribers, past buyers, SMS-only contacts. The Email plan starts at $20/month for up to 500 active profiles and climbs with list size (roughly $150/month at 10,000 profiles). The moment you cross into the next profile band, you pay the higher rate immediately, even mid-cycle. And unengaged profiles keep billing until you actively suppress them; there is no automatic cleanup.

Meter two: SMS messages. SMS can't be bought without a paid plan, the free plan includes $5 of mobile messages a month but no top-ups. You pay per message, priced by destination country and number type, and the monthly messaging balance does not roll over. The two meters never interact: a quiet email month doesn't discount a heavy SMS month.

What each meter costs in 2026

Email first, verified November 2026 (USD): $20/month covers up to 500 active profiles with 5,000 monthly email sends; around 1,000 profiles runs about $30/month; around 10,000 profiles roughly $150/month. Email and mobile messaging are bought together at checkout, but the two meters are priced separately inside the plan.

SMS pricing changed on July 13, 2026: Klaviyo retired its credit system and moved mobile messaging to per-message dollar rates (Klaviyo help centre, FAQ: Mobile Messaging per-message pricing, 2026). Your rate is the dollar price per message for a given channel, destination country and, in the US and Canada, number type (toll-free or short code); carrier fees are included in the rate, and larger messaging plans carry lower per-message rates. Klaviyo no longer publishes a single rate card: the rates for your account are shown in the app under Settings, Billing, Mobile messaging. What holds across Klaviyo's published material is the ordering: sends to Canadian numbers are priced above US sends, and an MMS costs more than an SMS. Older guides quote per-1,000 figures such as $15 in the US and $18 in Canada; those belonged to the credit system and no longer apply.

The combined bill: email + SMS at the entry tier

The email meter is the predictable half: $20/month at 500 profiles, about $150/month at 10,000. The SMS meter is now a multiplication, your per-message rate times your send count, and the rate lives in your account rather than on a public rate card. That is the two-meter problem in its new shape: the meter you thought of as an add-on is priced per message, varies by where your customers are, and can pass the email bill at a few thousand sends a month. Read your rate in the app before you budget, and re-read it when Klaviyo updates rates, which it says it does as carrier costs change.

Worked example: a Canadian store's real month

Take a Canadian Shopify store with 5,000 active profiles sending 5,000 SMS a month to Canadian numbers. Email at that list size runs roughly $100 to 125/month. The SMS half is 5,000 times the store's Canadian per-message rate, the figure Klaviyo shows in the app for its number type and plan size. Every cent per message is $50/month at this volume, so a rate in the low cents adds roughly $50 to 150/month, putting the combined bill around $150 to 275/month, with SMS rivalling the email cost. The worked point survives the pricing change: model the second meter before you turn it on, using the rate in your own account, not a rate card from before July 2026.

Now the trap inside the trap: the messaging balance resets monthly whether you send or not. A seasonal business that texts heavily in December and goes quiet in February pays for December-sized messaging all year unless it resizes the plan. And because Canadian sends are priced above US sends, a cross-border store's SMS bill can jump without sending a single extra message, the country mix is a cost driver most buyers never model.

Keeping the bill down

Three disciplines control the Klaviyo bill. Suppress ruthlessly: anyone who hasn't engaged in a year still counts as an active profile until you suppress them, schedule a quarterly suppression pass. Watch the tier edge: crossing from one profile band to the next raises the rate immediately, so time big list imports (a contest, a trade show) with eyes open. Size SMS to reality: size the messaging plan to the messages you'll actually send, not the campaign calendar you aspire to, unused balance evaporates monthly.

The pattern from SaaS pricing reviews holds here too: the surprise is never the headline fee. It's the second meter, the per-message charge, the per-country rate gap, the balance that doesn't roll over. Price both meters before you enable SMS, not after the first invoice.

FAQ

How much does Klaviyo SMS cost in Canada?

Klaviyo no longer publishes a fixed Canadian SMS rate. Since July 13, 2026, mobile messages are billed per message in dollars; the rate depends on the destination country and, in Canada, on whether you send from a toll-free number or a short code, with carrier fees included. Canadian sends are priced above US sends. Your account's exact rates are shown in the Klaviyo app under Settings, Billing, Mobile messaging.

Can you buy Klaviyo SMS without a paid email plan?

No. SMS requires an active paid plan, the free plan includes $5 of mobile messages but no top-ups. The entry Email plan starts at $20/month for up to 500 active profiles.

Does unused Klaviyo mobile messaging balance roll over?

No. There are no credits anymore; the monthly mobile messaging balance resets at the end of each billing period. Size the messaging plan to your real send volume, not your aspirations.
