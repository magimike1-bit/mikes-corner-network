LastPass Alternatives: Business Password Manager Pricing Compared (2026)

Published 2026-10-11

Every LastPass-alternatives roundup quotes the per-seat price and stops there. But the per-seat price is the cheapest part of switching: the migration itself — exporting vaults, rebuilding shared folders, re-enrolling users, running two subscriptions in parallel — is where the bill actually lands. This guide prices both halves: dated business-tier prices for the main alternatives, and the migration math that decides whether switching pays.

The migration bill nobody quotes

No comparison source prices the switch itself, so here is the honest version — framed as an illustrative planning estimate (assumptions stated below), not a published figure. A password-manager migration has four cost components:

  1. Admin labor. Exporting vaults, mapping shared folders, importing, verifying, and re-enrolling users. Budget 1–2 admin hours per 10 users for a clean team, more if shared folders are a mess.
  2. Overlap. You run both subscriptions for at least one billing cycle while users cut over — one extra month of the old tool plus the first month of the new one.
  3. Retraining. Browser extension differences, autofill quirks, the admin console learning curve. Small per user, real in aggregate.
  4. Risk. A botched export/import is the nightmare scenario: duplicated entries, lost attachments, broken shared-folder permissions. The mitigation is a staged rollout (pilot group first), which costs calendar time.

For a 10-person team at a $50/hour loaded admin cost, that's roughly $150–$400 of labor plus the overlap month — call it $200–$500 all-in before you count the new subscription. That number matters because it sets the bar the subscription savings have to clear.

Never switch to a pricier password manager "to save money."

When switching is worth it (and when it isn't)

  • Worth it: you're on a tool you don't trust anymore and a cheaper alternative clears the migration cost in under a year. Bitwarden Teams at $4/user/mo vs LastPass Business at $7 is the textbook case.
  • Worth it: your team is growing and the per-seat gap compounds — a $3/user/mo difference is $360/year at 10 users and $1,800/year at 50.
  • Not worth it: you're chasing a $1/user/mo saving on a 5-person team. The $85/year saving doesn't clear a $350 migration.
  • Not worth it: your shared folders, SSO integrations, and policies are deeply embedded. Migration risk rises with complexity, not headcount — a 10-person team with heavy SSO can cost more to move than a 30-person team on a simple setup.

FAQ

What is the cheapest business password manager in 2026?

Of the mainstream business tiers, Zoho Vault Standard at $1/user/month and NordPass Business from $1.79/user/month are the cheapest published options, with Bitwarden Teams at $4/user/month the cheapest of the full-featured admin-console set. Prices checked 2026-10-11.

How much does it cost to migrate a team off LastPass?

The subscription is the small part. Budget 1–2 admin hours per 10 users for export, import, shared-folder rebuild, and re-enrollment, plus one month of overlapping subscriptions. For a 10-person team that typically means $150–$400 of labor plus the overlap month.

Did 1Password raise its business price in 2026?

Yes. 1Password raised its Business tier to $8.99/user/month billed annually on March 27, 2026, up from $7.99. Older comparison sites still show the $7.99 figure.
