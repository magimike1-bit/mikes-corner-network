Lightspeed POS Pricing in Canada: Register Plus Payments Bundle Math

Published 2026-10-11

Lightspeed is the Montreal-built POS most Canadian retailers shortlist — but its pricing page sells software tiers while the real bill is software plus processing. Add the one-register-per-plan rule and the penalty for bringing your own processor, and the sticker price tells less than half the story. This guide works the bundle math.

The one-register rule nobody budgets for

Every Lightspeed retail plan — including Plus at $339/month — covers one POS register. A second register is not an add-on with a published price; it requires a custom quote from a Lightspeed sales rep. A two-register store is therefore not buying "a plan" but a plan plus a negotiated extra. Budget the conversation, not just the page price: ask for the per-register cost in writing before you commit, because it changes the plan comparison for any store with more than one till.

Register plus payments: the bundle math at real volumes

The software fee is the smaller half of the bill. Lightspeed Payments — available in Canada — charges 2.6% + 10¢ in person, 2.6% + 30¢ keyed-in, and 2.6% + 30¢ online, with deposits typically landing within two business days. Worked example: a store on Basic ($109/month) doing $30,000 a month across 1,000 in-person transactions pays $780 (2.6%) + $100 (10¢ × 1,000) = $880 in processing. Total monthly bill: $989. The software is 11% of the total — which means the processing rate, not the plan tier, is the number to negotiate.

Lightspeed Payments vs bringing your own processor

You can use a third-party processor with Lightspeed — but it can trigger up to $400/month in additional fees, on top of whatever that processor charges. At that level, almost no external rate is low enough to beat Lightspeed Payments once the surcharge is added. The practical read: Lightspeed's ecosystem is priced to keep processing in-house. If you have a strongly preferred processor, get the exact additional-fee figure from Lightspeed sales before assuming the external quote is comparable.

Decision framework: which plan for which store

  • Single-register shop, simple needs: Basic on annual billing — $89/month, and the loyalty/reporting layer of Core rarely pays for itself at low volume.
  • Multi-channel or loyalty-driven: Core — loyalty, integrations, and deeper reporting justify the step up once volume crosses roughly $25K–$40K a month.
  • Multi-register or API-driven: Plus — but only after you have the extra-register quote in writing, since that number can exceed the plan step-up.

Note: this is a pricing explainer, not a procedure guide — no steps here, just the bundle math to run before you sign.

FAQ

How many registers does a Lightspeed retail plan include?

One — even on the $339/month Plus plan. Additional registers require a custom quote from Lightspeed sales.

What are Lightspeed Payments processing rates?

In person 2.6% + 10¢, keyed-in 2.6% + 30¢, online 2.6% + 30¢. Funds are typically deposited within two business days.

Can I use my own payment processor with Lightspeed?

Yes, but choosing an external processor can trigger up to $400/month in additional fees, which usually erases any rate advantage.
