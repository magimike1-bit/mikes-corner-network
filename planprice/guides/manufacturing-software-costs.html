Manufacturing Software Costs for Small Shops: Katana, MRPeasy, Fishbowl, Odoo Real Pricing

Published 2026-10-12

Manufacturing software pricing splits in two: the small-shop tools with honest published prices, and the ERP world of quote-only tiers. This guide gives you the real 2026 numbers for Katana, MRPeasy, Fishbowl, and Odoo at 3 and 10 users, works the perpetual-license-vs-subscription crossover, and states the ERP quote wall honestly. All prices 2026, USD as listed.

General information, not financial advice. Prices change — always confirm current rates on the vendor's own pricing page before buying.

The fine print that matters: Katana's manufacturing capability is a separate US$199/month add-on on top of the US$299 Core — the sticker most comparisons quote doesn't make anything. 10 users, year one

At 10 users the ranking reshuffles: MRPeasy runs US$7,080/year in licenses plus roughly US$2,000 in onboarding — about US$9,080 year one (third-party estimates put the first-year total at US$7,500–21,000 including US$500–5,000 onboarding, June 2026). Katana lands around US$5,976/year on its flat pricing. And here's the structural alternative: Fishbowl sells a perpetual Manufacturing license from US$4,395 one-time — against MRPeasy's US$5,880/year at 10 users, the perpetual license pays for itself in under a year at that headcount. At 1 user the crossover stretches to ~7.5 years; at 5 users it's ~1.5 years. Small shops that hate subscriptions should price the perpetual option seriously.

What reviewers say

Katana carries a 93% recommend rate from 179 reviews: users praise the modern manufacturing focus and flag performance concerns at scale. Fishbowl vs MRPeasy head-to-head ratings run 75/100 vs 80/100 — MRPeasy ahead on the manufacturing depth that small shops actually buy. Fishbowl reviewers call the interface dated and warn implementation can run long; MRPeasy's cost rating is 2/10 on ITQlick's scale (less expensive than average). The consistent thread: budget the implementation as seriously as the license, whichever vendor you pick.

The ERP quote wall (stated honestly)

Oracle NetSuite (US$30k+/year and up), SAP Business One, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, and Infor publish no usable small-shop pricing — every figure you've seen is agent-reported or stale. The honest statement: small-shop ERP quotes run US$10k–100k+ depending on seats and modules — NetSuite alone starts at US$30k+/year — and implementation is quoted separately from the license. Before you take a quote, demand the per-seat price at your exact user count, implementation broken out separately, and the year-two renewal price — not the year-one discount.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Katana cost?

Katana Core starts at US$299/month, and manufacturing capability is a separate US$199/month Manufacturing Management add-on — US$498/month for a manufacturing-enabled shop. The Core sticker alone doesn't make anything.

Is MRPeasy cheaper than Katana?

Yes. MRPeasy runs US$59/user/month (US$49 at 10–200 employees) versus Katana's US$498/month manufacturing-enabled bundle. At 3 users that's US$177 vs US$498/month — and MRPeasy rates 80/100 vs Fishbowl's 75/100 on manufacturing depth.

Should I buy Fishbowl perpetual or subscribe to MRPeasy?

Fishbowl's perpetual Manufacturing license starts at US$4,395 one-time versus MRPeasy at ~US$5,880/year for 10 users — the perpetual pays for itself in under a year at 10 users. At 1 user the crossover is ~7.5 years; at 5 users ~1.5 years. The more users, the stronger the perpetual case.
