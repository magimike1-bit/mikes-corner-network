Marketing Agency Software Stack Costs: GoHighLevel vs Best-of-Breed at 5 and 20 Staff

Published 2026-10-12

Every agency runs on a stack, and every stack comparison prices the tools one at a time. The all-in-one price: GoHighLevel

GoHighLevel's agency plans are flat: Starter US$97/month (US$970/year, limited sub-accounts), Unlimited US$297/month (US$2,970/year, unlimited workspaces), Agency Pro US$497/month (US$4,970/year, white-label reselling) — verified September–October 2026. The sticker is not the price, though: email, SMS, AI, and phone are metered on top, and one 2026 review analysis advises planning 30–50% on top of the sticker for usage at scale. On reviews, GoHighLevel holds 4.6/5 from 651 G2 reviews (September 2026); the top-cited strength is consolidation and white-labeling, the top-cited weakness is the learning curve — 6–8 weeks, named in 140+ reviews — with email deliverability via Mailgun flagged as the weakest link.

5 staff vs 20 staff: the side-by-side bill

At 5 staff, the best-of-breed stack is: ClickUp 5 × US$7 = US$35, ActiveCampaign Lite US$29, Buffer 10 channels × US$6 = US$60, PandaDoc 2 × US$19 = US$38, SEMrush US$139 — US$301/month. GoHighLevel Unlimited at US$297 plus typical metered usage (~US$80–150) lands at ~US$377–447/month. At this size, best-of-breed is actually cheaper — the all-in-one premium buys consolidation, not savings.

At 20 staff the models diverge hard: the per-seat stack runs to roughly US$1,223/month (ClickUp, ActiveCampaign Plus, Buffer Team at 30 channels, PandaDoc Business, SEMrush at agency tiers), while GoHighLevel stays flat at US$297 plus usage. The crossover is the headcount growth curve: every hire compounds the per-seat stack and costs the all-in-one nothing. If your agency is growing, price the stack at next year's headcount, not today's.

The metered wallet (GoHighLevel's real price)

The US$297 sticker assumes your clients don't text much. SMS, email sends, AI conversations, and phone minutes all meter on top — and agencies report usage adding hundreds per month at scale. Budget the sticker plus 30–50% for a working agency, and model your heaviest SMS month, not your average one. This is also where the per-seat stack has a hidden advantage: its costs are predictable even if they're higher.

The resale P&L

GoHighLevel's agency economics run on sub-accounts: 20 client sub-accounts on the US$297 Unlimited plan works out to ~US$15 per client per month in software cost. White-label it and charge clients US$97–297/month for "your marketing platform," and the tooling becomes a revenue line — five clients at US$97/month is US$485/month against a US$297 cost. No per-seat stack can do this: you can't resell your ClickUp seats. The requirement is an agency that productizes onboarding — the 6–8 week learning curve applies to your clients too.

Frequently asked questions

Is GoHighLevel cheaper than buying separate tools?

At 5 staff, no — a ClickUp + ActiveCampaign + Buffer + PandaDoc + SEMrush stack runs ~US$301/month versus ~US$377–447 for GoHighLevel Unlimited with typical usage. At 20 staff, yes, decisively: ~US$1,223/month versus US$297 plus usage. The all-in-one wins on growth, not on day one.

What does GoHighLevel really cost per month?

US$97–497/month for the plan, plus metered email/SMS/AI/phone usage — budget 30–50% on top of the sticker for a working agency. The resale model (~US$15/client/month in software cost at 20 sub-accounts) can turn it into a profit center.

What is the cheapest agency stack?

At small headcount, the per-seat best-of-breed stack (~US$301/month at 5 staff) is the cheapest honest build. GoHighLevel becomes the cheapest once headcount growth would push per-seat costs past its flat fee.
