Notion Pricing Decoded for Canadian Teams (2026)

Published 2026-10-25 · Last updated 2026-10-25

Notion's pricing page is one of the shortest in work management: a free plan, Plus at $10 per user/month and Business at $20, both billed annually (checked October 23, 2026). The short ladder hides the three decisions that actually set a Canadian team's bill: who counts as a paid member, when the AI features matter, and which tier's admin controls a growing team can't skip.

The ladder, and the member trap

PlanPrice (USD)The line that matters
Free$0Real workspace for individuals and tiny teams; block/history limits
Plus$10/user/mo annualUnlimited blocks and files, longer version history, more guests
Business$20/user/mo annualPrivate teamspaces, granular permissions, Notion AI included in the tier story

Notion charges for members, not guests — and the recurring budget error is inviting collaborators as members who only ever view or comment. A ten-person company with six true members and four guest-shaped collaborators pays $60 a month on Plus if it keeps the roles straight, $100 if it doesn't. Audit the member list quarterly; at $10–20 a seat, role hygiene is the cheapest feature Notion sells.

Why teams jump to Business

The Plus-to-Business doubling buys control, not capacity: private teamspaces, finer permissions, and the AI package. For a team whose wiki holds client-confidential material, private teamspaces alone justify the tier. For a team buying Business "for the AI," the honest test is usage — AI you open twice a month is a $10/user/month ornament.

Canadian notes

Notion bills in USD as published; budget exchange on top of every seat, and GST/HST treatment per your province (see our province tax pages — the tax layer is recoverable for registered businesses in HST provinces, a real cost in BC PST terms). Against ClickUp ($7 Unlimited), Notion is the pricier workspace — the head-to-head explains when the premium is earned.

A five-person team's real bill

Five members on Plus: $50/month billed annually, $600 a year in USD. The same team mis-structured — nine members because four collaborators were invited as members instead of guests — pays $90 a month, $1,080 a year: a $480 annual penalty for an invitation setting. On Business it's starker: five true members cost $100/month, while nine costs $180. Notion's pricing rewards administrative tidiness more than most tools in this dataset, because its guest model is genuinely functional for view-and-comment collaborators. Set a quarterly ten-minute audit: members list versus payroll list, guests who became staff (upgrade them — they need the access), members who left (remove them — they still have yours).

Notion in the stack

For Canadian teams the seat rarely travels alone: Notion commonly sits beside Slack (from $7.25/user) or inside a Workspace/Microsoft seat that already includes a wiki-shaped tool (Google Sites/Docs, SharePoint). The honest comparison for a team already paying for Google Workspace Standard (C$18.40) is "Notion Plus at $10 USD on top" versus "the tools inside the seat we already own" — Notion wins that comparison on database and wiki craft for most teams, but it should be made consciously, because $10 × headcount × 12 months is how quiet stack creep happens. Frequently asked questions

Is Notion free for a whole team? The free plan supports small teams with limits on blocks, history and guests that working teams eventually feel. It's a genuine starting tier, not a demo — many two-person firms run on it for a year.

Does Notion AI cost extra? AI packaging has moved between add-on and Business-tier inclusion in recent plan revisions — check your workspace's plan page for the current arrangement before budgeting around it.

Can I pay in CAD? Notion bills in USD as published. Canadian firms should model the exchange rate and the typical card conversion spread on top of the seat price.

The bottom line

Notion's pricing is honest and its ladder is short; the buying skill is role hygiene. Keep true collaborators as members, everyone else as guests, and the Business tier becomes a deliberate purchase (privacy controls, AI, admin depth) instead of an accident of invitations. Canadian teams should budget the USD seats with exchange on top, note that per-seat tools compound quietly as headcount grows, and set the quarterly member audit on the calendar today — at $10–20 a seat, it is the highest-value recurring ten minutes in the whole work-management category.
