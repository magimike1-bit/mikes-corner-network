Notion vs Confluence Pricing: Per-Seat Math for Small Teams and AI Add-On Costs

Last updated 2026-10-11

On sticker price, Confluence undercuts Notion nearly two-to-one — and that's before the AI add-on math, which flips the value story again. But the sticker isn't the decision: the free-tier trigger decides year one, and the AI packaging decides year two. Prices below are published list rates (USD) checked against 2026 pricing trackers.

The base per-seat prices

Confluence is cheaper at every comparable rung — roughly half the per-seat price. Notion's counter is breadth: databases, project views, and templates that can replace a second tool, which is a real saving only if it actually replaces one.

The AI add-on: $10/seat extra vs bundled

Here's the math most comparisons skip. Notion AI has been sold as a $10/user/month add-on on lower tiers — doubling the cost of a Plus seat from $10 to $20. On the Business tier and up, AI is included. Confluence takes the opposite approach: Atlassian Intelligence (Rovo) AI is bundled into Premium and Enterprise plans at no extra per-seat charge.

Price the AI-inclusive seat: Notion Plus + AI = ~$20/user/month; Confluence Premium with AI = ~$10.44/user/month. If AI writing, summaries, and search are part of the requirement, Confluence's effective price is roughly half of Notion's. One caution: Notion has changed its AI packaging more than once — verify the current bundle on Notion's pricing page before you budget, because trackers disagree by date.

The 10-user row is the striking one: ten users fit exactly inside Confluence's free tier, so year one costs $0 — the same team pays $1,200 on Notion Plus before AI. At 25 users the gap is $1,275/year on base plans and widens with AI.

The free-tier trigger: when paying starts

Notion's free tier is generous for individuals but the paid trigger arrives per member as the team grows. Confluence draws its line at 10 users — cross to user 11 and Standard billing starts for the paid seats. For teams that will sit at 8–10 people for a while, Confluence's free tier is effectively a long-term plan, not a trial.

Who the cheaper seat actually suits

  • Confluence suits teams already in the Atlassian orbit — native Jira integration, structured spaces, and granular permissions are the real reasons to pick it; the lower price is a bonus.
  • Notion suits teams that want one flexible workspace for docs, databases, and light project tracking — the premium is justified only if it consolidates tools.
  • AI-first buyers should price the AI-inclusive seat, not the base seat — that's where Confluence's lead doubles.

FAQ

Is Notion AI included in the subscription or extra?

It depends on tier and timing. Pricing trackers report Notion AI as a $10/user/month add-on on lower tiers, with AI included on the Business tier and up. Notion has changed this packaging more than once, so verify on Notion's pricing page before budgeting.

How many users can use Confluence free?

Up to 10 users on the free tier. A 10-person team can run Confluence free indefinitely, which makes Confluence's year-one cost $0 at exactly the headcount where Notion starts charging per member.

Which is cheaper for a 10-person team, Notion or Confluence?

Confluence, by a wide margin. Ten users fit inside Confluence's free tier ($0/year), while Notion Plus costs $1,200/year for ten members — $2,400/year if you add Notion AI per seat.
