Pipedrive Plans Decoded: What Each Tier Unlocks and the Real Per-Seat Cost

Last updated 2026-10-11

Pipedrive sells four CRM tiers — Lite, Growth, Premium, and Ultimate — and the pricing page answers "what does each cost" while leaving the harder questions unasked: what does each tier actually unlock, what does the team pay per year at 5, 10, or 25 seats, and does the advertised annual discount hold up per tier? This guide decodes the tiers with dated prices, works the seat math, and audits the discount claim.

What each tier unlocks

Lite ($14). The entry tier: visual pipelines, calendar management, lead/deal/contact tracking, AI-generated reports, and 500+ integrations. Enough when someone will manually maintain the deal record.

Growth ($39). Adds full two-way email sync, workflow automation, nurturing sequences, subscription tracking, forecasts, and the meeting scheduler. Growth is the cheapest plan with the automation builder — the practical starting point for most sales teams.

Premium ($59). Adds lead generation and routing, AI-powered email tools, contracts and e-signatures via Smart Docs, enhanced customization, the Projects add-on, and 24/7 live chat plus phone support.

Ultimate ($79). Adds fortified security rules and alerts, email and phone data enrichment, unlimited usage limits, and a sandbox testing account. Buy it for a specific security or capacity requirement.

The annual-discount audit: "up to 42%" vs the real numbers

Moving a 5-person team from Lite to Growth adds $1,500 a year — and each new salesperson on Growth adds $468 a year. For Canadian budgeting, convert at your card's rate: at ~1.36, five Growth seats run about CA$3,180 a year. One more rule that changes the math: Pipedrive charges for seats even when they are unassigned, so review the paid seat count before a renewal, especially after a rep leaves.

The add-on stack: what the real bill looks like

The per-seat price is only part of the bill. LeadBooster (chatbot, web forms, prospecting) is a paid add-on on Lite and Growth and included on Premium and Ultimate; Projects is bought per seat on Lite and Growth and included on Premium and Ultimate; Campaigns (email marketing) is a separate add-on. The good news: add-ons are priced per company rather than per user, so they scale gently as the team grows. The watch-out: buyers of the cheapest tiers should price the add-ons they will actually need before concluding that Lite or Growth is the cheap option — the "stack" can close the gap to the next tier up.

Trade note for pricing veterans: the surprise charges that sting most in SaaS are rarely the seat price — they are per-device connections and per-integration fees. One provider priced a device connection even when it only read data. On Pipedrive, run the same discipline on add-ons and top-ups, which follow the plan's billing cycle.

Decision framework: which tier fits your team

  • Start with Lite if one person owns the pipeline and is willing to maintain it manually.
  • Start with Growth if the CRM must manage email follow-up as well as the pipeline — it's the cheapest tier with email sync and automations.
  • Step up to Premium when its included add-ons (LeadBooster, Projects) replace tools you would otherwise pay for separately.
  • Buy Ultimate only for a concrete security, sandbox, or capacity requirement — not for features.

Note: this is a pricing explainer, not a procedure guide — there are no steps to follow here, just tier math to check against your headcount before you buy.

FAQ

Is Growth worth it over Lite for a small sales team?

Growth is the cheapest plan with two-way email sync and the automation builder. If someone will manually maintain the pipeline and you don't need automated follow-ups or sequences, Lite is enough.

Do I pay for Pipedrive seats nobody uses?

Yes — Pipedrive charges for seats even when they are unassigned. Review the paid seat count before every renewal, especially after a rep leaves.

How much does annual billing actually save per tier?

The real discount is 41.7% on Lite, 20.4% on Growth, 25.3% on Premium, and 20.2% on Ultimate. The advertised "up to 42%" figure applies to Lite alone.
