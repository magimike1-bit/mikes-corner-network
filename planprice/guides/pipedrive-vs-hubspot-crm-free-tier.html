Pipedrive vs HubSpot Free CRM: Only One of These Is Free

Published 2026-10-11

Most "Pipedrive vs HubSpot" comparisons frame this as two free tiers competing. It isn't. Pipedrive has no free tier at all — a 14-day trial, then paid from $14/user/month. HubSpot's free CRM is genuinely free for up to 2 users. The real comparison isn't free vs free; it's what each tool's first paid invoice looks like, and what triggers it. That's what this guide prices.

The headline neither vendor leads with: only one of these is free

Pipedrive's entry point is a 14-day free trial with full feature access — then you pay. There is no permanent free plan, and there never has been. HubSpot's free CRM, by contrast, is free with no expiry for up to 2 users. If "free" is a hard requirement, the comparison ends here — Pipedrive is disqualified and HubSpot wins by default.

HubSpot free: what's included and where it ends

HubSpot free covers up to 2 users and about 1,000 contacts, with a 1-million-record ceiling across other standard objects (per HubSpot's Free Tools pricing FAQ). The free tier covers the core CRM — contacts, companies and deals — with the paid gates documented below. What forces the upgrade is rarely the contact count. It's the feature gates: no workflow automation, no custom reporting, no branding removal. The day you need any of those, free is over.

Pipedrive: no free tier — the trial-to-paid math

When Pipedrive's trial ends, the ladder is (per user per month, annual billing): Essential $14, Advanced $39, Professional $49, Power $64, Enterprise $99. Monthly billing costs 30–40% more (Essential jumps to $24). A 3-person team on Essential pays $504/year — the minimum viable Pipedrive bill. But Essential is deliberately thin: email sequences and workflow automation unlock at Advanced ($39/user/month), which is where Pipedrive actually starts working as a sales tool. That same 3-person team on Advanced pays $1,404/year.

The upgrade triggers, priced

  • HubSpot's trigger — the Starter-to-Professional cliff. Starter runs ~$20/seat/month and removes branding with simple automation. The features growing teams actually need — sequences, workflow automation, custom reporting, multiple pipelines — live on Professional at ~$90–100/seat/month (commonly sold from $1,170/month for 5 seats), plus a mandatory onboarding fee. Going from 2 free users to a 5-seat Professional setup can take you from $0 to over $14,000 in year one. That's the number to budget for before committing to HubSpot's ecosystem.
  • Pipedrive's trigger — the Essential-to-Advanced jump. $14 to $39 per user per month for email sequences and automation. Meaningful but predictable: a 3-person team goes from $504 to $1,404 a year, and the price grows linearly with headcount from there.

Net: HubSpot is cheaper at the bottom (free beats $504/year) and dramatically more expensive at the top. Pipedrive is never free but never cliffs — its cost curve is a steady slope where HubSpot's is a staircase.

Decision framework: which free-to-paid path fits your team

  1. 1–2 people, simple pipeline: HubSpot free. $0, no expiry, no reason to pay Pipedrive $28–$78/month for the same job.
  2. 3+ people who live in the pipeline: Pipedrive Advanced at $39/user/month. Predictable, sales-focused, no cliff.
  3. You need marketing automation too: HubSpot — but budget Professional from day one, because Starter's ceiling arrives fast and the cliff is the real price.
  4. Ask before committing: on HubSpot, "which of my workflows need Professional, and what's the onboarding fee?" On Pipedrive, "can we run on Essential for 90 days before judging whether we need Advanced?"

A note on structure: this is a price-comparison explainer, not a repair procedure — there are no settings to change, only the upgrade-trigger math to run before you commit.

FAQ

Does Pipedrive have a free plan?

No — 14-day trial only, then paid from $14/user/month (annual). If a free CRM is a requirement, HubSpot's free tier is the one priced in this guide.

How many contacts does HubSpot's free CRM allow?

About 1,000 contacts for up to 2 users, with 1 million records across other objects. The practical upgrade trigger is the lack of automation, custom reporting and branding removal — not the contact cap.

What is HubSpot's pricing cliff?

The Starter (~$20/seat/month) to Professional (~$90–100/seat/month, from $1,170/month for 5 seats plus mandatory onboarding) jump, where sequences, automation, custom reports and multiple pipelines unlock.
