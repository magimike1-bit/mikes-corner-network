QuickBooks Online Canada Tiers: EasyStart vs Essentials vs Plus in CAD

Published 2026-10-11

Most QuickBooks comparisons quote US dollars and the US plan name "Simple Start" — but Canadians buy "EasyStart" in Canadian dollars, at different prices, with different promo cycles. Pick the wrong tier and you either pay for features you'll never touch or hit a user cap that forces a jump. Here's the Canadian ladder in CAD, the cliffs between tiers, and the cheapest tier that fits common business types.

The four Canadian tiers, in CAD

Regular monthly prices, verified October 2026: EasyStart from $24 (1 user) — invoicing, expense tracking, sales tax, bank connections, for sole proprietors. Essentials from $45 (up to 3 users) — adds bill management and bill pay, time tracking, and multi-currency. Plus from $85 (up to 5 users) — adds inventory tracking, project profitability, budgets, and class/location tracking. Advanced from $200 (up to 25 users) — adds custom reporting, workflow automation, role-based permissions, and data restore. Intuit runs frequent 50%-off introductory promos, so the first months are cheaper — budget from the regular rate, not the promo.

The user-count cliffs

The user caps are hard plan boundaries — there is no per-seat add-on. Adding a 4th user forces Essentials ($45) to Plus ($85): $40/month more, $480/year, for one login. Adding a 6th user forces Plus ($85) to Advanced ($200): $115/month more, $1,380/year, for one login. This is the most expensive way to discover the tier structure, and it's how most growing businesses meet it — mid-year, when the bookkeeper needs access.

Plan your 12-month headcount before you subscribe. If you'll plausibly need 4 users within the year, the "cheap" Essentials plan costs you the Plus price anyway — subscribe to Plus from the start and skip the mid-year migration.

The feature gates that matter

Three gates decide the tier for most businesses. Bill pay and multi-currency gate Essentials: if you pay suppliers through the software or invoice in USD, EasyStart can't do it — that's a $21/month step. Inventory and project profitability gate Plus: product businesses, trades, and anyone tracking job costs need Plus; the $40/month step from Essentials buys the entire operational layer. Role permissions and automation gate Advanced: only businesses with a finance team and real approval workflows need this — everyone else is over-buying at $200/month.

Note what no tier includes: payroll is a separate add-on at every level, and the August 2026 renewal price changes (Essentials $75→$85, Plus $115→$140, Advanced $275→$340 on the US list) are a reminder that Intuit reprices regularly — today's tier math deserves a re-check at each renewal.

Which tier for which business

  • Solo freelancer, invoices only → EasyStart ($24). The cheapest sufficient tier. Upgrade the month you hire help or need bill pay.
  • Service business with suppliers, 2–3 users → Essentials ($45). Bill management, time tracking, and multi-currency cover the standard service workflow.
  • Product business, trades, project work, 4–5 users → Plus ($85). Inventory, purchase orders, budgets, and project profitability are the whole reason this tier exists.
  • Finance team, 6+ users, approval workflows → Advanced ($200). Don't buy this for the user count alone — buy it for permissions and automation.

The annual math

At regular rates: EasyStart $288/year, Essentials $540/year, Plus $1,020/year, Advanced $2,400/year — before GST/HST and before payroll. The jump from Essentials to Plus costs $480/year; from Plus to Advanced, $1,380/year. Against those numbers, the intro promos are noise. The decision that saves real money isn't timing a promo — it's refusing the tier your business doesn't need yet, and knowing exactly which hire or feature will force the next crossing.

A note on format: this is a pricing comparison, not a how-to — there are no step-by-step actions because the question it answers is which plan fits your business, and that is what the tables above are for.

FAQ

How much is QuickBooks Online in Canada per month?

In CAD: EasyStart from $24/month (1 user), Essentials from $45/month (up to 3 users), Plus from $85/month (up to 5 users), and Advanced from $200/month (up to 25 users). Prices verified October 2026.

What happens if I need more users than my QuickBooks plan allows?

You must upgrade the whole plan: a 4th user forces Essentials ($45) to Plus ($85), and a 6th user forces Plus ($85) to Advanced ($200). There is no per-seat add-on — the user cap is a hard plan boundary.

Which QuickBooks Online plan does a freelancer need in Canada?

EasyStart ($24/month) covers invoicing, expense tracking, and sales tax for one user. Move to Essentials ($45) when you need to pay bills, track time, or handle multiple currencies.
