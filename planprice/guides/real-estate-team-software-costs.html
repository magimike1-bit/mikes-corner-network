Real Estate Team Software Costs: The Per-Agent Real Price

Published 2026-10-12

Every real estate CRM comparison prices the CRM. But the CRM is barely half the bill — teams also buy the dialer, the IDX website, and the lead sources, and the vendors that bundle everything publish no pricing at all. This guide prices the full stack for a 5-agent team, with October 2026 figures and Canadian-dollar conversions (all vendors below bill in USD).

General information, not financial advice. Prices change — always confirm current rates on the vendor's own pricing page before buying.

The CRM is half the bill

Follow Up Boss publishes transparent tiers: Grow at US$69/user/month (US$58 billed annually), Pro at US$499/month for 10 users (US$416 annual), Platform at US$1,000/month for 30 users (US$833 annual). No long-term contracts, unlimited contacts and lead sources. It holds 4.6/5 from 118 G2 reviews (September 2026), with lead routing, Smart Lists, and Action Plans its most-cited strengths.

The line CRM tables skip: calling is a US$39/user/month add-on on the Grow plan (US$33 annual), included from Pro up. And Follow Up Boss deliberately doesn't bundle an IDX website or managed marketing — teams connect separate providers. So the honest 5-agent build is: Grow annual 5 × US$58 = US$290, plus dialer 5 × US$33 = US$165, for US$455/month (about CA$649 at recent retail rates) — before the website.

The BoldTrail quote-checklist

BoldTrail (formerly kvCORE) publishes no pricing — every number online is agent-reported: roughly US$499–750/month for a solo agent plus a US$500–1,000 setup fee, ~US$1,200/month for a 3–10 agent team, US$1,800+/month at brokerage scale. Before the sales call, demand in writing: the monthly figure at your exact agent count, the setup fee, what's bundled (website, IDX, dialer, AI nurture), the contract term and exit, and what happens to your site and data if you leave. And check one thing first: many brokerages (eXp among them) bundle kvCORE into their technology package — if your brokerage already pays, your cost is zero and this whole comparison is moot.

Where the money actually goes (dialer, IDX, leads)

Notice what's missing from every table: lead sources. Portal leads (Zillow, Realtor.com) are the real budget line — if you spend a few hundred dollars a month on portal leads, Follow Up Boss earns its price on one converted lead, as one October 2026 analysis puts it. If your business comes from past clients and referrals (about half the typical book, per NAR's 2026 Member Profile), you're paying for machinery built to work strangers — a simpler CRM like Wise Agent or even a contact manager may be the better buy. Price the lead source before the software.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Follow Up Boss cost per month?

Grow: US$69/user/month, or US$58/user/month billed annually. Pro: US$499/month for 10 users (US$416 annual). Calling is a US$39/user/month add-on on Grow. A solo agent on Grow with calling pays about US$1,092/year on annual billing.

How much does kvCORE (BoldTrail) cost?

It publishes no pricing. Agents report roughly US$499–750/month solo (plus US$500–1,000 setup), ~US$1,200/month for small teams. Get a written quote — and check whether your brokerage already includes it.

What's the cheapest real estate CRM for a team?

On published pricing, LionDesk (US$30–99/month) and Wise Agent (~US$41.58/month annual) are the budget picks. They trade polish and support depth for price — fine for referral-heavy teams, risky for lead-buying teams that live in the dialer.
