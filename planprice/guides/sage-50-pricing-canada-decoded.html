Sage 50 Pricing Canada: Pro, Premium, and Quantum, Decoded

Published 2026-10-12

Sage 50 — formerly Simply Accounting — still runs the books of countless Canadian shops and trades. But buying it in 2026 is trickier than the pricing page suggests: the Canadian price book is completely separate from the US one, the product is subscription-only, and the per-user math changes dramatically between tiers. This guide lays out the real numbers.

General information, not financial advice. Prices change — always confirm the current rates on the vendor's own pricing page before buying.

Pro vs Premium vs Quantum: the Canadian tiers

Sage 50 Canada sells three subscription tiers, billed annually, checked 2026-10-12 on Sage's own pricing page. Pro Accounting costs CA$916/year and covers one user: income, invoices, expenses, payments, bank reconciliation, remote data access, and inventory tracking. Premium Accounting is CA$1,371/year for two or four users and adds advanced budgeting, multi-location inventory, multi-company consolidation, unlimited currencies (Pro caps at two), and serial-number tracking. Quantum Accounting starts at CA$6,341/year for five or more users, with role-based security and faster processing for larger teams.

Every plan includes automatic updates and unlimited phone and chat support, with a 30-day free trial that asks for no payment details. One warning: these are Canadian prices. Search results constantly mix in US Sage 50cloud figures — around US$668/yr for Pro and US$1,147 for Premium — a different price book entirely. If a number you find online is far below the Canadian figures above, you are reading the US edition.

The per-user math: where the jump hurts

The headline prices hide the real story: cost per user. On Pro, one user costs CA$916 a year. On Premium with four users, that falls to about CA$343 per user per year. Quantum's entry at CA$6,341 for five users lands around CA$1,268 per user per year, because you are paying for the enterprise scaffolding (role permissions, workflow controls) as well as seats.

Payroll changes the equation. The Pro + Payroll bundle starts at CA$1,758/year and Premium + Payroll at CA$2,248/year. For a business already running Canadian payroll with T4s, T4As, RL-1s and ROEs, Sage 50's payroll is one of its genuine strengths — bilingual English/French, with CRA plus Revenu Québec compliance built in.

What reviewers actually say

On G2, Sage 50cloud Accounting holds 3.9 out of 5 from 163 reviews, praised for customizable reports and deep inventory and payroll features. A verified G2 reviewer, Anthony M., a general accountant at a small business, gave it 4.5/5 in May 2026: invoicing and expense tracking "save me a lot of time" — while noting the software can feel outdated and support is sometimes slow.

The recurring pricing complaint: G2's own comparison pages flag that "costs can add up quickly with more users or features," and several reviewers report switching to Xero or QuickBooks Online on cost. Sage 50 is a desktop program with cloud connectivity — not browser-native software — and since the move to subscription-only, stopping payment means losing access. Treat it as a lease, not a purchase.

The decision framework

  1. Buy Sage 50 if inventory or Canadian payroll is the job. Multi-location inventory, FIFO costing, serial numbers, and deep CRA payroll are its moat.
  2. Count users honestly. A solo operator on Pro pays CA$916/user/yr; a four-person team on Premium pays ~CA$343/user/yr. The second seat is cheap — the first is expensive.
  3. Price the payroll bundle against your payroll provider. Pro + Payroll at CA$1,758/yr may beat a standalone payroll service; if you already use one, the bundle premium is dead money.
  4. Confirm you're reading the Canadian price book. US figures are roughly 25–30% lower and will mislead any budget.
  5. Accept the subscription. There is no perpetual license anymore; budget it as an annual operating cost, not a one-time buy.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sage 50 cloud software?

Not exactly. It is Windows desktop software with cloud connectivity for remote data access and automatic updates. If you need a tool that runs fully in a browser, Sage 50 is the wrong product.

Can you still buy Sage 50 outright?

No. Sage 50 is subscription-only now — you pay annually, and access ends if you stop paying.

Does Sage 50 handle Canadian payroll and Quebec taxes?

Yes — payroll bundles cover T4, T4A, RL-1 and ROE filings, and the product is bilingual English/French.

Why is Sage 50 cheaper in the US?

It is a separate price book, not a currency difference. US Sage 50cloud pricing (around US$668/yr Pro) does not apply to Canadian buyers — always use the en-ca pricing page.
