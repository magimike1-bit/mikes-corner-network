Salon & Spa Software Pricing: Subscriptions vs Marketplace Commissions

Published 2026-10-12

Salon software splits into two pricing models: flat subscriptions (Vagaro, Mangomint, GlossGenius, Square) and Fresha's hybrid — per-member plans plus a 20% commission on clients its marketplace sends you. This guide prices both with October 2026 figures and works the 4-chair crossover math that decides between them.

Note the correction to Fresha's reputation: it is not a $0 platform. The per-member plans (US$14.95–19.95) are the subscription, and the 20% commission applies only to new clients who find you through Fresha's marketplace — your own repeat book costs nothing extra. That distinction is the whole decision.

The 4-chair math: where the crossover sits

Take a 4-chair salon with an US$80 average new-client booking. On Fresha Team: 4 × US$14.95 = US$59.80 in subscriptions, plus 20% commission on marketplace-sourced new clients. At 50 new marketplace clients a month, that's 50 × US$16 = US$800 in commission — US$859.80/month total. Against Vagaro's ~US$30 flat, the crossover sits at roughly 10 new marketplace clients a month (US$160 in commission): past that, the subscription wins; below it, Fresha's marketplace is cheap client acquisition.

The growth trap: Fresha is cheapest when your repeat clientele books direct and the marketplace is bonus volume. As the marketplace becomes your primary client source — which is exactly what happens as you grow on the platform — the 20% commission compounds into the most expensive plan on this page. Users praise Fresha's seamless booking and marketplace discovery while criticizing exactly this: the commission accumulating at scale.

What reviewers actually say

Fresha: 4.8/5 from 1,448 Capterra reviews and 4.8 from 7,200+ Trustpilot reviews — users praise the booking flow and discovery, criticize the commission at scale. Vagaro: 4.7 from 3,661 Capterra reviews against 3.6 from 706 Trustpilot reviews — the split suggests the product outruns its support reputation. Mangomint carries a 5.0 on Capterra; Boulevard 4.6; Square 4.5; Zenoti 4.4; Mindbody 4.0. Solo plans across the category run US$20–30/month; multi-staff builds run US$165–370/month.

Which model fits your shop

Pick Fresha when you're a new shop with a thin book: the per-member cost is low and every marketplace client is revenue you wouldn't have had — but track the new-client share monthly, because the model that acquired your clients can become the model that taxes them. Pick a flat subscription when you're established with a full repeat book: Vagaro at ~US$30 or Mangomint at US$120 + US$10/user protect your margins as volume grows. Pick Square Appointments when you're already on Square for payments — the free tier is genuinely free, and Plus at US$49 covers most single-location needs.

Frequently asked questions

Is Fresha really free?

No — that's outdated. Fresha charges US$14.95–19.95 per bookable member per month, plus a 20% commission (US$6 minimum) on new clients who book through its marketplace. Your own repeat clients cost nothing extra.

Vagaro vs Fresha: which is cheaper?

Below ~10 new marketplace clients a month, Fresha (US$59.80 for 4 chairs + small commission). Above that, Vagaro's ~US$30 flat subscription wins — the 20% commission overtakes it fast.

How much does Mangomint cost?

US$120/month base plus US$10 per user — about US$165/month for 10 staff. It carries a 5.0 Capterra rating, the highest in the category.
