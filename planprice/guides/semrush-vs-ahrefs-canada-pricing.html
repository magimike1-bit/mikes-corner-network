Semrush vs Ahrefs Pricing in Canada: CAD Prices, New Plans, Real Limits

Published 2026-10-11

Every Semrush-vs-Ahrefs comparison quotes USD and lists plan names Semrush retired months ago. This is the Canadian buyer's version: prices converted to CAD at the October 2026 rate, Semrush's renamed 2026 ladder, and the usage limits that actually force your upgrade — projects, credits, report rows, and the seat surcharges that reorder the whole comparison for teams.

The live prices, October 2026 (and Semrush's renamed ladder)

Semrush retired Pro/Guru/Business in May 2026. The live lineup, verified on semrush.com/pricing in September 2026 (monthly / annual per-month): SEO — $139 / ~$117.33 (5 websites, 500 tracked keywords — the old Pro at the same price and limits); Starter — $199 / ~$165.17 (5 sites, 500 keywords, 50 AI prompts/day); Pro+ — $299 / ~$248.17 (15 sites, 1,500 keywords — old Guru limits plus AI visibility, $49/month more than Guru was); Advanced — $549 / ~$455.67 (40 sites, 5,000 keywords — old Business limits plus AI, $49/month more than Business was). Annual billing saves roughly 17% across the line.

Ahrefs (monthly / billed yearly): Starter — $29 (monthly billing only; 200 credits/month, 1 project, 50 tracked keywords); Lite — $129 / $1,290 per year (1,000 credits/month per user, 5 projects, 750 tracked keywords, 6 months history); Standard — $249 / $2,490 per year (unlimited credits with fair use, 20 projects, 2,000 keywords, 2 years history, Content Explorer); Advanced — $449; Enterprise — $1,499 on annual commitment. Ahrefs Webmaster Tools remains free for sites you verify.

What Canadians actually pay: the USD billing reality

Both vendors bill in USD. At the October 2026 mid-market rate of about 1.43 CAD per USD:

  • Semrush SEO $139/mo → ~$199 CAD/month (~$2,385 CAD/year); annual billing ~$117.33/mo → ~$168 CAD/month
  • Ahrefs Lite $129/mo → ~$184 CAD/month; annual $1,290/yr → ~$154 CAD/month (~$1,845 CAD/year)
  • Ahrefs Standard $249/mo → ~$356 CAD/month; Semrush Pro+ $299/mo → ~$428 CAD/month

Your card's billing rate includes an FX markup over mid-market, so treat these as floors. The practical point: a "$129 tool" is a $184 CAD line item, and the $10 USD gap between Semrush SEO and Ahrefs Lite is a $15 CAD gap every month.

The limits that decide your tier: projects, credits, seats

The sticker price is rarely the binding constraint. On Ahrefs, Lite's 1,000 credits per user per month is what frustrates heavy researchers — Standard removes the credit meter entirely, which is an underrated reason to upgrade. Every Ahrefs tier also includes exactly one user; extra seats cost $40/month on Lite, $60 on Standard, $80 on Advanced. A 3-person team on Standard pays $249 + $120 = $369/month, 48% above the sticker price.

On Semrush, the 5-project ceiling and the 500-tracked-keyword cap are the usual tripwires, and extra users start at $45/month. A 3-person team on Pro+ lands around $299 + $90 = ~$389/month — seats reorder the comparison before features do. Always model your actual seat count, not the headline price.

Solo operator vs agency: who each suits

Solo / freelancer, pure SEO: Ahrefs — the $29 Starter for light use or Lite at $129 for serious work. Solo, broader marketing: Semrush SEO at $139 — keyword research plus PPC, content-marketing and competitive-intelligence tools in one subscription. Agency / team: count seats first, then compare — and note Semrush bundles AI-visibility tracking into its $199 Starter.

The decision framework: three questions before you buy

  1. How many seats? Multiply the sticker price by your real seat count with surcharges — this alone often decides it.
  2. Which meter binds first? Credits (Ahrefs Lite), projects, report rows, or keyword counts — buy for the meter you'll hit, not the feature list.
  3. Do you need the AI-visibility bundle? If yes, Semrush includes it from $199; if no, don't pay the $49/month premium baked into Pro+ and Advanced over the old Guru/Business prices.

A note on structure: this is a price-comparison explainer, not a repair procedure — there are no settings to change, only the per-seat and per-limit math to run before you buy.
