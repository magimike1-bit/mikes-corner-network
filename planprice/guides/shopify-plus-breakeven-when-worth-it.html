Shopify Plus Breakeven: When the $2,300/Month Plan Pays vs Advanced

Published 2026-10-11

Shopify Plus costs roughly six times Advanced — $2,300/month against $399 — so the question isn't whether Plus is better, it's at what sales volume the lower fees and exclusive features cover the $1,901/month gap. The answer is arithmetic, not vibes: a GMV crossover you can compute, a term-price swing worth $2,400/year, and feature gates that often decide before the math does. Prices are published USD rates, checked October 2026.

The term swing deserves emphasis: the same Plus contract costs $2,400/year more on the 1-year term than the 3-year term. And above roughly $800,000/month in revenue, the flat fee gives way to a variable platform fee whose percentage Shopify does not publish — blog figures like 0.25% or a $40,000 cap describe particular contracts, not a public rate card. The breakeven math: when fee savings cover the delta

The formula is simple: monthly sales × (fee-rate difference) = platform-fee difference. The platform gap is $1,901/month ($2,300 − $399 on monthly billing). Calculated from Shopify's published US rates (October 2026):

  • On Shopify Payments: crossover at about $760,000/month in sales (monthly billing).
  • With a third-party gateway: crossover at about $292,000/month — because the added fee falls from 0.5% to 0.2%, upgrading pays off at much lower volume when you can't use Shopify Payments.

The $0.30 per-order fee is identical on every plan, so it cancels out of the comparison. Note the irony: the Shopify Payments crossover (~$760k/mo) sits just under the ~$800k/mo line where the variable platform fee can kick in — at the very top end, the math gets renegotiated anyway.

The feature gates: what money alone doesn't decide

Many Plus upgrades happen far below the GMV crossover, triggered by features Advanced can't offer:

  • Checkout customization — full Checkout Extensibility and Shopify Functions are Plus-only; Advanced's checkout is locked.
  • Native B2B — wholesale catalogs and company-level pricing built in, versus apps on Advanced.
  • Automation — Launchpad for scheduled launches is Plus-exclusive (Flow is available on Advanced).
  • Scale limits — unlimited staff accounts (Advanced caps at 15), ~10x API limits, POS Pro for up to 200 locations.

The expansion-store factor

Selling in several countries changes the math independently of GMV. Plus includes your main store plus 9 expansion stores; the 11th costs $300/month ($3,600/year). A brand running 12 regional storefronts pays $7,200/year in extra stores on top of the $27,600 licence — while on Advanced each storefront would need its own plan. Multi-region sellers should price the storefront count before the GMV crossover.

Decision framework: stay on Advanced or move to Plus

  • Stay on Advanced if: under ~$500k/month GMV, single storefront, no B2B, checkout works as-is. It's the better plan for most growing stores.
  • Move to Plus if: approaching the $760k/month fee crossover, need a second storefront, B2B is meaningful, checkout customization is required, or you've hit the 15-staff or API limits.
  • Negotiate if: above ~$800k/month — the variable fee is bespoke, and the published numbers stop applying.

Why no step-by-step here: this is a buying-decision guide, not a setup walkthrough — the breakeven framework above replaces procedural steps.

FAQ

At what revenue does Shopify Plus pay for itself?

On fee math alone, about $760,000/month in sales on Shopify Payments (monthly billing), or about $292,000/month with a third-party gateway — calculated from Shopify's published US rates, October 2026. But feature gates (second storefront, B2B, checkout customization) often trigger the move earlier.

Is Shopify Plus $2,300 or $2,500 per month?

Both — $2,300/month on a 3-year term, $2,500/month on a 1-year term. The term choice is worth $2,400/year. Above roughly $800,000/month in revenue, Plus moves to a variable platform fee with an unpublished rate.

How many stores are included in Shopify Plus?

Your main store plus 9 expansion stores are included in the platform fee. Each store beyond the tenth costs $300/month — so 12 storefronts add $7,200/year on top of the licence.
