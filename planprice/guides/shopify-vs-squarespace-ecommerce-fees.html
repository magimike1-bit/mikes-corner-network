Shopify vs Squarespace Transaction Fees: The $10k/Month Math

Published 2026-10-12

The Shopify-vs-Squarespace price debate is usually fought over subscription stickers — US$39 vs US$16 a month. That's the wrong fight. The money is in the transaction fees: a percentage of every sale, compounding monthly. This guide re-checks both vendors' current 2026 tiers and fee tables in one place, then works the annual bill at US$10,000/month in sales.

General information, not financial advice. Prices and fees change — always confirm the current rates on each vendor's own pricing page before buying.

$10,000/month in sales: the real annual bill

At US$120,000/year in sales, comparing the sensible entry commerce tiers:

  • Squarespace Core (US$23/mo, 0% platform fee): US$276/yr subscription + US$0 platform fees = US$276/yr before card processing
  • Shopify Basic with Shopify Payments (US$29/mo annual, 0% platform fee): US$348/yr + US$0 = US$348/yr before card processing
  • Shopify Basic with a third-party gateway (+2%): US$348 + US$2,400 = US$2,748/yr before card processing
  • Squarespace Basic (US$16/mo, 2% on physical products): US$192 + US$2,400 = US$2,592/yr before card processing

The verdict flips on one decision: which payment processor you use. On Shopify Payments vs Squarespace Core, the annual gap is US$72 — noise. Route around Shopify Payments (or sit on Squarespace Basic), and the platform fee adds ~US$2,400/year. As one video comparison puts it: "a lower subscription with a 2% fee can cost more than a higher subscription without one — run your own numbers."

Where the fees actually come from

Two different fees get conflated. The platform transaction fee is the platform's cut for the sale happening on its rails — avoidable on Shopify via Shopify Payments, avoidable on Squarespace from Core upward. The card processing fee (~2.9% + 30¢, lower on higher tiers) goes to the payment processor and applies on both platforms no matter what. At US$10k/month, card processing is ~US$3,500/year on either platform — bigger than every number in the table above. Nobody escapes the rail; the game is eliminating the platform's extra cut.

The plans around the fees

Fees aside, the tiers buy different capabilities. Shopify's edge is commerce depth: 8,000+ apps, multi-channel selling (Amazon, TikTok, Instagram), advanced inventory, and a real POS ecosystem. Squarespace's edge is the all-in-one: scheduling (Acuity), email campaigns, and memberships included natively — things Shopify needs third-party apps for — plus ~200 polished templates. Cybernews' side-by-side build test clocked ~40 minutes for Shopify and ~35 for Squarespace, and noted Squarespace's 14-day money-back guarantee on annual plans versus Shopify's 7-day trial with no refunds.

The Canadian seller angle

Both platforms serve Canadian sellers, but neither publishes Canada-specific fee tables in the sources checked — confirm CAD billing and the effective exchange rate at checkout, and verify GST/HST handling for your province before launch. Shopify's much larger app ecosystem is the practical edge for sellers with complex fulfillment.

What reviewers say

HostAdvice, after building real stores on both, concludes Shopify wins on ecommerce pricing at comparable volume — but their worked example used older plan numbers; re-run with the current tiers above. JPK Design reframes it as a business-model question, not a price question: service providers and creatives who also sell some products fit Squarespace; dedicated product businesses with large inventories and multi-channel needs fit Shopify. DecodeIQ adds the hard limits: Squarespace caps products at 3 variants each, and abandoned-cart recovery needs the top Advanced tier.

Decision: which store buys which

Buy Squarespace Core if your website's main job is presence — portfolio, services, content — with a shop on the side: US$23/month, zero platform fees, everything bundled. Buy Shopify Basic if the website IS the shop: use Shopify Payments to keep the platform fee at zero, and you get the commerce depth for US$72/year more than Core. Whatever you choose, the fee decision matters more than the subscription: pick the payment setup first, the plan second.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shopify charge transaction fees?

Only when you use a third-party payment gateway instead of Shopify Payments: 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow, 0.6% on Advanced, 0.2% on Plus. With Shopify Payments, the platform fee is 0% — card processing still applies.

Does Squarespace charge transaction fees?

On the Basic plan, 2% on physical product sales. Core, Plus, and Advanced charge 0% platform fees. Card processing fees apply on all plans.

Which is cheaper for a small store?

At US$10k/month with fee-free setups, Squarespace Core (US$276/yr) vs Shopify Basic annual (US$348/yr) — nearly identical. The expensive mistake is the wrong payment setup, not the wrong platform: a 2% platform fee at that volume costs ~US$2,400/year.
