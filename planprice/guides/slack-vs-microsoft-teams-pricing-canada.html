Slack vs Microsoft Teams Pricing in Canada: Per-User Costs and the Free-Tier Gap

Published 2026-10-11

Slack and Teams look close on a pricing page and far apart on an invoice. The published per-user rates tell maybe half the story: the free tier's 90-day history wall pushes teams to paid Slack, the AI add-on stacks $21–$30 per user per month onto Teams while Slack bundles it, and Slack bills Canadians in USD while Microsoft bills in CAD. Prices below are published list rates checked October 2026.

Microsoft's prices reflect the July 1, 2026 increase: Business Basic rose from $6 to $7, Business Standard from $12.50 to $14. Teams Essentials held at $4.00. The free-tier gap: 90 days vs unlimited

This is the quietest price in the comparison. Slack's free plan keeps only the last 90 days of messages searchable. For a team that lives in chat, the history wall arrives fast — and the fix is Pro at $7.25/user/month, not because the team needs Pro's features but because it needs its own conversations back. Teams' free tier offers unlimited chat history, so a cost-conscious team can sit on free far longer.

Price it: a 10-person team hitting the history wall pays $870 USD/year for Pro (about $1,244 CAD at October 2026 rates) largely to preserve search. If your team is choosing free tiers, this gap alone can decide the platform.

The AI multiplier: bundled vs add-on

2026 split the two vendors' AI strategies. Slack bundles AI — search, recaps, summaries — into every paid plan at no extra charge. Microsoft sells Copilot separately: $30/user/month at enterprise, with a Copilot Business promo at $18/month through December 2026, then $21.

Add it to the seat: M365 Business Standard ($14) + Copilot Business ($21) = $35/user/month for the AI-equipped Teams experience, versus Slack Business+ at $15 with AI included. If AI assistance is a requirement rather than a nice-to-have, Teams' list-price advantage reverses.

The CAD wrinkle: who bills you in what currency

Slack's Canadian pricing page lists in USD per user per month — Canadian buyers pay the USD price converted at their card's rate. At October 2026's ~1.43 rate, Pro's $7.25 becomes roughly $10.36 CAD per user per month before card FX markup; Business+ lands near $21.45 CAD. Microsoft lists in USD too on its US pages, but Canadian Microsoft 365 billing runs in CAD. Convert Slack to CAD before comparing — the sticker gap is smaller than it looks in USD.

FAQ

Is Microsoft Teams really cheaper than Slack?

At list price, yes at the entry tier — Teams Essentials is $4/user/month versus Slack Pro at $7.25. But the gap narrows once you add Microsoft Copilot ($21–$30/user/month) for AI features that Slack bundles into its paid plans, and it reverses if you're already paying for Microsoft 365.

What happens to Slack messages after 90 days on the free plan?

They fall out of searchable history. The free plan keeps only the last 90 days of messages searchable, which is the most common reason small teams end up upgrading to Pro — to keep their own conversation history.

Does Slack bill Canadians in CAD or USD?

USD. Slack's Canadian pricing page lists prices in USD per user per month, so a Canadian buyer pays the USD price converted at their card's rate — about 1.43 in October 2026, putting Pro near $10.36 CAD per user per month before card FX markup.
