SurveyMonkey vs Typeform Pricing: The Per-Response Cost Nobody Computes

Published 2026-10-11

Both vendors price around the same unit — responses — but they quote it in different units, which makes every tier table misleading. SurveyMonkey's paid plans advertise responses per year; Typeform advertises responses per month. Until you normalize that, you can't compare them. This guide normalizes everything to responses per month, computes the cost per response on every tier, and compares the two overage policies — using prices checked in October 2026.

The published prices and response limits, October 2026

Typeform (monthly billing list prices, captured October 1, 2026; annual billing roughly 30% cheaper): Free — $0, 10 responses/month. Basic — $39/month, 100 responses/month. Plus — $79/month, 1,000 responses/month. Business — $129/month, 10,000 responses/month. On annual billing the per-month equivalents run about $25 (Basic), $50 (Plus) and $83 (Business).

SurveyMonkey (individual plans): Basic — $0, 10 questions and 25 responses per survey. Standard Monthly — $99/month, 1,000 responses/month. Advantage Annual — $39/month billed annually ($468/year), 15,000 responses/year. Premier Annual — $139/month billed annually ($1,668/year), 40,000 responses/year. Team plans start at 3 users: Team Advantage $30/user/month, Team Premier $92/user/month, billed annually.

Normalizing the units: responses per month, both tools

Divide SurveyMonkey's annual allowances by 12: Advantage = 1,250 responses/month for $39/month; Premier = 3,333 responses/month for $139/month. Now the tiers line up: Typeform Basic (100/mo) vs nothing on SurveyMonkey's side at that volume; Typeform Plus (1,000/mo) vs SurveyMonkey Advantage (1,250/mo); Typeform Business (10,000/mo) vs SurveyMonkey Premier (3,333/mo) — where Typeform actually offers triple the response volume at a lower price.

The per-response cost table

  • Typeform Basic ($39/mo monthly): 100 responses → $0.39/response
  • Typeform Plus (~$50/mo annual): 1,000 responses → ~$0.050/response
  • Typeform Business (~$83/mo annual): 10,000 responses → ~$0.008/response
  • SurveyMonkey Standard Monthly ($99/mo): 1,000 responses → $0.099/response
  • SurveyMonkey Advantage ($39/mo annual): 1,250 responses → ~$0.031/response
  • SurveyMonkey Premier ($139/mo annual): 3,333 responses → ~$0.042/response

The pattern: SurveyMonkey wins the mid-volume value game (Advantage at ~$0.031/response is the cheapest way to collect ~1,000 responses a month), while Typeform's pricing punishes low volume brutally — $0.39 per response on Basic is the worst value on either price sheet. If you collect fewer than 100 responses a month, neither paid tier makes sense; that's Google Forms territory.

Overage policies: $0.15 a response vs a forced upgrade

This is the sleeper difference. SurveyMonkey charges $0.15 per response beyond your plan's limit — 200 extra responses cost $30, no plan change required. Typeform has no overage meter at all: exceed your cap and you must jump a full tier. Blow past Plus's 1,000 responses by 50 and your only option is Business at roughly double the price. If your response volume swings month to month — a viral survey, a seasonal campaign — SurveyMonkey's overage model is far more forgiving than Typeform's cliff.

Which buyer should pick which

Pick SurveyMonkey Advantage if you run steady research volume around 1,000 responses a month and want skip logic, exports and benchmarking at the lowest per-response cost. Pick Typeform if the conversational one-question-at-a-time experience measurably lifts your completion rate — but buy Plus or higher, because Basic's 100-response cap at $39/month is a trap. And if your volume is spiky, weight the overage policy as heavily as the sticker price.

A note on structure: this is a price-comparison explainer, not a repair procedure — there are no settings to change, only the per-response math to run before you buy.

FAQ

Which is cheaper per response, SurveyMonkey or Typeform?

At around 1,000 responses a month, SurveyMonkey Advantage (~$0.031/response) beats Typeform Plus (~$0.050/response on annual billing). At very low volume both are expensive per response — Typeform Basic costs about $0.39 per response.

What happens if you exceed your response limit?

SurveyMonkey bills $0.15 per extra response. Typeform's published pricing shows tier-based caps with no per-response overage rate listed, so exceeding a cap means moving to the next tier.

Why is comparing their pricing so confusing?

SurveyMonkey quotes paid-plan responses per year (15,000/year on Advantage) while Typeform quotes per month (1,000/month on Plus). Divide SurveyMonkey's annual figure by 12 before comparing.
