Tally vs Jotform Pricing: When the Submission Meter Costs More Than the Tool

Last updated 2026-10-11

These two form builders sell opposite things. Jotform sells volume — every paid tier is a bigger submission allowance. Tally sells polish — submissions are unlimited on every tier including free, and paid tiers buy branding removal, custom domains and team features. This guide computes what each submission actually costs at real volumes, using prices checked in October 2026.

The two pricing philosophies: meters vs flat

Jotform's free Starter plan caps you at 5 forms and 100 submissions per month. Every dollar you spend goes toward raising those meters. Tally's free plan includes unlimited forms, unlimited submissions and unlimited questions within a published fair-use policy — Tally's own comparison page frames it as "no $39+/mo upgrade required to remove limits." The philosophical split: Jotform assumes forms are a metered utility; Tally assumes the builder is the product and volume is free.

The published prices, October 2026

Tally: Free — $0 (unlimited forms/submissions, fair use). Pro — $24/month (branding removal, custom domains, team collaboration, advanced customization, enhanced analytics). Business — $74/month (everything in Pro plus email verification, data-retention control, extended version history).

Jotform (annual billing): Starter — $0 (5 forms, 100 submissions/month). Bronze — $34/month (25 forms, 1,000 submissions). Silver — $39/month (50 forms, 2,500 submissions). Gold — $99/month (100 forms, 10,000 submissions). Enterprise — custom (unlimited, multi-user).

Per-submission math at 500, 2,000 and 10,000 submissions

  • 500 submissions/month: Jotform needs Bronze at $34 → $0.068 per submission. Tally: $0 on the free plan.
  • 2,000 submissions/month: Jotform needs Silver at $39 → ~$0.020 per submission. Tally: $0 free, or $24 Pro for branding → $0.012 per submission.
  • 10,000 submissions/month: Jotform needs Gold at $99 → ~$0.010 per submission. Tally: $0 on the free plan.

At every volume, Tally's per-submission cost is zero — the meter simply doesn't exist. Jotform's per-submission cost falls as volume rises, but you're still paying $99/month at 10,000 submissions for something Tally gives away. In CAD at the October 2026 rate (~1.43), Jotform Gold is about $142 CAD/month; Tally Pro is about $34 CAD/month.

What paid actually buys on each side

On Tally, paying removes Tally's branding, adds your domain, unlocks team workspaces and (on Business) data-retention control and email verification. On Jotform, paying buys headroom: more forms and more submissions. Neither vendor's paid tier is "better" in the abstract — they solve different problems. If your complaint about Jotform is purely the quotas, Tally removes exactly that complaint. If your complaint is missing workflows, Tally won't help.

The compliance fork: HIPAA and regulated forms

This is where the price comparison stops mattering. Jotform offers HIPAA compliance on Gold and Enterprise tiers — the route for clinics and regulated workflows. Tally offers no HIPAA compliance, no SOC 2, no ISO 27001, and no SSO or 2FA at any tier, including the $74/month Business plan (it is GDPR-compliant and EU-hosted). If a security questionnaire governs your choice, Tally is disqualified regardless of price; if it doesn't, Jotform's compliance premium is money for nothing.

A note on structure: this is a price-comparison explainer, not a repair procedure — there are no settings to change, only the per-submission math to run before you buy.

FAQ

Is Tally really free?

Yes — unlimited forms, submissions and questions on the free plan within a fair-use policy. Pro ($24/month) and Business ($74/month) sell branding removal, custom domains, team features and data controls, not volume.

How many free submissions does Jotform allow?

100 per month across 5 forms on the free Starter plan. Bronze ($34/month annual) raises it to 1,000, Silver ($39) to 2,500, and Gold ($99) to 10,000.

Which is better for a business, Tally or Jotform?

Tally wins on cost at any volume. Jotform wins on breadth — 40+ payment processors versus Tally's Stripe-only, and HIPAA compliance on Gold/Enterprise, which Tally doesn't offer at any price.
