Toast POS Pricing in Canada: Hardware, Software Tiers, and the Processing Tie-In

Published 2026-10-11

Nearly every Toast pricing article quotes US tiers and calls it a day. Canadian restaurants face a different page: Starter at CA$120/month and Essentials at CA$220/month — software only, with hardware, add-ons, and mandatory flat-rate processing on top. This guide prices the real Canadian bill, and flags the one number Toast's Canadian page doesn't publish.

The processing rows on the Canadian page read "per transaction flat rate" — the actual rate is not published there.

The hardware bill: what a starter kit costs

Toast sells hardware as subscriptions and one-time purchases depending on the plan structure. On the US Traditional plan structure, the one-time hardware fee runs about $1,024.10 for a starter configuration, and the Starter Kit tier in the US bundles a hardware kit with no upfront costs for single-location restaurants needing 1–2 terminals. Canadian hardware pricing is quoted per terminal in the sales process — get the per-device figure in writing, because each additional handheld and terminal beyond the first adds to the monthly bill.

The processing tie-in: the rate behind the subscription

Here is the number that drives the real total: Toast's tiers are built around integrated flat-rate processing, and on the Canadian pricing page that rate is not published. On the US plan structures, the Traditional tiers pair subscriptions with 2.49% + 15¢ processing, while the $0/month pay-as-you-go structures run 3.09% to 3.69% + 15¢. The Canadian flat rate is set in your quote — and because it applies to every transaction for the life of a two-year contract, it deserves more scrutiny than the software tier. Ask for it explicitly, and model it against your monthly volume before comparing tiers.

Worked monthly totals (software side)

Essentials (CA$220) + digital ordering (~CA$102 at 1.36) + loyalty (~CA$68) ≈ CA$390/month before processing and hardware — and before the unpublished Canadian flat processing rate. A restaurant doing $60,000 a month in card volume should treat the processing line as the headline number, not the $220 tier. Finally: Toast requires a two-year contract for most plans, with early termination fees if you cancel. Read the termination clause before the feature list.

Decision framework: is Toast the right fit

  • Single-location restaurant wanting one throat to choke: Starter or Essentials — POS, payments, and support from one vendor, with the flat-rate processing priced into the deal.
  • Online-ordering-heavy: Essentials minimum — digital ordering is the tier's reason to exist.
  • Multi-location or full-platform: Custom — but negotiate the processing rate and the hardware-per-device cost first, because the two-year contract locks both in.

Note: this is a pricing explainer, not a procedure guide — no steps here, just the tier and add-on math to verify against your own quote before you sign.

FAQ

How much does Toast POS cost per month in Canada?

Toast's Canadian pricing page lists Starter at CA$120/month and Essentials at CA$220/month, with a custom quote tier above. That is software only — hardware, add-ons, and processing sit on top.

Does Toast require you to use their payment processing?

Yes — Toast's tiers are built around integrated flat-rate processing. The Canadian pricing page does not publish the flat processing rate, so get it in writing in your quote.

Is there a contract with Toast POS?
