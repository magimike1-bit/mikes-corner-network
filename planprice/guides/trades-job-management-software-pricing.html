Job Management Software Pricing for Trades: What Contractors Pay at 1, 5 & 10 Techs

Published 2026-10-12

Job management software for trades is sold on "from $29/month" pricing pages, but contractors buy at crew size — and the pricing model matters more than the sticker. This guide prices Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, and Workiz at 1, 5, and 10 technicians with October 2026 figures, adds the add-on menus and processing fees the tables skip, and gives you the hiring-trigger rule. PlanPrice also maintains a Jobber deal page with current pricing.

General information, not financial advice. Prices change — always confirm current rates on the vendor's own pricing page before buying.

The two pricing religions

Jobber and Housecall Pro price in tiers: a plan covers a user band, and adding a technician costs nothing until you cross a tier boundary — then it jumps. Jobber charges US$29/month for each extra user beyond a plan's cap; Housecall Pro's Teams plans cap at 5 and 8 users. ServiceTitan prices per technician: every hire is a permanent monthly increase, at contractor-reported rates of roughly US$245–500+/tech/month plus implementation.

The structural consequence: at small crew sizes the tiered vendors are an order of magnitude cheaper; the per-tech model compounds with every hire. Workiz sits with the tiered camp: Lite is free for 2 users, then Kickstart US$187/month, Standard US$229, Pro US$270 (annual billing), with extra users at US$46–54/month.

The add-on menu (where the bill doubles)

Jobber's headline tiers don't include the tools most contractors actually want: Marketing Suite US$99/month, Receptionist US$29/month, Pipeline US$49/month — sold separately below the Plus tier (verified September 2026). A realistic 10-person Jobber team lands at US$450–750/month before processing. Housecall Pro's marketing, GPS, and price-book bundles are similarly separate. Budget the add-ons, not the tier.

Processing: the line that dwarfs the subscription

Jobber charges 2.9% + US$0.30 per card payment (2.7% + US$0.30 tap-to-pay, 1% bank transfer). On a business doing US$800,000/year, processing at 2.9–3.9% is US$23,000–31,000 a year — which dwarfs the subscription on every platform on this page. Compare the processing rate before you compare the software price, and ask whether you can bring your own processor.

The hiring-trigger rule

Before you sign, map your next three hires against each vendor's tier boundaries: which hire pushes you over the 5-user or 8-user cap, and what does the next tier cost? On tiered plans, the 6th hire can be the most expensive hire of the year. On per-tech plans, every hire costs the same — which is only attractive once you're big enough that the tier jumps would cost more. Also check Jobber's one-year commitment option: it can't be cancelled or downgraded mid-term, with remaining installments still payable.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Jobber cost per month?

Solo: Core US$49/month (US$29 annual), Connect US$139 (US$99 annual), Grow US$199 (US$149 annual). Teams: 5 users from US$149/month; 10 users US$229–449/month depending on tier. Extra users US$29/month each; add-ons US$29–99/month.

Is ServiceTitan worth it for a small contractor?

Rarely under ~15 technicians: contractor-reported US$245–500+/tech/month plus US$10k+ implementation puts a 5-tech shop at US$24,700–33,000 in year one. It's built for larger operations that will use its reporting and marketing attribution depth.

Jobber vs Housecall Pro for a small crew?

Jobber wins on job costing and quoting tools; Housecall Pro wins on consumer booking and review automation. At 5 users they're close (Jobber Connect US$1,788/yr vs Housecall Pro Essentials US$3,588/yr on annual math) — pick by which workflow your crew will actually use.
