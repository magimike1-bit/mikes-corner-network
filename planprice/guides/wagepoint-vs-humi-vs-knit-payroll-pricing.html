Wagepoint vs Humi vs Knit: Canadian Payroll Pricing per Employee, Worked

Published 2026-10-11

Canadian payroll software mostly uses the same formula — a monthly base fee plus a per-employee charge — but the numbers differ enough to matter, and one of the three won't even publish its prices. Here's Wagepoint, Humi, and Knit compared in CAD, with worked totals at 5, 10, and 20 employees, so you can see what each one really costs at your headcount.

The three pricing models

Wagepoint (official fee schedule): Solo at $20/month base + $4 per employee — one payroll run and one pay group a month. Unlimited at $40 + $6 per employee — unlimited pay runs and pay groups. Both include direct deposit, tax calculations and CRA remittances, year-end reporting, T4s/T4As, and Records of Employment. Knit (published pricing): Lite at $39 + $4 per employee (full payroll plus most HR tools), Complete at $39 + $6 per employee (full payroll plus the complete HR suite), and Concierge at $199 + $15 per employee (Knit runs payroll and HR for you) — all with unlimited pay runs. Humi: no published pricing — custom quote only, per-employee monthly billing in CAD.

Side-by-side at 5, 10, and 20 employees

Monthly totals at regular rates, before tax:

  • 5 employees: Wagepoint Solo $40 · Wagepoint Unlimited $70 · Knit Complete $69 · Humi ~$30–35 (reported, unconfirmed)
  • 10 employees: Wagepoint Solo $60 · Wagepoint Unlimited $100 · Knit Complete $99 · Humi ~$60–70 (reported, unconfirmed)
  • 20 employees: Wagepoint Solo $100 · Wagepoint Unlimited $180 · Knit Complete $159 · Humi ~$120–140 (reported, unconfirmed)

Two patterns emerge. First, Wagepoint Solo is the cheapest published option at every headcount — if you run one payroll a month, nothing beats $20 + $4/employee. Second, Wagepoint Unlimited and Knit Complete are priced almost identically ($40+$6 vs $39+$6) — the decision between them is about product, not price: Knit bundles HR tools (time off, onboarding, e-signing), Wagepoint is the deeper pure-payroll product.

What year-end includes (and what it costs extra)

A cheap base fee that bills extra for year-end isn't cheap. Wagepoint includes year-end reporting, T4s/T4As, and ROEs on both plans — no year-end upsell. Knit includes CRA remittance and T4/T4A generation in its published plans, with unlimited pay runs (off-cycle corrections and bonuses cost nothing extra — a real advantage for businesses that run corrections often). For Humi, confirm year-end handling in the written quote: it's the line item most likely to differ from the headline per-employee rate. Whatever you choose, get the year-end scope in writing before you sign — January is the wrong time to discover an extra fee.

The Humi quote problem

Humi's pricing is custom-quote only, which makes it the hardest of the three to evaluate. Third-party directories report roughly $6–$7 per employee per month in CAD, with plan minimums around $70/month for payroll — but those are reported figures, not published rates, and your quote will depend on headcount and modules. The practical move: get Humi's written quote first, then compare it against the published Wagepoint and Knit totals above at your exact headcount. Don't compare a quote's headline per-employee rate against a competitor's base fee — compare the monthly totals, with year-end included, in writing.

Which payroll for which business

One payroll a month, payroll only → Wagepoint Solo ($20 + $4/employee). The cheapest honest payroll in Canada. Multiple pay runs or pay groups → Wagepoint Unlimited ($40 + $6). Payroll plus HR in one system → Knit Complete ($39 + $6). The HR bundle (onboarding, time off, documents) replaces a second subscription. HR-led organization that wants one platform → Humi. Get the quote, verify year-end scope, and compare totals — Humi's strength is breadth (recruiting, performance, benefits alongside payroll), not price.

A note on format: this is a pricing comparison, not a how-to — there are no step-by-step actions because the question it answers is which plan fits your business, and that is what the tables above are for.

FAQ

How much does Wagepoint cost per employee in Canada?

Solo: $20/month base + $4 per employee (one payroll run a month). Unlimited: $40/month base + $6 per employee (unlimited runs). Year-end reporting, T4s, ROEs, and CRA remittances are included on both plans.

How much does Humi payroll cost?

Humi publishes no standard rates — pricing is by custom quote only. Third-party directories report roughly $6–$7 per employee per month in CAD, with payroll minimums around $70/month. Get a written quote before comparing.

Is Knit payroll cheaper than Wagepoint?

At small headcounts they're close: Knit Complete ($39 + $6/employee) vs Wagepoint Unlimited ($40 + $6/employee) cost nearly the same, but Knit bundles HR tools while Wagepoint is payroll-focused. Wagepoint Solo ($20 + $4) wins for single-payroll businesses.
