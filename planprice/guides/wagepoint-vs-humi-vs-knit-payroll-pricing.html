Wagepoint vs Humi vs Knit: Canadian Payroll Pricing per Employee, Worked

Published 2026-10-11 · By Mike, Editor

Canadian payroll software mostly uses the same formula, a monthly base fee plus a per-employee charge, but the numbers differ enough to matter, and the minimums and bundles differ as much as the rates. Here's Wagepoint, Humi, and Knit compared in CAD, with worked totals at 5, 10, and 20 employees, so you can see what each one really costs at your headcount.

The three pricing models

Wagepoint (official Wagepoint 2.0 Service Fee Schedule, last updated 2026-10-22): Lite at C$20/month base + C$4 per employee, one payroll run per month. Core at C$40 + C$6 per employee, unlimited pay runs. Plus at C$80 + C$10 per employee, unlimited pay runs, plus clock in and out, geo-fencing, paid time off management, and priority support. All three plans include direct deposit, tax calculations and CRA remittances, year-end reporting, T4s/T4As, and Records of Employment. Knit (published pricing): Lite at C$40 + C$6 per employee (full payroll plus lite HR tools: time off, documents, e-signing), Complete at C$50 + C$8 per employee (full payroll plus the complete HR suite), and Concierge at contact sales pricing (Knit runs payroll and HR for you; pricing is not currently published), all with unlimited pay runs. Humi (published payroll pricing): Payroll at CAD 7 per employee per month with a CAD 70 monthly minimum, Managed Payroll at CAD 30 per employee per month with a CAD 500 monthly minimum, and the core HR platform priced by quote.

Side-by-side at 5, 10, and 20 employees

Monthly totals at regular rates, before tax:

  • 5 employees: Wagepoint Lite C$40 · Wagepoint Core C$70 · Wagepoint Plus C$130 · Knit Complete C$90 · Humi Payroll C$70 (the CAD 70 minimum applies)
  • 10 employees: Wagepoint Lite C$60 · Wagepoint Core C$100 · Wagepoint Plus C$180 · Knit Complete C$130 · Humi Payroll C$70
  • 20 employees: Wagepoint Lite C$100 · Wagepoint Core C$160 · Wagepoint Plus C$280 · Knit Complete C$210 · Humi Payroll C$140

Two patterns emerge. First, Wagepoint Lite is the cheapest published option at every headcount: if you run one payroll a month, nothing beats C$20 + C$4/employee. Second, Knit Complete now costs clearly more than Wagepoint Core (C$50 + C$8 versus C$40 + C$6), C$30 a month more at 10 employees and C$50 a month more at 20, so the decision between them is about the bundle, not a tie on price: Knit bundles HR tools (time off, onboarding, e-signing), Wagepoint is the deeper pure payroll product.

What year-end includes (and what it costs extra)

A cheap base fee that bills extra for year-end isn't cheap. Wagepoint includes year-end reporting, T4s/T4As, and ROEs on all three plans, no year-end upsell. Knit includes CRA remittance and T4/T4A generation in its published plans, with unlimited pay runs (off-cycle corrections and bonuses cost nothing extra, a real advantage for businesses that run corrections often). For Humi, confirm year-end handling in writing: it's the line item most likely to differ from the headline per-employee rate. Whatever you choose, get the year-end scope in writing before you sign. January is the wrong time to discover an extra fee.

Humi: Published Payroll Rates and a Quote Priced HR Platform

Humi publishes its payroll rates, which makes it easier to evaluate than it used to be: CAD 7 per employee per month for Payroll and CAD 30 per employee per month for Managed Payroll. The catch is the minimums. Payroll carries a CAD 70 monthly minimum, so a five person firm pays CAD 70, not CAD 35, and Managed Payroll carries a CAD 500 monthly minimum, which binds below about 17 employees. What Humi does not publish is the core HR platform price, which is quote priced and depends on headcount and modules. The practical move: price Humi Payroll from the published rates, get the HR platform quote in writing, then compare the combined monthly total against the published Wagepoint and Knit totals above at your exact headcount, with year-end included.

Which payroll for which business

One payroll a month, payroll only → Wagepoint Lite (C$20 + C$4/employee). The cheapest honest payroll in Canada. Multiple pay runs → Wagepoint Core (C$40 + C$6). Pay runs plus time tracking and priority support → Wagepoint Plus (C$80 + C$10). Payroll plus HR in one system → Knit Complete (C$50 + C$8). The HR bundle (onboarding, time off, documents) replaces a second subscription. HR-led organization that wants one platform → Humi. Payroll is published at CAD 7 per employee with a CAD 70 minimum; get the HR platform quote, verify year-end scope, and compare totals. Humi's strength is breadth (recruiting, performance, benefits alongside payroll), not price.

FAQ

How much does Wagepoint cost per employee in Canada?

Lite: C$20/month base + C$4 per employee (one payroll run a month). Core: C$40/month base + C$6 per employee (unlimited runs). Plus: C$80/month base + C$10 per employee (unlimited runs, plus clock in and out, geo-fencing, paid time off management, and priority support). Year-end reporting, T4s, ROEs, and CRA remittances are included on all plans.

How much does Humi payroll cost?

Humi Payroll is CAD 7 per employee per month with a CAD 70 monthly minimum. Managed Payroll is CAD 30 per employee per month with a CAD 500 monthly minimum. The core HR platform is quote priced, so get that part in writing before comparing.

Is Knit payroll cheaper than Wagepoint?

Not on list price: Knit Complete (C$50 + C$8/employee) costs more than Wagepoint Core (C$40 + C$6/employee), C$130 a month versus C$100 at 10 employees, but Knit bundles HR tools while Wagepoint is payroll-focused. Wagepoint Lite (C$20 + C$4) is the cheapest published option for single-payroll businesses.
