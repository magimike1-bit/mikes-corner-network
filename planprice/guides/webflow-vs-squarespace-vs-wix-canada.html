Webflow vs Squarespace vs Wix in Canada: 3-Year TCO Compared

Published 2026-10-11

"0% transaction fees" is the line all three website builders advertise in 2026 — and it's the line that makes their pricing pages useless for comparison. The platform fee depends on the plan tier, not the platform, and the real cost of a builder includes the price of leaving it. This guide computes a Canadian 3-year total cost of ownership: published plan prices in CAD, the transaction-fee math on a real revenue figure, and the template-lock-in cost priced in.

The divergence starts with transaction fees.

The transaction-fee math nobody does

The "0% platform fees" claim is tier-conditional. The published breakdown: Webflow charges 2% on its Standard e-commerce plan and 0% on Plus/Advanced; Squarespace charges 3% on the Business plan and 0% on Commerce plans; Wix charges 0% via Wix Payments. On top of every platform fee sit the card-processor fees (about 2.9% + 30¢ via Stripe/PayPal), which apply everywhere and cancel out of the comparison.

At $60k/year in sales, Webflow's entry e-commerce plan costs $1,200/year in platform fees alone — more than double its own subscription. This is the number that never appears on the pricing page: the fee scales with your revenue, so the "cheapest" plan gets more expensive exactly as your business succeeds. Upgrading to Webflow's 0%-fee Plus tier removes the percentage but raises the subscription — do that math before revenue forces it on you.

The cost of leaving (template lock-in)

The third cost is the exit. Webflow lets you export full HTML/CSS/JS — you can take your site elsewhere. Squarespace offers no code export, and Wix's portability is similarly limited: leaving either means rebuilding from scratch, a cost one 2026 guide flags as the "migration cost if you outgrow the platform." A 2026 Toronto agency pricing guide puts the DIY/template rebuild tier at $1,500–$5,000 CAD all-in, before you count redirect mapping, SEO recovery, and the weeks of your time.

Price the lock-in the way you'd price insurance: multiply the rebuild cost by your probability of outgrowing the platform in 3 years. A brochure site that will never need custom functionality can treat the lock-in cost as near zero. A store planning custom checkout flows, memberships, or deep integrations should budget the full rebuild — because "we'll migrate later" on a no-export platform means paying the rebuild and the years of platform fees in the meantime.

FAQ

Which is cheapest for selling online: Webflow, Squarespace, or Wix?

On plan price plus platform transaction fees, Wix and Squarespace entry e-commerce plans (from $27/month, 0% platform fee) undercut Webflow's entry e-commerce ($42/month with a 2% platform fee on the Standard tier). The gap widens with revenue because Webflow's 2% scales with sales.

Can you export a Squarespace or Wix site?

Webflow exports full HTML/CSS/JS. Squarespace offers no code export — leaving means rebuilding. Wix similarly keeps you in its ecosystem. Price a rebuild ($1,500–$5,000 CAD per a 2026 Toronto agency guide) before you commit.

Do website builders charge transaction fees in 2026?

It depends on the plan tier. Webflow charges 2% on its Standard e-commerce plan and 0% on Plus/Advanced; Squarespace charges 3% on the Business plan and 0% on Commerce plans; Wix charges 0% via Wix Payments. Card-processor fees (about 2.9% + 30¢) apply on top everywhere.
