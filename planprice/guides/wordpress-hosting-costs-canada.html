WordPress Hosting Costs in Canada: The Renewal-Price Jump and Real 3-Year TCO

Last updated 2026-10-11

WordPress hosting is advertised at $2–$3 a month and billed at $11–$18 a month. The gap between those two numbers is the entire business model of budget hosting: the intro rate is a loss-leader for the first term, and the renewal price — the one you'll pay for years — hides in the fine print. This guide shows both prices for the main options, converts them to CAD, and computes the honest 3-year total.

Shared vs managed — what the price gap buys

Shared WordPress hosting is a tuned shared plan with WordPress pre-installed: cheap, fine for most sites. Managed WordPress hosting takes over updates, backups, staging, and security at several times the price. The rule of thumb from Canadian hosting guides: buy managed when downtime or a hack would cost you real money; not before.

The features that actually change daily life: server-level caching (LiteSpeed or a custom stack), automatic core updates, a staging environment for testing changes safely, and one-click installs. Nightly backups are non-negotiable — check they're included, not an upsell.

The CAD problem (only some hosts bill in CAD)

Of the hosts in 2026 Canadian comparisons, only WHC.ca bills in CAD. Everyone else bills in USD, which means a soft loonie quietly raises your effective price at every renewal — a $17.99 USD renewal was about $25.66 CAD at the 2026-10-11 mid-market rate (1 USD = 1.42616 CAD). Your card's exchange rate will be worse than mid-market, so treat every USD figure in this guide as a floor, not a quote.

Canadian-published CAD figures from one 2026 guide: Hostinger roughly C$4/month entry and C$9 at renewal; SiteGround roughly C$5 entry and C$19 at renewal; Cloudways roughly C$18 flat; Kinsta roughly C$47 flat. If Canadian data residency matters to you — Montreal or Toronto servers keep PIPEDA and provincial privacy rules simple — GreenGeeks, WHC.ca, and HostUpon run Canadian infrastructure, and Cloudways can deploy to Toronto.

FAQ

Why did my WordPress hosting renewal price jump?

Introductory rates are loss-leaders for the first term (usually 12 months). Renewal jumps of 2–4x are normal in shared hosting; SiteGround's StartUp plan jumps 6x, from $2.99 to $17.99/month. The renewal price is the real price — always check year two before signing.

Is managed WordPress hosting worth it over shared?

Managed hosting ($20–$100/month) buys flat renewal pricing, staging, nightly backups, and WordPress-specialist support. It's worth it when downtime or a hack would cost you real money; for a brochure site, good shared hosting with caching is fine.
