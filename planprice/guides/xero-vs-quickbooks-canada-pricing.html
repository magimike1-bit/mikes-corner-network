Xero vs QuickBooks Online in Canada: CAD Prices, Payroll Math, and the Switching Cost

Last updated 2026-10-11

Every Xero-vs-QuickBooks comparison seems to quote a different currency. Here's the Canadian version, in Canadian dollars, with the two things comparisons skip: the payroll add-on math (Xero has no native Canadian payroll) and the switching cost of actually moving. The cheapest subscription is not always the cheapest decision.

The CAD ladders side by side

Xero Canada: Starter $25/month (quotes, 20 invoices and 5 bills a month), Standard $60/month (unlimited invoices, bills, and reconciliations), Premium $80/month (adds multi-currency, 180-day cash flow forecast, and KPI benchmarks). Every Xero plan includes unlimited users and Hubdoc receipt capture; Expenses and Projects are add-ons on all plans ($5 and $10/month). Prices exclude GST/HST.

QuickBooks Online Canada: EasyStart from $24/month (1 user), Essentials from $45 (up to 3 users), Plus from $85 (up to 5 users), Advanced from $200 (up to 25 users). At the comparable mid tier — Xero Standard vs QuickBooks Plus — Xero is $25/month cheaper on subscription. But subscriptions are only the first line of the bill.

The user-count asymmetry

This is Xero's structural advantage: unlimited users on every plan, including the $25 Starter. Your accountant, your bookkeeper, and three staff all log in free. QuickBooks charges for users by tier — 1 on EasyStart, 3 on Essentials, 5 on Plus — and the 4th user forces a $40/month jump to Plus, the 6th a $115/month jump to Advanced. A five-person business that grows to six pays QuickBooks $1,380 more per year for one login; on Xero the sixth login costs nothing.

The asymmetry flips at the bottom: a true solo operator pays $24 for QuickBooks EasyStart vs $25 for Xero Starter — essentially tied — while QuickBooks' interface and phone support are widely considered friendlier for non-accountants. Don't pay for unlimited users you won't use.

Payroll: the add-on that decides it

QuickBooks offers native payroll as an add-on. Xero does not offer Canadian payroll at all — you pair it with a third party. The standard pairing is Wagepoint: Solo at $20 base + $4 per employee per month (one payroll run a month), or Unlimited at $40 + $6 per employee (unlimited runs), with direct deposit, CRA remittances, T4s, and Records of Employment included on both plans.

For a 5-employee business: Xero Standard ($60) + Wagepoint Unlimited ($40 + 5×$6 = $70) = $130/month all-in, before GST/HST. Against QuickBooks Plus ($85) plus its native payroll add-on, the gap narrows or reverses depending on payroll pricing — which is exactly why subscription-only comparisons mislead. Always price the stack: accounting + payroll + the add-ons you need (Xero Expenses/Projects at $5/$10 if you need claims or job costing).

The switching cost nobody models

Moving accounting systems is a project, not a signup. Budget for: data migration and cleanup (chart of accounts mapping, historical invoices, open bills — usually with accountant hours attached); re-learning (workflows, keyboard habits, report locations — plan two to four weeks of reduced speed); app re-integration (every connected app — Shopify, Stripe, payroll, time tracking — must be reconnected and re-tested); the payroll move (switching payroll mid-year means T4 continuity planning with your provider); and parallel running (most accountants recommend 1–2 months of dual entry to validate the new books).

Rule of thumb: if the annual subscription savings are smaller than one month of your bookkeeper's time, switching loses money in year one. Switch for capability or growth fit — the user math, the multi-currency need, the ecosystem — not for $25/month.

FAQ

Is Xero cheaper than QuickBooks Online in Canada?

At the mid tier, yes: Xero Standard is $60 CAD/month with unlimited users versus QuickBooks Plus at $85 CAD/month for up to 5 users. But payroll changes the math — compare all-in stacks, not subscriptions.

Does Xero include payroll in Canada?

No. Xero has no native Canadian payroll — most businesses pair it with a third party like Wagepoint ($20 base + $4 per employee per month on Solo, or $40 + $6 on Unlimited), which adds $40–$70/month for a small team.

What does it cost to switch from QuickBooks to Xero?

Beyond the subscription: data migration and cleanup, re-learning, re-connecting app integrations, moving payroll providers, and usually 1–2 months of parallel running. Budget the migration as a project, not a signup.
