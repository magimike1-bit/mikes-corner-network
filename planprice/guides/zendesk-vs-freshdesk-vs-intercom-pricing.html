Zendesk vs Freshdesk vs Intercom Pricing: Per-Agent Math and the Per-Resolution Twist

Last updated 2026-10-11

Helpdesk pricing has two meters now: the per-agent seat and the per-resolution AI charge. Compare only the seats and you'll misprice the decision by 2–7x — Zendesk's $55 sticker can pass $150 fully loaded, and Intercom's $0.99 per AI resolution can outrun its seat fees entirely. Prices below are published list rates (USD, annual billing) checked October 2026.

Zendesk also sells a standalone Support Team plan from $19/agent/month for email-only ticketing — the $55 figure is the Suite, which bundles chat and voice. Freshdesk's free tier for 2 agents makes it the cheapest starting point of the three.

The per-resolution twist: what the meter adds

Intercom's Fin AI agent bills about $0.99 per resolution — each conversation the AI closes end-to-end. That's on top of seat fees. At 500 AI-resolved conversations a month, the meter adds ~$495 — more than sixteen Essential seats. Zendesk meters AI resolutions too, at roughly $1.50–$2.00 each. Freshdesk's Freddy bills per session, win or lose.

The twist: per-resolution pricing rewards deflection. If the AI resolves 50% of tickets, the meter is your second-biggest line item; if it resolves 10%, it's noise. Your resolution rate matters more than the per-unit price.

FAQ

How does Intercom's per-resolution pricing work?

Intercom charges per seat for the platform (from $29/seat/month) and then bills its Fin AI agent about $0.99 for each conversation it resolves. The meter runs on top of seats, so a high AI-resolution rate can exceed the seat cost.

Why is Zendesk more expensive than the sticker price?

The add-on stack. A $55 Suite Team seat becomes $105 with the $50 Copilot add-on, and AI resolutions are metered on top at roughly $1.50–$2.00 each. Fully loaded, real-world Zendesk seats run well past $150.

Does Freshdesk have a free plan?

Yes — Freshdesk offers a free tier for up to 2 agents. Zendesk offers only a 14-day trial and Intercom offers a trial of Fin, so Freshdesk is the cheapest way to start.
