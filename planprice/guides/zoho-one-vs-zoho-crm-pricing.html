Zoho One vs Zoho CRM Plus: The Per-Employee Crossover Math

Published 2026-10-11

Zoho One ($37 per employee per month) looks cheaper than Zoho CRM Plus ($57 per user per month) — until you notice the unit. One is priced per employee and the other per user, and Zoho One's headline price requires licensing every employee on payroll. This guide works the crossover arithmetic so you can see exactly when the bundle wins.

The crossover table: when Zoho One beats CRM Plus

For a company of E employees where n of them need CRM-grade apps: Zoho One costs $37 × E per month; CRM Plus costs $57 × n per month. The bundle wins when n/E exceeds 37/57 — about 65%. Worked for a 25-person company:

So: if only your sales, marketing, and support teams need Zoho, buy CRM Plus for that subset. If most of the company will use Zoho apps — email, docs, books, people — the all-employee bundle wins, and you get the finance and HR stack effectively free.

The Flexible User pricing trap

Zoho offers a third option most comparisons skip: Flexible User pricing — Zoho One for a subset of users at $67/user/month billed annually ($90 on monthly billing). For that 25-person company with 12 CRM users, Flexible costs $804/month — cheaper than all-employee's $925 but still more than CRM Plus's $684. The trap: Flexible looks like a middle ground, but it is the most expensive per-user option Zoho sells. It only makes sense when a subset of employees needs more than the 14 apps in CRM Plus (e.g., Books and People too) while the rest of the company needs nothing.

What CRM Plus depth gets you that One doesn't

Price is not the whole story. The CRM features inside CRM Plus are deeper than what ships inside Zoho One: more automation triggers, better AI scoring through Zia, and tighter cross-channel visibility across sales, marketing, and support. CRM Plus also layers in SalesIQ (real-time visitor tracking) and Motivator (sales gamification) natively connected to the pipeline. For a sales-led startup or agency, that depth pays off — and Zoho One's broader setup takes longer to roll out well.

Decision framework: three questions

  1. What share of employees needs Zoho apps? Above ~65%, price Zoho One all-employee. Below it, price CRM Plus for the subset.
  2. Do non-CRM staff need Books, People, or Projects? If yes, the bundle's finance/HR stack tips the scale toward One even below the crossover.
  3. How deep does the CRM layer need to be? If the answer is "very" — advanced automation, Zia scoring, cross-channel customer view — CRM Plus earns its per-seat premium.

Note: this is a pricing explainer, not a procedure guide — no steps here, just the crossover math to run against your headcount before you sign.

FAQ

At what point does Zoho One become cheaper than Zoho CRM Plus?

When more than about 65% of your employees need CRM-grade apps. Below that share, CRM Plus for the subset is usually cheaper than licensing every employee on Zoho One.

Does Zoho One really require a licence for every employee?

Yes, on the All Employee plan — Zoho's pricing page requires licences for all employees on payroll. Flexible User pricing covers a subset but costs $67/user/month on annual billing, the most expensive per-user option.

Is the CRM inside Zoho One the same as the one in CRM Plus?

Mostly — Zoho One includes enterprise-level Zoho CRM. But CRM Plus adds depth in the customer-experience layer: more automation triggers, deeper Zia AI scoring, and tighter cross-channel visibility.
