Zoom vs Google Meet Pricing in Canada: Per-User Costs and the Webinar Add-On

Last updated 2026-10-11

Most Zoom-vs-Meet comparisons stop at the meeting-plan sticker and call it a day. That's where the expensive surprises live: the webinar add-on that can double the invoice, the USD list prices that land on a Canadian card in CAD, and the bundling question — are you already paying for Workspace? — that settles most comparisons before price does. This guide prices all three, with numbers checked October 2026.

Two footnotes matter. First, Zoom's Business plan carries a 10-license minimum — a team of 8 buys 10 licenses, so the step from Pro to Business is bigger than the per-seat numbers suggest. Second, Google's annual-commitment pricing saves about 16% versus monthly, and every Workspace tier includes Gmail, Drive, Docs, and Chat alongside Meet — you're not buying a meeting tool, you're buying the suite.

The add-on that doubles the bill: webinar math

Here is the line item most comparisons skip. Neither Pro nor Business includes webinars. Zoom's Webinar add-on lists at $79/month per license ($690/year) for up to 500 attendees — and it requires a paid Zoom Workplace plan underneath, roughly $149.90/year for Pro. Year-one webinar cost: about $840 USD before exchange, for a single host license. Need 1,000 attendees? That's $340/month. Branded webinar sessions run $3,490/year for 500 attendees.

Google Workspace has no equivalent webinar product at these tiers — large meetings top out at 500 participants on Plus with attendance tracking, but that's a meeting, not a webinar with registration and branding. If webinars are core to your marketing, price the add-on before you compare the meeting plans: it can exceed the cost of the seats themselves.

The Canadian billing wrinkle: USD list, CAD invoice

Zoom's prices are USD list. A Canadian buyer pays the USD amount converted at their card's rate. At the October 2026 mid-market rate of about 1.43, Zoom Pro at $13.33 USD lands near $19.06 CAD per seat per month — before any card FX markup, which typically adds another 2.5%. The $79 webinar add-on converts to roughly $113 CAD monthly.

Google Workspace Canada, by contrast, lists directly in CAD on its Canadian page — $9.20 to $28.70 per user per month on annual commitment, taxes extra. When you compare, convert Zoom to CAD first; comparing USD Zoom against CAD Workspace flatters Zoom by roughly 40%.

The bundling question: already on Workspace or buying standalone?

This question decides most comparisons before the price table matters. If your team already pays for Google Workspace for email and storage, premium Meet's marginal cost is $0 — longer meetings, and recording from Standard up, ride along with the suite you're already buying. Adding Zoom Pro on top means paying twice for overlapping capability.

The reverse is also true: if you're on Microsoft 365 for email and want best-in-class meetings without switching suites, Zoom is a standalone purchase that doesn't require changing anything else. Price the decision as: (cost of the suite you already pay) + (marginal cost of the meeting tool) — not as two standalone products.

Decision framework: which one, at your headcount

  • Under 10 people, already on Workspace: Meet wins on price — marginal cost zero, no new vendor, no FX surprise.
  • Under 10 people, not on Workspace: compare Zoom Pro (~$19 CAD/seat) against Workspace Starter ($9.20 CAD/seat) — but remember Starter buys you email and storage too.
  • 10–50 people needing webinars: add ~$840 USD/year per webinar host to Zoom's total before comparing; this is where Meet-plus-a-dedicated-webinar-tool sometimes beats Zoom-plus-add-on.
  • 50+ people: both go custom — negotiate, and get the FX and add-on terms in writing.

Why no step-by-step here: this is a buying-decision guide, not a setup walkthrough — the decision framework above replaces procedural steps.

FAQ

Is Google Meet really free with Google Workspace?

Yes, at the margin. Meet is included in every paid Google Workspace tier, so a team already paying for Workspace email and storage gets premium Meet — longer meetings, recording on Standard and up — for no additional per-user fee.

How much does a Zoom webinar actually cost per year?

The 500-attendee Webinar add-on lists at $79/month per license ($690/year), and it requires a paid Zoom Workplace plan underneath (about $149.90/year for Pro). Budget roughly $840 USD in year one before exchange.

Are Zoom prices in CAD or USD for Canadian buyers?

Zoom lists in USD. A Canadian buyer pays the USD price converted at their card's rate — at the October 2026 rate of about 1.43, a $13.33 USD seat lands near $19.06 CAD before any card FX markup. Google Workspace Canada lists directly in CAD.
