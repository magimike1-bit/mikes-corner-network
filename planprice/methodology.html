How PlanPrice ranks and verifies

How listings are ordered

  • Order is never sold. No vendor can pay to appear, to rank higher, or to be featured on PlanPrice. There are no sponsored placements anywhere on this site.
  • Default ordering is our editorial featured order. Category and directory pages present tools in the order our research team features them. Every listing shows its verified starting price, or is marked Custom where the vendor publishes no list price, and every listing shows what the tool is best for. The deals directory can be re-sorted by price. Alternatives tables on compare pages are ordered by aggregate review score.
  • Affiliate status is labelled per listing. No affiliate links are live today, so no listing earns PlanPrice a commission. When that changes, each affected listing will say so. See the affiliate disclosure.
  • Review scores are aggregate and attributed. Where we show G2 or Capterra scores, we show the average, the review count, and the date we checked them. We do not adjust scores.

About our data

  • Every price comes from the vendor's own published pricing. A person on our team reads the vendor's pricing page and records the price and the date. Vendor-submitted list prices and press releases are not sources.
  • Checks are dated, per product. Deal pages show when each price was last verified, and the verification log keeps the full record. A monthly sweep re-checks the catalogue; the registry date for the current catalogue is 2026-10-25.
  • Quote-only pricing is flagged, not guessed. When a vendor does not publish a price, our tables say so. We never estimate a hidden price.
  • Changes are tracked in the open. Our price changes page logs starting-price and promotion changes between sweeps, with dates, and every deal page carries its own price history table.
  • Corrections are welcome. If a price here does not match the vendor's page, tell us through the contact page and we will re-check it.

How other directories rank

On Capterra, vendors pay referral fees for sponsored profiles, and Capterra's own transparency page says sponsored profiles may appear first on its product listings, identified by their link-out buttons. On G2, vendors can buy advertising in sponsored positions on category and competitor pages; G2 says paying does not change its organic ranking or Grid scores. We publish those mechanics because buyers deserve to know how any directory they use makes its ordering decisions, including ours.
