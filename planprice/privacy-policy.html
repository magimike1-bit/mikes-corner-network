Advertising And Cookies

We've signed up for Google AdSense and placed the account-verification code on our pages, but ads aren't showing yet, they start only if Google approves this site. When ads show on this site, third-party vendors, including Google, will use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to visitors based on their visits to this site and to other sites on the internet. One of the cookies Google uses for this purpose is the DoubleClick cookie, also known as the DART cookie. The DART cookie is used by Google in the ads served on the websites of its partners, such as websites displaying AdSense ads, and Google uses information about visits to this and other websites, not including name, address, email address, or telephone number, to serve ads about goods and services of interest to visitors.

Third-party ad servers and ad networks use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript, and web beacons in the advertisements and links that appear on this site, and those technologies are sent directly to a visitor's browser. The third-party servers automatically receive the visitor's IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and to personalise the advertising content a visitor sees on this and other websites. PlanPrice has no access to or control over the cookies and web beacons used by third-party advertisers.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Visitors may opt out of the use of the DART cookie, and of Google's personalised advertising, by visiting Google Ads Settings at https://www.google.com/settings/ads. Visitors may also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting https://www.aboutads.info, and may opt out of cookies used by members of the Network Advertising Initiative at https://optout.networkadvertising.org. Visitors can also disable cookies entirely in their own browser settings, although some parts of other websites may not work correctly without them.

Other third-party vendors or ad networks may also use cookies to serve ads on this site. When any such vendor or network serves ads here, its use of cookies is governed by its own privacy policy, and visitors can opt out of its personalised advertising through the routes above and through the vendor's own website.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland will see a consent message from Google's certified consent management platform before personalized ads can show.

We do not sell personal information because we do not collect any. If you contact us about the site, we use your message only to respond.
