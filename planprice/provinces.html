Software & SaaS Taxes by Province: GST, HST, PST and QST

Published 2026-10-25 · Last updated 2026-10-25

The sticker price of a subscription is not its cost — the tax layer on top differs by province, and so does whether a registered business can recover it. These guides explain, per province, how GST/HST/PST/QST applies to the SaaS and service categories PlanPrice tracks, with CRA or provincial sources and check dates.

  • Ontario — 13% HST
  • Quebec — 5% GST + 9.975% QST
  • British Columbia — 5% GST + 7% PST
  • Alberta — 5% GST only
  • Atlantic Canada — 15% HST, Nova Scotia 14%
