CRM Migration Cost Estimator

The licence is the easy part of switching CRM. This estimator prices the work: cleaning the data, running test imports, validating, training the team, and paying for two systems while you parallel run.

Tool data comes from the deal pages. Prices were last checked in the full sweep dated 2026-09-30, with spot checks logged since then in the verification log.

How the estimate is built

Every rate below is a PlanPrice editorial planning figure, a fixture for budgeting, not a vendor quote and not any vendor's published price. Vendors do not publish migration rates; anyone quoting you an exact migration price without seeing your data is guessing. The fixtures:

  • Data cleanup: 1 hour per 250 records (dedupe, field fixes, format normalization), scaled by the complexity you pick.
  • Test imports and the real run: 1 hour per 1,000 records for mapping, test imports, the production run, and spot checks, scaled by complexity.
  • Validation and fixes: half an hour per 250 records after import, comparing counts and samples against the old system, scaled by complexity.
  • Training: 2 hours per person, a workshop plus first week floor support.
  • Cutover coordination: a flat 4 hours for the go live window, freeze, final import, and go or no go checks.
  • Parallel licences: your seats × your combined per seat cost × the parallel months you enter. This line uses your numbers only.

Totals are shown as a planning range, 25% under to 25% over the fixture math, because real migrations land inside a band, not on a point. For the reasoning behind each cost line, read our guide on data migration costs when switching tools, and the total cost worksheet for what the new system costs after the move.
