Multi-Meter Cost Estimator: HubSpot

Some products bill on one meter. HubSpot bills on several at once: platform seats, sales seats, marketing contact tiers, and a required one time onboarding fee. Buyers budget on the headline price and meet the other meters later. This estimator runs the meters together, from HubSpot's published pricing.

The meters, from HubSpot's published pricing

Every figure below is HubSpot's own published pricing, checked 2026-11-10 on hubspot.com. Prices are USD, annual billing unless noted.

  • Marketing Hub Professional: $800 per month billed annually ($890 billed monthly). Includes 3 Core Seats and 2,000 marketing contacts.
  • Extra Core Seats: $45 per seat per month beyond the 3 included.
  • Contact tiers: crossing 2,000 marketing contacts moves you to the 5,000 contact band at $250 per month. HubSpot raises the tier automatically when you cross the limit, and a selected tier cannot be downgraded until the contract renews. Bands above 5,000 exist (12,000 and up) and cost more; this estimator models up to the 5,000 band and says so rather than guessing the higher bands.
  • Sales Hub Professional: $90 per seat per month, with a 5 seat minimum in PlanPrice's verified record.
  • Required onboarding, one time: $3,000 for Marketing Hub Professional and $1,500 for Sales Hub Professional. It is not optional on Professional editions.

The pattern to notice: none of these meters is hidden, but they are never added together on the pricing page. The first year total below is the number to budget. For licence only comparisons across the CRM category, see the HubSpot deals page; for what a platform costs beyond the licence entirely, read the total cost worksheet.

Worked Example: The Meters, Added Together

Price a small team on the figures in the section above (HubSpot published pricing, checked 2026-11-10). Marketing Hub Professional is USD 800 per month on annual billing, including 3 Core seats and 2,000 marketing contacts. The team needs 5 Core seats, so 2 extra seats at USD 45 add USD 90. Sales Hub Professional for 5 sales seats at USD 90 per seat adds USD 450. The running total is USD 1,340 per month, USD 16,080 per year, before the one time onboarding the Professional editions require: USD 3,000 for Marketing Hub and USD 1,500 for Sales Hub, USD 4,500 in year one. If the contact list grows past 2,000, the 5,000 contact band adds a further USD 250 per month from the month the limit is crossed. The headline price in this example, 800, is barely half the real monthly figure, which is the point of running the meters together.

Limitations

HubSpot's meter definitions and band prices change, and tiers above the 5,000 contact band are not modelled here rather than guessed. Contact counts depend on which contacts HubSpot classifies as marketing contacts, a definition worth confirming on a trial before signing. This estimator is an estimate only; HubSpot's own quote for your portal is the final figure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why Does HubSpot Bill On Several Meters At Once?

Because its hubs are sold as one platform: platform seats, hub specific seats, and marketing contact volume each scale differently, so each carries its own price.

Can A Selected Contact Tier Be Lowered Mid Contract?

On the published terms recorded above, no: a tier raised by crossing a limit stays until the contract renews, which is why the growth check input matters.

Is Onboarding Really Mandatory?

On Professional editions, yes, on HubSpot's published pricing as checked above. Budget it as part of the purchase price, not as an optional extra.
