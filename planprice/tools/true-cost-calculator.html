How The True Cost Is Built

A subscription price is only the first line of the real bill. This calculator adds the lines vendors quote separately: the subscription itself (per seat price times billable seats, or the flat rate for the whole team), paid add ons the team actually needs, one time onboarding or implementation fees spread over the first year, sales tax where it applies, and currency conversion when a US dollar price lands on a Canadian card. True annual cost is the sum of those lines for twelve months, which is why two products with the same sticker price can land hundreds or thousands of dollars apart by December.

Worked Example: The Tier Jump Is The Cost

Five seats on HubSpot Starter at USD 20 per seat per month (HubSpot pricing checked 2026-10-27) cost USD 100 per month, USD 1,200 per year, with no mandatory onboarding. Step up to Sales Hub Professional at USD 90 per seat per month on annual billing (checked 2026-10-27) and the same five seats cost USD 450 per month, USD 5,400 per year, plus a one time onboarding fee of USD 1,500 that Professional editions require. The sticker comparison, 20 against 90, understates the first year gap: USD 1,200 against USD 6,900 once the onboarding line is in. That onboarding line is exactly the kind this calculator exists to expose.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why Compare Per Seat And Flat Rate Side By Side?

Because the cheaper model flips with team size. Per seat pricing wins for very small teams, flat rates usually win as headcount grows, and this calculator finds the crossover for your numbers.

What Costs Do Buyers Miss Most Often?

One time onboarding fees, paid add ons that turn out to be required, and the renewal price after an introductory term ends.

Does The Calculator Include Tax?

It shows cost lines before tax unless you add a tax figure yourself. Canadian sales tax on software depends on the vendor and your province, so no single rate is baked in.
