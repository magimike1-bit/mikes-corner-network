Verification log

What was checked, when it was checked, and whether it was a full sweep or a spot check.

PlanPrice re-checks every tracked product's list prices and promotions in a full sweep once a month. This log is the record behind the dates on every deal page, and it keeps the two kinds of check separate. A Full Sweep entry covers every tracked product in one monthly pass, and it is the only entry that moves a product's full sweep date. A Spot Check entry covers one product, or one part of one product, between sweeps. A spot check is labelled with its scope, and it never stands in for a full sweep. A page keeps its older full sweep date wherever a later check was a spot check only.

Before the monthly cadence began, the catalogue was baselined on 2026-09-30 (22 products then tracked), and on 2026-10-25 the promotion status of every tracked vendor was spot-checked in one pass. That October pass did not re-read list prices, except for the products whose entries below say so, and it is recorded here as a set of spot checks, not as a full sweep.
