What Pretending to Listen to Your Kid's Drama Practice is

Pretending to Listen to Your Kid's Drama Practice — A comedic survival guide for parents in the audience of one. The guides here are practical, plain-language explainers: what something is, how it works, what it costs, and how to avoid the common mistakes.

How the guides are written

Every guide is researched from real sources and written to be genuinely useful — no filler, no invented facts, no copied content. Numbers and recommendations are starting points for your own research, not guarantees.

What this site is not

Pretending to Listen to Your Kid's Drama Practice — a tongue-in-cheek parenting humor site. The joke is on the parent, never the kid. Content is for entertainment only. Nothing here is professional advice — consult a qualified professional for decisions that matter.

Who runs this site

Pretending to Listen to Your Kid's Drama Practice is operated by the Mike's Corner network, a collection of practical guide sites. Content is produced with AI assistance and human oversight.
