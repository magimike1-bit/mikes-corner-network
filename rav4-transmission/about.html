About

What this site is

RAV4 Transmission Fluid Guide is a small, single-topic site about one job on one vehicle: servicing the automatic transmission fluid in the 2012 Toyota RAV4 — the last year of the third generation, covering both the 2.5-litre four-cylinder with its four-speed automatic and the 3.5-litre V6 with its five-speed. It exists because this particular vehicle rewards its owners unusually well: it still has a dipstick, a drain plug, and a serviceable internal strainer, at a moment when most newer transmissions are sealed units that push owners toward a dealership for even a fluid check.

How the guides are written

Every specification on this site — the ATF WS fluid requirement, the 8.6 and 9.3 US quart total capacities, the 47 N·m drain-plug torque, the 70–80 °C level-check temperature, the 60,000-mile / 72-month severe-service interval — comes from Toyota's own publications: the 2012 owner's manual, Toyota's scheduled maintenance guidance, the factory repair manual for this transmission family, and Toyota's service bulletin on ATF WS fluid. Where a number is practical experience rather than a factory specification — such as how much fluid a typical drain-and-fill yields — the guides say so. We don't invent statistics, prices, or part numbers, and where sources disagree we say that too.

What this site is not

Content here is general information for owners, not a substitute for the factory repair manual or a qualified technician. Transmission work involves hot fluid and a raised vehicle; every procedure page on this site carries the safety notes that apply — jack stands, never a jack alone, and proper disposal of used fluid at a recycling depot. If a symptom described here matches a transmission that is slipping badly or won't engage, the guides' advice is to park it and have it diagnosed, and this page repeats that advice.

Who runs this site

RAV4 Transmission Fluid Guide is operated by the Mike's Corner network, a collection of practical guide sites. Content is produced with AI assistance and human oversight.

Spotted an error — especially in a specification? The Toyota publications named above are the authoritative record; if anything here disagrees with them, they win, and the guides get corrected. This site has no contact inbox; the contact page explains.
