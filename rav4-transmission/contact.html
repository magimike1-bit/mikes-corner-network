The guides on this site are checked against Toyota's own publications for the 2012 RAV4: the owner's manual, the scheduled maintenance guide, the factory repair manual, and Toyota's ATF WS service bulletin. Those documents are the authoritative record; if anything here ever disagrees with them, follow them.

Working on your own RAV4 and stuck on a step? Your vehicle's owner's manual and a Toyota dealer's parts and service counters are the reliable next stops — a parts counter can confirm fluid and gasket fitment from your VIN for free.

RAV4 Transmission Fluid Guide is general information for owners of the 2012 Toyota RAV4 — not a substitute for the factory repair manual or a qualified technician. Work safely: hot fluid burns, and a vehicle must be supported on jack stands, never on a jack alone. Used transmission fluid is hazardous waste — take it to a recycling depot.
