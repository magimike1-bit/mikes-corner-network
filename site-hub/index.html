About the author

I’m Mike — a Toronto-based dad and IT professional. By day I work with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central and retail systems; on the side I run BestMikeDeals, my little resale store.

My kids range from teens to college-age, so this network covers a bit of everything our household actually talks about: parenting, cooking, tech, saving money, fixing things around the house — and the occasional completely ridiculous project. Roughly half of it is practical how-to guides, and the other half is just for fun.

When I’m not writing or working, I’m usually baking, 3D printing, out with the metal detector, or gaming. Everything here is written in plain language from real experience — starter guides, not textbooks. I hope something here helps you out, or at least makes you laugh.
