Teen Money Lessons

What Teen Money Lessons is

No lectures, no jargon. Practical guides for parents helping teenagers learn how money works — allowances, first jobs, budgeting, saving, and the traps that drain a teen's wallet before they see them coming.

How the guides are written

Every guide is researched from real sources and written to be genuinely useful — no filler, no invented facts, no copied content. Numbers and recommendations are starting points for your own research, not guarantees.

What this site is not

Teen Money Lessons — everyday money guidance for parents and teens. Content is for general information only — this is not financial advice. For specific financial questions, talk to a qualified professional. Nothing here is professional advice — consult a qualified professional for decisions that matter.

Who runs this site

Teen Money Lessons is operated by the Mike's Corner network, a collection of practical guide sites. Content is produced with AI assistance and human oversight.
