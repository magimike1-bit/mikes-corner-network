What Typing 101 is

Typing 101 — Learn to touch type the right way — no apps to buy, no magic required. The guides here are practical, plain-language explainers: what something is, how it works, what it costs, and how to avoid the common mistakes.

How the guides are written

Every guide is researched from real sources and written to be genuinely useful — no filler, no invented facts, no copied content. Numbers and recommendations are starting points for your own research, not guarantees.

What this site is not

Typing 101 — practical touch-typing lessons for beginners. Content is for general information only; the ergonomics article is not medical advice. If you have pain, numbness, or a medical condition, consult a qualified health professional. Nothing here is professional advice — consult a qualified professional for decisions that matter.

Who runs this site

Typing 101 is operated by the Mike's Corner network, a collection of practical guide sites. Content is produced with AI assistance and human oversight.
