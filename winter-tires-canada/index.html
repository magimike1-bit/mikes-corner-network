01

It's about temperature, not snowfall

Below about 7°C, winter-tire rubber stays flexible while all-season compounds stiffen — and grip is what keeps you out of the ditch.

02

Four winter tires, never two

Mixing winter and all-season tires makes the car's handling unpredictable. If you can't do four, don't do winter tires at all.

03

Quebec made it the law

Winter tires are mandatory in Quebec from December 1. The rest of Canada treats it as strong advice — the physics doesn't change at the provincial border.
