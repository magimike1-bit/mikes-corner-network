Why year-end matters more than you think

Most small business money stress doesn't come from a bad year — it comes from a good year that was never organized. A few focused hours in the fourth quarter turn January from a panic into paperwork: receipts sorted, books reconciled, invoices collected, and a plan on the calendar for next year.

These guides are starter checklists written in plain language. They're not a substitute for professional advice — but they make the advice you do get much more useful.
